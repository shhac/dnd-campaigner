## Act 2: The Burning Garden
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- At least one companion has begun visible physical transformation (Visible stage or higher)
- The hero has learned the name "Ember Tithe" from the Rootbound and understands that bonds have a cost
- Gorundtzal has grown into a functional settlement (Population 50+, Defenses 4+)
- The party has had at least one direct confrontation or negotiation with a Communion subfaction
- Minimum sessions: 6

**Foreshadowing hints**:
- Companions remark that the frontier feels different — warmer in some places, colder in others. The well water at Gorundtzal sometimes tastes sweeter than iron.
- New arrivals comment that the hero seems impossibly magnetic — people follow without knowing why.
- The old-growth forest west of Gorundtzal feels alive in ways forests shouldn't. Trees creak in patterns. The air is warm even in winter.
- Animals near the settlement behave strangely — dogs follow bonded companions, birds avoid the Old Shrine.

**Mechanics unlocked at Act 2**:
- `mechanics/transformation/recovery.md` — If not already available from Beat 6

## Act 3: The Tithe Comes Due
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- The hero fully understands what the Mark does and has confronted the moral reality of the bonds
- At least one companion has reached Advanced transformation (compelling but undeniably inhuman)
- The Communion's cauterization has reached the northern frontier (the well or the Rootbound Grove is threatened)
- The hero has contact with at least two of the three major factions
- A recovery has been attempted (successful or not)
- At least one significant romantic bond has been tested by the knowledge of what bonds do
- Minimum sessions: 10

**Foreshadowing hints**:
- Dreams of branching patterns extending beyond the body, connecting to the earth. The hero wakes with soil under their fingernails.
- The Old Shrine at Gorundtzal hums at night. The branching patterns on the stone have shifted since the campaign began.
- Takkele reports that the Communion's texts reference a "final cauterization" — sealing the entire network permanently.
- The western forest whispers. Companions who walk there feel watched — not threatened, but *recognized*. As if the trees know their names.
- A companion at Advanced transformation hears something beneath the earth and describes it as beautiful.

**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-3.md
