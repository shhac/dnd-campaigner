# Story Arc Unlock Conditions

## Act 1: Ashes and Roots
**Status**: UNLOCKED
**Read**: story-arcs/act-1.md

## Act 2: The Burning Garden
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- At least one companion has begun visible physical transformation (sessions 7+)
- The hero has learned the name "Ember Tithe" from the Rootbound and understands that bonds have a cost
- Gorundtzal has grown into a functional settlement with at least 50 inhabitants
- The party has had at least one direct confrontation or negotiation with a Communion subfaction
- Minimum sessions: 6
**Foreshadowing hints**: Companions remark that the frontier feels different than it used to — warmer in some places, colder in others. The well water at Gorundtzal sometimes tastes sweeter than iron. New arrivals at the settlement comment that the hero seems impossibly magnetic — people follow them without knowing why.
**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-2.md

## Act 3: The Tithe Comes Due
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- The hero fully understands what the Mark does and has confronted the moral reality of the bonds
- At least one companion has reached advanced transformation (attractive but undeniably inhuman)
- The Communion's cauterization has reached the northern frontier (the well or the Rootbound Grove is threatened)
- The hero has contact with at least two of the three major factions
- A recovery has been attempted (successful or not)
- Minimum sessions: 10
**Foreshadowing hints**: Dreams of branching patterns that extend beyond the body, connecting to the earth itself. The Old Shrine at Gorundtzal hums at night. Takkele reports that the Communion's texts reference a "final cauterization" that would seal the entire network — permanently.
**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-3.md
