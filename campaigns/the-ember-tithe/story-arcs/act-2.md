# Act 2: The Burning Garden

## Status
LOCKED

## Key Secrets
- The hero's domain-building success is not entirely natural. The Mark amplifies the hero's charisma and emotional resonance — people follow them because the channels are enhancing the hero's presence. This means the hero's leadership is built on the same parasitic mechanism that's transforming their companions. The settlement itself is, in a sense, feeding on its inhabitants' emotional investment in the hero.
- General Ossivane is real, alive, and building a rival power base in the western forests. He's encountered the Rootbound and has his own understanding of the channels — incomplete and filtered through military pragmatism. He sees the hero as either a weapon to use or a threat to neutralize. He'll approach with alliance in mind, but his true priority is command.
- Gvirokha's decision to release Takkele was calculated, not merciful. He's testing whether information about the Communion's activities will cause the refugees to fight or flee. If they fight, the Communion can justify escalation. If they flee, the frontier clears itself. Either way, his faction benefits. He is, however, genuinely troubled by the cauterization — his calculation and his doubt coexist.
- The Communion's texts reference a previous civilization that maintained the channels — not the Rootbound (who are descendants of that civilization's lowest caste) but a sophisticated culture that understood the channels as technology, not mysticism. The "Ember Tithe" was that civilization's term for the Mark's function: a levy paid in human essence to keep the system running. The civilization collapsed when the last marked individual refused to pay the tithe and the channels went dormant.
- Lavrenne, the noblewoman claiming authority by birth, has a connection to the channels that neither she nor anyone else suspects. The Valduri royal line was originally established to serve as custodians of the channel nexus at Kethenvari. Over centuries, they forgot this purpose. But the bloodline still resonates with the channels — which is why the Communion targeted the nobility specifically.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Ossivane**: Wants to unite the Valduri Remnant under military discipline and drive the Communion out by force. He sees the hero as a charismatic rallying point but doesn't understand the Mark. If he learns what it does, his reaction depends on his pragmatism vs. his morality — he might see it as a weapon to exploit or an abomination to destroy.
- **Lavrenne**: Claims authority but is genuinely uncertain whether she deserves it. She feels a pull toward the hero that she interprets as political instinct. In truth, the channels are recognizing the connection between the Mark and the royal bloodline. She doesn't know this.
- **Dzveli (Covenant Remnant)**: Pushing for human sacrifice because he believes it's the only way to complete the cauterization. He's not wrong that the process is stalling — the channels resist more strongly as more are sealed. But his solution is monstrous. He may attempt to kidnap marked individuals (including the hero) for ritual purposes.
- **Birukta (Rootbound elder)**: Knows the full history of the Ember Tithe but believes sharing it will doom the hero to repeat past failures. She'll resist sharing until the hero demonstrates they're different from previous marked individuals — specifically, that they prioritize their companions over their own power.

## Planned Events
- Ossivane's emissaries arrive at Gorundtzal proposing an alliance — with conditions
- Lavrenne's retinue clashes with the hero's informal authority, creating political tension within the settlement
- A bonded companion's physical transformation becomes visible to others, forcing the hero to address it publicly or privately
- The Communion increases pressure on the northern frontier — a garrison is established closer to Gorundtzal
- A Rootbound elder invites the hero to a deep ritual that reveals the channels' true nature and the Mark's full history
- Pellinaste's intelligence-selling is discovered (or nearly discovered), creating a trust crisis
- The Covenant Remnant faction kidnaps or targets someone connected to the hero, escalating the conflict

## Foreshadowing
- Companions who've been bonded longest report dreams of the underground channels — vast networks of warmth and light. The dreams are beautiful and terrifying.
- The Grey Wasting patch in the western forest grows. Dead earth spreading in a perfect circle. Animals avoid it. The Rootbound are frightened.
- The hero's decisions carry more weight than they should — people agree too easily, follow too willingly. This isn't natural charisma. Something is amplifying it.
