## Recent Events Summary

Three weeks ago, the Ash Veil Communion broke through Kethenvari's walls in a single night. The hero survived the chaos and fled north with other refugees. During the fall — in a moment of extreme fear or grief — a branching, ember-veined pattern appeared on the hero's chest and arm. It hasn't faded. Since then, the hero has felt everything more intensely and experienced an inexplicable pull toward certain people.

The journey north was hard. Roads clogged with refugees. Communion patrols sweeping the countryside. Food scarce. The hero has arrived at Gorundtzal to find a waystation that's been raided but not destroyed — its walls still stand, its well still works, and a few dozen other survivors have had the same idea.
