# The Ember Tithe — Campaign Mechanics

These mechanics are specific to this campaign. They supplement standard D&D 5e rules.

## For AI Players (Player-Visible)

Read ONLY the README.md files listed below. Do NOT read any gm-guide.md or other GM-only files.

- `the-mark/README.md` — What the Mark looks and feels like (what your character observes)
- `transformation/README.md` — Changes you might notice in yourself or companions
- `romance/README.md` — How emotional bonds work in this campaign
- `domain-building/README.md` — Settlement life at Gorundtzal
