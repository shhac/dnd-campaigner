# Romance and Intimacy

**For**: All companions bonded to the hero
**What this is**: What you're feeling is real. This is about navigating it honestly.

---

## Connections Feel Intense Here

You've formed bonds before. Friendships. Loyalties. Maybe love. But nothing that felt like this.

The connections you're building at Gorundtzal — with the hero especially, but with each other too — arrive with a weight and a velocity that doesn't match the timeline. You've known this person for weeks, maybe days, and something in your chest says *I would die for them* with a certainty that should take years to earn. The warmth when they're near. The way a shared silence feels like a conversation. The pull to be closer, always closer, even when you just left their side.

It feels like genuine chemistry. It feels like recognition — like finding someone you were supposed to find. And maybe it is. The frontier strips away the usual barriers. Shared danger accelerates trust. Surviving together builds bonds that peacetime never could.

But you might also catch yourself wondering: *Is it supposed to be this fast? Is it supposed to feel this necessary?*

You don't have to answer that yet. Just notice it.

## Trust Is Everything

These bonds deepen through vulnerability. Through saying the thing that scares you. Through showing someone the parts you keep hidden and watching them not flinch.

Shared danger matters — you don't truly know someone until you've seen how they move when the world is falling apart. But it's the quiet moments after the danger that build the real foundation. The confession by firelight. The admission of fear. The story you've never told anyone.

Withholding corrodes these bonds. Not privacy — everyone deserves their silence. But active deception, emotional walls maintained out of habit rather than necessity, the performance of closeness without the substance — these things create distance the bond can feel. You'll know it when it happens. Something that was warm will cool. A connection that felt effortless will require maintenance. And you'll feel the absence like a draft through a crack in a wall.

Honesty deepens things. Dishonesty thins them. The bond keeps score in ways you can't quite track.

## Others Notice Your Bonds

You are not invisible to each other.

The companions around you are watching the same dynamics you are. They see the way the hero looks at you — or doesn't. They notice when you return from a conversation with flushed cheeks and a quieter voice. They register the way your hand lingers on someone's arm, the way your laugh changes depending on who you're laughing with.

This creates texture. Jealousy — not the petty kind, but the deep ache of watching someone receive what you hunger for. Protectiveness — the fierce instinct to guard a bond you can see forming between two people you care about. Alliance — the unspoken agreement between companions who understand each other's position relative to the hero.

None of this is wrong. These are natural responses to an unnatural intimacy. Don't suppress them. Don't perform indifference you don't feel. If seeing the hero touch someone else makes your stomach clench, that's information worth having. If watching a companion's face soften around the hero makes you want to shield them, that's worth honoring.

You are all in this together. That means navigating each other as much as navigating the bond itself.

## You May Find Yourself Changing

Strong emotional bonds seem to accelerate the changes described in the [transformation guide](../transformation/README.md). This isn't a warning to stop connecting — the connections are sustaining you, grounding you, giving you reasons to hold onto who you are.

But notice the intersection. The moments when desire and strangeness overlap. When the new urge you can't explain surfaces during an intimate conversation. When the shift in your priorities aligns suspiciously well with what the hero needs.

Connection and transformation are tangled here. That doesn't make the connection less real. It makes it more complicated.

## Intimacy Matters

Physical and emotional closeness intensifies everything in this campaign. A held hand, a forehead pressed against someone's shoulder, the weight of another person beside you in the dark — these aren't incidental. They carry the full gravity of what's building between you.

Intimacy here is meaningful. It isn't casual, even when it's playful. Every moment of genuine closeness adds another thread to the bond, and you'll feel it — the warmth, the rightness, the terrifying sense of falling without a net. The almost-moments carry as much weight as the actual ones. A hand that nearly reaches out. A silence where someone almost says something. The electric distance between two people who haven't yet touched but both know they're going to.

## How to Play These Scenes

Scenes of emotional and physical closeness should be written with sensory detail and emotional depth. Focus on what it *feels* like — the vulnerability, the electricity, the weight of it. The hitch in breathing. The temperature of someone's skin. The specific quality of silence between two people who are about to change something between them.

Lean into the tension, the almost-moments, the loaded silence. The space before a first touch is more charged than the touch itself. The words someone doesn't say carry more than the ones they do.

Fade to black for explicit content. But everything leading up to that threshold — the approach, the decision, the crossing — should be rendered fully. This campaign treats intimacy as one of its most important dramatic spaces. Give it room. Give it weight. Give it the attention it deserves.

---

**These bonds are real.** Whatever else is happening — whatever the Mark is doing, whatever the frontier is doing to all of you — the way you feel about each other is not a lie. It may be amplified. It may be accelerated. But the tenderness, the wanting, the willingness to be seen — those are yours.

That has to matter. Even if it costs something.
