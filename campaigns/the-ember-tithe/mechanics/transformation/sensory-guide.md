# Transformation — Sensory Guide

**Audience**: GM only. Reference sheet for narrating transformation in scenes.
**How to use**: Scan before a scene involving a transforming companion. Pick 1-2 details to weave in. Never more than 2 per scene — restraint is what makes each detail land.

---

## Temperature

1. A companion's skin runs warmer where transformation advances. The hero feels this during any touch — hand to shoulder, fingers interlaced, bodies pressed together. The heat is localized at first: one forearm, the back of the neck, the palms. Over sessions, it spreads.

2. Heat maps: some patches of skin are noticeably warm, others still human-temperature. The boundary between them shifts week by week, like a tide line moving up a beach. A companion might press their own hand to their ribs and frown, feeling the difference.

3. A transforming companion's breath is slightly warmer than it should be. Noticeable when speaking close, or when breathing on skin during intimacy. Not unpleasant — like standing near a hearth.

4. In cold weather, a companion doesn't shiver as much as they used to. They notice before others do. Standing in sleet that makes everyone else huddle and clench, and feeling merely cool.

5. The companion's warmth becomes ambient. Others sitting near them at a fire feel slightly more comfortable than the fire alone explains. The companion becomes a source of heat, and people unconsciously orient toward them in cold rooms.

6. Hot drinks cool faster in a transforming companion's hands. Cold drinks warm faster. The companion's internal temperature is asserting itself on everything they hold.

---

## Texture

7. Skin develops a faint grain — like polished wood or river stone. Still soft under a casual touch, still pleasant, but different when a hand lingers. A lover would notice first.

8. Hair changes texture before it changes color. Coarser, or silkier, or with a metallic sheen that catches light at unexpected angles. Running fingers through it feels different than it used to.

9. Scars fade faster on a transforming companion. A cut that should take weeks to heal leaves only a faint silver line after days. The companion might not notice, but a healer would.

10. Fingertips feel slightly different — as if the whorls have smoothed. The companion's grip hasn't changed, but the texture of their touch has. Like being touched by something wearing human skin very well.

11. Lips are fuller, or smoother, or have a warmth that lingers after a kiss longer than body heat should. The person kissed finds themselves touching their own lips afterward, puzzled.

12. Calluses that took years to build soften and vanish. A swordfighter's palm, a musician's fingertips — the body is remaking itself and doesn't care about earned history.

---

## Movement

13. One too many degrees of neck articulation when turning to look at something. The head swivels just slightly past where a human neck should stop, then corrects. Happens unconsciously. The companion doesn't feel it.

14. An arm extends slightly further than it should when reaching for something, then corrects — like a rubber band snapping back to normal length. Blink and you'd miss it.

15. Sitting perfectly still for minutes without shifting weight. Not meditation. Not concentration. Just an absence of the constant micro-adjustments that human bodies make. A predator's stillness, or a statue's.

16. Walking with a fluidity that wasn't there before. The companion used to have a particular gait — everyone does. Now there's something smoothed out, the small inefficiencies of human movement polished away. Beautiful. Uncanny.

17. Reflexes slightly too fast. Catching a dropped cup before it's clearly falling. Turning toward a sound before it's fully registered. Flinching from a blow that hasn't been thrown yet.

18. Standing from a seated position without the preparatory shift of weight that humans unconsciously perform. Just vertical, suddenly, as if gravity released them.

19. Sleeping in positions that shouldn't be comfortable. Curled at angles that would cramp a human body, or flat on bare stone as easily as feather bedding. The body no longer demands the comforts it used to.

---

## Sound

20. Voice develops a resonance — richer, deeper, like hearing the same voice in a cathedral. The words are the same. The person is the same. But the sound fills more space than it used to, as if the chest producing it is larger than it appears.

21. Breathing at night takes on a rhythm that doesn't match normal sleep patterns. Too slow. Too regular. A metronome precision that human breathing never achieves. A companion sharing a bedroll with them may find it soothing — and then disturbing once they realize why.

22. A laugh that carries harmonic overtones. Not a different laugh — the same laugh, with something layered underneath. A second note, almost inaudible, that resonates in the listener's sternum.

23. Footfalls lighter than they should be for the companion's weight. Crossing a creaking floorboard without a sound. Stepping on dry leaves that don't crunch. As if the earth is accommodating them.

24. When angry or aroused, a low vibration that others can almost feel rather than hear. Sub-vocal. Below the threshold of conscious perception. It makes nearby people's hair stand up without knowing why.

25. Humming or singing unconsciously in moments of contentment — but in tones that don't match any known song. Melodies that feel ancient and aimless, as if the companion is channeling something rather than composing.

---

## Scent

26. Each transforming companion develops a unique scent that is attractive and subtly wrong. Not a smell they've always had — a new one, emerging:
    - **Petrichor**: Rain on hot stone, constant, even indoors on dry days
    - **Heated amber or resin**: Sweet, warm, like standing near a forest fire that's burning only beautiful things
    - **Deep cave air**: Mineral-clean, ancient, the smell of stone that hasn't seen sunlight in centuries
    - **Ozone before a storm**: Electric, sharp, making the nose tingle
    - **Fresh-turned earth after rain**: Rich, fertile, alive in a way that has nothing to do with decay
    - **Woodsmoke and honey**: Domestic warmth with an edge of wildness underneath

27. Scent intensifies with emotion and proximity to the hero. A companion in the hero's arms during an emotional moment produces a scent strong enough to fill a room. In calm moments, it's barely perceptible.

28. The hero may find themselves tracking a companion by scent before consciously realizing it. Turning toward a doorway a moment before the companion walks through it. Knowing which companion slept in a bedroll by smell alone.

29. Other companions — even unbonded ones — find a transforming companion's scent pleasant in a way that's hard to articulate. It creates an unconscious desire for proximity. Animals react too: dogs press closer, horses nicker, birds go quiet.

---

## Vision

30. Eyes catch light differently — a slight reflective quality in dim conditions. Not a full animal eyeshine, but a flash. A companion turns their head near a candle and for one heartbeat their eyes throw light back. Then it's gone.

31. Pupils that dilate or contract slightly too slowly when moving between light and shadow. A beat behind what they should do. The companion's vision seems unaffected — they see fine — but their eyes aren't performing the normal human adjustments.

32. Skin that seems to have depth — not just surface color but layers visible underneath. Like looking into shallow, clear water. In certain light, you can see something beneath the surface: darker patterns, a warmth that looks like banked embers, the suggestion of movement.

33. In firelight, faint branching patterns visible on the skin that echo the hero's Mark. They appear for an instant — a flicker — and then they're gone. The companion doesn't feel them. Only an observer catches it, and only if they're watching closely.

34. Hair that moves slightly in still air. Not dramatically. A single strand drifting as if in a breeze that isn't there. The companion reaches up to tuck it back absently, not realizing there's no wind.

35. A companion caught in a moment of strong emotion — rage, desire, grief — whose features seem to blur at the edges for a heartbeat. Not a change of shape. More like the boundary between them and the air has softened. Then it resolves, and they look normal again.

---

## The Wrong Moments

Scenes where transformation surfaces during closeness — intimacy, comfort, shared vulnerability. These are the most powerful because the context makes the wrongness impossible to ignore or dismiss.

36. A grip that's too strong during an embrace. Not painful — controlled — but the compression exceeds what the companion's frame should produce. Released immediately when the companion registers the hero's flinch. The companion stares at their own hand.

37. Eyes that reflect firelight like an animal's for one heartbeat during a moment of eye contact. The companion doesn't notice. The hero does.

38. A sound during physical closeness that isn't quite a human vocalization. Lower. More resonant. A vibration in the chest rather than a sound in the throat. The companion may not realize they made it.

39. Skin that's electric to touch — a charge that makes contact feel heightened, every nerve ending responding more than it should. Not painful. Overwhelming. Like touching a live wire that happens to feel exquisite.

40. The companion's heartbeat, felt through pressed-together bodies, that is slightly too slow and too powerful. Each beat thuds rather than taps. The rhythm is off from human norms — not irregular, but operating on a different clock.

41. A moment during sleep where the companion's face, relaxed and unguarded, looks subtly different than it does when awake. The bone structure hasn't changed — or has it? In the grey light of predawn, with the companion's new scent filling the air, the hero lies awake and watches and isn't sure.

42. The companion's body temperature rising during emotional intensity — not a blush, not exertion, but core temperature climbing until the hero can feel the heat radiating through clothing. The companion doesn't seem to notice they're running hot.

43. A tear that, in firelight, looks faintly luminous as it tracks down a companion's cheek. It might be the light. It might not.
