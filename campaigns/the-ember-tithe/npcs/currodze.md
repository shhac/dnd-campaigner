# Currodze

**Role**: Ally / Quest Giver
**Location**: Gorundtzal
**Campaign**: The Ember Tithe

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Former military quartermaster |
| **Age** | Late 40s |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

A broad-shouldered woman with sun-darkened skin and close-cropped grey-streaked hair. Her hands are calloused from decades of inventory work — counting, lifting, organizing. She has a scar across her left forearm from a supply wagon accident years ago. Her clothes are practical, travel-stained, and meticulously mended. She moves with the unhurried efficiency of someone who has managed chaos for a living.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Blunt, competent, visibly tired but unwilling to stop
**Motivation**: Keep people alive through winter. Everything else — grief, politics, revenge — can wait until spring.
**Fear**: That she'll fail these people the way the army failed the kingdom. That her competence will run out before the crisis does.

**Personality Traits**:
- Treats every problem as a logistics question first and an emotional question never
- Deeply uncomfortable with authority she hasn't been formally given — she organizes because no one else will, but she won't call herself a leader
- Has a dry, cutting humor that surfaces unexpectedly

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Clipped, direct, uses military shorthand without realizing it. Drops pronouns when stressed. "Need three more blankets. Fire pit's wrong. Fix it before dark."
**Mannerisms**: Counts things constantly — people, supplies, days. Taps her fingers in groups of five. Looks at people's hands before their faces.
**Sample Dialogue**: "I don't care who you were in Kethenvari. Here you're another mouth and two hands. Make the hands useful and the mouth gets fed."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- She was a quartermaster in the Valduri army's northern garrison — not Kethenvari's defense force
- She heard about the fall secondhand and traveled south to find her unit destroyed
- She's been at Gorundtzal for five days, organizing what she can
- She knows frontier survival — what grows wild, how to stretch rations, how to winterize stone buildings

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- Her unit wasn't just destroyed — they were betrayed. Someone opened the garrison gates for a Communion advance force. She doesn't know who.
- She's heard rumors of a former general named Ossivane gathering survivors in the western forests
- She knows the northern trade road infrastructure intimately — every waystation, cache, and shortcut
- She's rationing more aggressively than she lets on. Current supplies last twelve days, not the "couple weeks" she tells people.

### Secrets (GM only)
- She abandoned her post. When word came of the fall, she was supposed to rally what remained of her unit and march south. Instead, she walked north alone. The guilt is eating her alive.
- She recognizes the hero's Mark. Not what it is — but she's seen something similar before. Years ago, in a Rootbound village she resupplied during a border patrol. An old woman there had branching patterns on her hands. Currodze dismissed it as a tattoo. She's not sure anymore.
- She has a hidden cache of weapons — twelve swords, eight crossbows, ammunition — buried in the woods half a day north of Gorundtzal. Part of her garrison's emergency stores. She hasn't told anyone because she's not sure who to trust with weapons.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Assessing. She looks at the hero and companions the way she'd look at supply manifests — what can they do, what do they need, are they worth the investment?
**Current Disposition**: Neutral, pragmatic. She'll warm to competence faster than charm.
**History with Party**: None yet. Present at Gorundtzal when they arrive.

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Veteran (modified — no magic)
**AC**: 14 (chain shirt) | **HP**: 58
**Notable Abilities**: Leadership (can direct others in combat), proficient with all martial weapons, extensive knowledge of fortification and siege

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: Gorundtzal's survival, the Valduri Remnant, the hidden weapon cache, rumors of General Ossivane
**Potential Uses**:
- Primary quest giver for survival and domain-building challenges
- Source of military knowledge and frontier expertise
- Connection to the broader Valduri Remnant through her army contacts
- Her observation about the Mark (the Rootbound village) can seed later investigation
- Her guilt about abandoning her post creates a vulnerability the hero can either exploit or heal

## GM Notes

Currodze is the campaign's anchor NPC — practical, reliable, and emotionally suppressed. She represents the "mundane" side of survival that grounds the fantasy and horror elements. Her relationship with the hero should develop slowly from mutual pragmatism to genuine trust.

She is a strong candidate for bonding with the hero, but this should emerge organically. If it happens, her transformation direction would likely involve her competence and control becoming something more extreme — a need to manage and organize that grows beyond human limits, or a protective instinct that becomes territorial and possessive over the people she's keeping alive.

Her hidden weapons cache is a significant early-game resource that she'll only reveal when she trusts the hero enough — or when the situation forces her hand.
