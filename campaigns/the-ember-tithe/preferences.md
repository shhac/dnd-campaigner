# Campaign Preferences

**Campaign**: The Ember Tithe

## Narrative Style

**narrative_style**: hybrid

Uses clear speaker labels with flowing prose. Dialogue is attributed but woven into descriptive narrative rather than bare script format. Descriptions maintain literary quality while keeping conversation easy to follow.

## Tone Notes

- Dark romance horror — intimacy and dread woven together
- Hero's journey opening that steadily darkens
- Psychological horror before body horror — the mind changes first
- Relationships carry real weight and real cost
- Theater of mind combat — narrative, not tactical
- Transformation is slow, subtle, and personal — never sudden or gratuitous
- Beauty shifts but doesn't disappear — the uncanny, not the grotesque (until extreme neglect)
- Domain building should feel earned — small victories in a hostile world
