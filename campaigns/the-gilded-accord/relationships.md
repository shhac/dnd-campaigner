- **Status**: Mutual suspicion
- **Origin**: Assigned to joint investigation by opposing factions
- **Bonding Moments**: None yet

- **Potential**: Could develop grudging respect if they discover shared commitment to truth over faction

#### Vélissande ↔ Goitxarri
- **Status**: Professional wariness
- **Origin**: Assigned to joint investigation
- **Bonding Moments**: None yet
- **Tension Points**: She reads his straightforwardness as either genuine or a mask; he watches her hands, not her words
- **Potential**: Natural investigative partnership if trust develops — her analysis, his evidence discipline

#### Vélissande ↔ Théodarrhe
- **Status**: Chess game

- **Bonding Moments**: None yet

- **Potential**: Either the strongest alliance or the deepest betrayal in the party

#### Kes ↔ Goitxarri
- **Status**: Unspoken understanding
- **Origin**: Assigned to joint investigation
- **Bonding Moments**: None yet
- **Tension Points**: Kes distrusts the chain of command; Goitxarri embodies it

#### Kes ↔ Théodarrhe
