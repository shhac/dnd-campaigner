# Act 2: The Gilded Cage

## Status
LOCKED

## Key Secrets
- The altered Accord doesn't just favor the Mint financially — it contains a dormancy clause that allows any single faction to invoke emergency powers if the Council fails to seat a Fifth Principal. The Mint has known about this clause for two centuries. It's the real reason the Thirty-Day Clock exists.
- The cross-faction cabal (called the Dorêve Circle among themselves) has been manipulating Môrivôs politics for over a century. Members include senior figures in the Mint, the Rampart, and — most shockingly — the Veil. The Commons Assembly is the only faction not represented, which is why the cabal has always worked to keep the Commons weak.
- The cabal's plan is not to install their own Fifth Principal but to prevent any confirmation, triggering the dormancy clause, which the Mint will invoke to seize emergency financial control of the city.
- Caudressô was himself a former member of the Dorêve Circle. He turned against them when he realized the dormancy clause existed — the altered Accord wasn't just unfair, it was a ticking bomb.
- The Founding Seal is hidden inside a secret compartment in the Accord Chamber's oldest wall — Caudressô used knowledge from the original (unaltered) architectural plans that only the Dorêve Circle was supposed to have.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Rôsamunde Ghalliverre**: Preparing to invoke the dormancy clause. Has already drafted the legal framework. Believes she's saving the city from chaos — the others can't govern without Mint money.
- **Marshal Aitzolenne**: Learning the truth about the Rampart's involvement in the cabal through generations past. Torn between loyalty to the Rampart's honor and the reality that his predecessors were complicit.
- **Speaker Brûveisse**: Has acquired partial evidence of the altered Accord independently. Plans to reveal it publicly at the worst possible moment for the other factions — maximum leverage for the Commons.
- **Director Pallenne**: Has identified the Veil members of the Dorêve Circle but cannot move against them without revealing how deep the Veil's internal corruption goes.
- **The Dorêve Circle's enforcer** (Gattô Théveresse, a retired Veil operative): Responsible for both murders. Still active, still watching.

## Planned Events
- The PCs obtain Caudressô's cipher journal (or enough of it to understand the altered Accord)
- Each faction's candidate for Fifth Principal is formally announced — at least one is a Dorêve Circle member
- A public scandal breaks: leaked documents suggest one faction murdered Caudressô. The leak is a Dorêve Circle disinformation operation.
- Street violence erupts as faction loyalists clash. The Rampart must choose between keeping order and pursuing the investigation.
- The PCs are forced to decide: share what they know with their factions (risking the information being suppressed) or go public (risking civil unrest)
