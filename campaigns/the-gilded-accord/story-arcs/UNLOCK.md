## Act 2: The Gilded Cage
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- Party has discovered that Caudressô's death is connected to the original Accord document
- At least one PC has been forced to choose between faction loyalty and the investigation
- Party has identified or encountered the conspiracy's intermediary
- Minimum sessions: 3
**Foreshadowing hints**: Old-city archivists grow nervous when asked about the founding. The Accord Chamber's wards aren't just broken — they were turned off from inside.
**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-2.md
