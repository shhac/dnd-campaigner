# Act 3: The Fifth Principal

## Status
LOCKED

## Key Secrets
- The original, unaltered Gilded Accord still exists — hidden by one of the five founders who objected to the changes but was outvoted by the others. It's sealed in a vault beneath the Mendizabal Steps, accessible only with the Founding Seal.
- The dormancy clause has already been secretly invoked by Rôsamunde Ghalliverre. She has three days of emergency financial authority before it becomes public. She's using those three days to secure the treasury and lock out the other factions.
- Marshal Aitzolenne is preparing to close the city gates — he believes he's preventing outside interference, but it will also trap the PCs and their allies inside with the Dorêve Circle.
- Speaker Brûveisse has called for a general strike. The docks are shut. The city is running out of fresh food. The Commons Assembly is flexing the one form of power it has — the ability to stop everything.
- Director Pallenne has gone dark. The Veil is fracturing between loyalists and Dorêve Circle members. A shadow civil war is happening inside the intelligence service.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Rôsamunde**: Believes she is the only person capable of holding the city together. Willing to become a tyrant to prevent anarchy. Not evil — desperate, and wrong.
- **Aitzolenne**: Will follow the law to its final letter. If the PCs can show him the original Accord, he will enforce it — even against his own predecessors' legacy.
- **Brûveisse**: Wants a new Accord, written with the Commons as full equals. Will accept nothing less. The general strike is his leverage.
- **Pallenne**: Has one card left — she knows the identity of every Dorêve Circle member. She'll trade that list for immunity and the Veil's survival.
- **Gattô Théveresse**: Has been ordered to eliminate the PCs. This is the campaign's climactic physical threat.

## Planned Events
- The PCs must find and retrieve the original Accord from beneath the Mendizabal Steps
- Gattô Théveresse makes his move — an assassination attempt on the PCs during the retrieval
- The Mint invokes emergency powers publicly; the city erupts
- The PCs must choose their endgame:
  - **Expose everything**: Reveal the original Accord, the alteration, the cabal. Maximum truth, maximum chaos. The Accord is rewritten from scratch, but the city may not survive the transition.
  - **Negotiate a new Accord**: Use the truth as leverage to force all factions to the table. Controlled reform. Some guilty parties walk free.
  - **Crown a Fifth Principal**: Find a candidate all factions can accept, confirm them with the recovered Seal, and bury the truth. Stability preserved, justice denied.
  - **Something else**: The PCs may find a path no one anticipated. Good.
- The confirmation ceremony (or its equivalent) — the campaign's final scene

## Foreshadowing
- The harbor is too quiet. The Rampart's banners fly at half-staff. Shops in the upper terraces board their windows.
- Old men and women who remember the Second Crisis (when the Accord nearly failed before) start telling their stories openly, as if preparing the young for what comes next.
