# Act 1: The Thirty Days

## Status
UNLOCKED

## Key Secrets
- Caudressô was murdered by a rare alchemical poison called Veilsleep — it stops the heart without leaving traces. Only the Veil knows how to make it, but a batch went missing from their stores six months ago.
- Caudressô discovered that the original Gilded Accord was altered after signing. The document currently in the archives is not what the five founders agreed to. The changes subtly favor the Mint's financial authority.
- The Founding Seal was taken by Caudressô himself before he died — he hid it somewhere in the Accord Chamber to prevent the confirmation of a new Principal until the truth was revealed. The killer didn't know this.
- Caudressô's cipher journal contains his research into the Accord's true text. He was working with a Rampart archivist named Zubeldia Galarreta who has since gone into hiding.
- The conspiracy is not a single faction's plot — it's a cross-faction cabal of old-family elites who have known about the altered Accord for generations and profit from it. They killed Caudressô because he was about to go public.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Rôsamunde Ghalliverre (Mint Councillor)**: Knows the Accord was altered. Her family has benefited for generations. She didn't order the murder but isn't sorry it happened. Will subtly steer the investigation away from Mint financial records.
- **Marshal Etxebarria Aitzolenne (Rampart Councillor)**: Genuinely doesn't know about the alteration. Furious about the murder — it violates everything the Rampart stands for. But will protect Rampart secrets if pressed.
- **Speaker Tamô Brûveisse (Commons Councillor)**: Suspects the Accord was built to disadvantage the Commons (it was). Wants the investigation to succeed but is playing his own game — using the crisis to push for structural reforms.
- **Director Lôcren Pallenne (Veil Councillor)**: Knows the poison is Veilsleep. Knows a batch went missing. Is investigating internally while appearing cooperative externally. Terrified that the Veil is being framed.
- **Zubeldia Galarreta (Rampart archivist)**: In hiding. Has a copy of Caudressô's research. Afraid for her life. Will surface when she trusts someone enough — or when she has no choice.

## Planned Events
- Day 4-5: The PCs examine the Accord Chamber and Caudressô's office. Physical evidence is sparse — the scene was professionally cleaned. But small details remain (scratch marks on the Seal's display case, a faint alchemical residue if they know what to test for).
- Day 6-8: Each faction privately contacts their operative with "supplementary instructions" — i.e., things to look for that serve faction interests. This is the first loyalty test.
- Day 10-12: A second death. A minor clerk in the Pallisette archives who had been helping Caudressô access old records is found dead — same method, same pose. The investigation just got personal.
- Day 12-15: Zubeldia Galarreta makes contact with one of the PCs (most likely Goitxarri, as a fellow Rampart member). She has information but demands protection.
- Day 15+: Political pressure escalates. Each faction floats a candidate for Fifth Principal. The confirmation process begins even as the investigation continues.

## Foreshadowing
- Elderly residents of the old city speak of the Accord with a cynicism that feels too specific — "The Accord serves those it was written to serve"
- The Accord Chamber's protective wards were maintained by the Veil. Their failure suggests either negligence or complicity.
- Old portraits of the five founders in the Pallisette show subtle differences from the official histories — a founding family whose crest has been quietly removed from one painting
