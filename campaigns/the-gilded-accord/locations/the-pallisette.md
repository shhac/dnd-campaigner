# The Pallisette

**Type**: Government Quarter / Political District
**Region**: Central Môrivôs, raised plateau
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Overview

The seat of Môrivôs's shared governance, built on a natural limestone plateau at the city's geographic center. White-washed buildings arranged around a central courtyard, connected by covered walkways. The architecture is deliberately austere — no faction colors, no ornamental excess. This is neutral ground, and it looks like it.

## Atmosphere

**Sights**: White stone against blue sky. Officials in faction colors crossing the courtyard. Guards at every entrance — Rampart soldiers in formal armor, ceremonial but alert. The Accord Chamber's dome rising above the other rooftops, its bronze weathervane turning slowly.
**Sounds**: Footsteps on stone. Hushed conversations that stop when you approach. The scratch of pens from open office windows. A bell that rings each hour from the Chamber's tower.
**Smells**: Limestone dust, ink, lamp oil, and the faint scent of the harbor carried up by the wind
**Feeling**: Tense formality. Everyone watches everyone. Conversations have subtext. The air itself feels like it's taking notes.

## Key Features

### The Accord Chamber (Etxebarria Zahar)
The oldest building in Môrivôs. A circular hall beneath a copper dome, with five seats arranged around a central table. The walls bear murals depicting the founding. Currently sealed — crime scene, accessible only to the investigation team with Council authorization.

### The Salle de Consigne
A formal briefing room adjacent to the Accord Chamber. Rectangular, high-ceilinged, with a long oak table. Where the PCs receive their mandate. Maps of the city cover one wall; the other three are bare stone.

### Faction Offices
Each faction maintains a permanent office in the Pallisette:
- **Maison Dorêve** (Mint): Largest office, finest furniture, a vault in the basement
- **Irrintzi Hall** (Rampart): Spartan, functional, doubles as a garrison command post
- **Salle Ghalliverre** (Commons): Smallest, most crowded, full of guild petitions
- **The Quiet House** (Veil): Unremarkable from outside. Interior layout unknown.

## Points of Interest

- **The Archives**: Basement level beneath the Chamber. Historical records going back to the founding. Access restricted by Council vote.
- **The Courtyard**: Central open space where faction agents cross paths. Benches where deals are made and overheard. A fountain that hasn't worked in decades.
- **The Morgue**: A clinical room in the Pallisette's basement, normally unused. Currently holding Caudressô's body.

## Secrets & Hidden Things

- The Accord Chamber's walls contain hidden compartments — part of the original construction, known only to those with access to the architectural plans
- The Veil's Quiet House has tunnels connecting to the city's sewer system
- The Archives have a restricted section that requires two different Council members' keys to access

## History

Built on the site of the original peace talks, three hundred years ago. The limestone plateau was chosen because it was defensible and equidistant from the five original faction territories. The Accord Chamber was the first permanent building; the others grew around it over decades. The Pallisette's neutrality has been violated only twice in three centuries — both times during succession crises. This is the third.

## GM Notes

The Pallisette is the campaign's hub. Most investigation scenes start or end here. Its neutral status means all factions operate under a fragile truce — which creates constant tension. The PCs have a temporary office here (assigned as part of their mandate) that serves as their base of operations.

The sealed Accord Chamber is the campaign's most important locked room. The PCs will need to examine it carefully; the clues inside are subtle but critical. The Founding Seal is hidden in a wall compartment that won't be found without either the architectural plans or a very thorough investigation (DC 20 Investigation, or DC 15 with Stonecunning).
