# Salthook District

**Type**: Urban District / Waterfront
**Region**: Lower Môrivôs, harbor-side
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Overview

The working waterfront of Môrivôs. A dense tangle of warehouses, fish markets, chandleries, rope walks, and tenement housing stacked five stories high along narrow streets that smell of brine and tar. This is where the city's wealth is made, by people who never see most of it. The Commons Assembly's power base and the heartbeat of the city.

## Atmosphere

**Sights**: Crane booms swinging against the sky. Laundry strung between buildings. Chalk tallies on warehouse doors. Children running barefoot. Guild signs in faded paint. Ships' masts visible from every east-facing window.
**Sounds**: Shouting — always shouting. Crane chains rattling. Gulls. Hammers. Songs from the rope walk. Arguments in three languages from a single tavern.
**Smells**: Salt, fish, tar, woodsmoke, cheap food frying in oil, and underneath it all the constant mineral tang of the harbor
**Feeling**: Vital, crowded, raw. Nothing here is pretending to be something it isn't. The wealth that flows through the docks is visible in the cargo, not in the buildings.

## Key Features

### The Docks
A quarter-mile of stone quays handling everything from fishing boats to deep-water traders. The Dockmaster's Office sits at the center — a squat building with a view of every berth. Mint customs agents inspect cargo; Rampart guards patrol the perimeter; dock workers do the actual work.

### The Spillways
Residential tenements built along the old drainage channels. The cheapest housing in Môrivôs. Cramped, damp, but fiercely community-oriented. Everyone knows everyone. Strangers are noticed immediately.

### Brûno's Ledger
A dockside tavern that serves as Kes's unofficial headquarters. Named for its owner, an elderly bookkeeper who retired from the Chandlers' Guild. Good cheap food, terrible expensive drink, and the best information network below the second terrace.

## Points of Interest

- **The Chandlery Walk**: A covered market street where the famous wage strike began. Now a symbol of labor organizing. Guild posters cover every surface.
- **The Tidewall Steps**: Stairs carved into the harbor wall, leading from the water to the second terrace. The fastest route between Salthook and the upper city — and the place where class boundaries are most visible.
- **The Guild Hall Row**: Six guild halls packed into a single block. Chandlers, Coopers, Rope-makers, Fishers, Stevedores, and the combined Trades Guild.

## Secrets & Hidden Things

- Several warehouses along the eastern quay have been quietly purchased by Mint intermediaries — the dock workers don't know their new landlords
- The Spillways contain tunnel access to the old city sewers — useful for moving unseen
- Brûno's Ledger has a basement room where the Assembly's organizing committee meets privately

## History

Salthook predates the Accord. The original fishing village that became Môrivôs started here, on this waterfront. When the factions divided the city, the docks were claimed by whoever could hold them. The Commons Assembly was formed here — born from the guilds that organized to demand a seat at the table the other four factions had built. Every labor reform, every wage increase, every expansion of voting rights started as a conversation in a Salthook tavern.

## GM Notes

Salthook is Kes's territory and the emotional heart of the campaign's class conflict. Scenes set here should feel viscerally different from the Pallisette — louder, messier, more honest. The people here care about the investigation only insofar as it affects their lives. If the Accord falls, they're the first to suffer.

This is also where the Commons Assembly's general strike (Act 3) will begin. The organizing infrastructure is already in place. The PCs may discover this during the investigation, creating a dilemma: reveal Brûveisse's plans to the other factions, or let the Commons keep their leverage.
