# The Accord Chamber (Etxebarria Zahar)

**Type**: Sacred Government Building / Crime Scene
**Region**: The Pallisette, central
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Overview

The oldest building in Môrivôs and the spiritual heart of the Accord. A circular hall beneath a copper dome, built three hundred years ago on the exact spot where the five faction leaders signed the founding pact. No violence has occurred within its walls in three centuries — until now. Currently sealed and under investigation.

## Atmosphere

**Sights**: A circular room, perhaps forty feet across. Five high-backed chairs arranged around a pentagonal stone table. The dome above is lined with copper that has oxidized to green, casting a particular underwater light. Murals of the five founders ring the walls. The Founding Seal's display case — glass, gold-framed — stands empty near the east wall.
**Sounds**: Silence that feels deliberate, as if the room itself is holding its breath. Footsteps echo. Even whispers carry.
**Smells**: Old stone, polished wood, a trace of the ritual incense burned during Council sessions, and underneath — faint, almost imagined — something chemical. Alchemical.
**Feeling**: Violated. A sacred space that has been profaned. The reverence the room commands makes the crime worse.

## Key Features

### The Council Table
A pentagonal table carved from a single block of limestone, brought up from the plateau's quarry during the founding. Each face bears the founding seal of one faction, deeply carved. The Fifth Principal's seat faces the entrance.

### The Founding Murals
Five panels depicting the signing of the Accord. Each founder is portrayed in their faction's style. Close examination reveals subtle differences from the official histories — details that may have been altered over the centuries.

### The Seal's Display Case
A glass-and-gold case designed to hold the Founding Seal (Zigilua Zahar). The glass is intact — no signs of forced entry. The lock has not been picked. The Seal is simply gone.

### The Wards
Magical protections maintained by the Veil, designed to prevent violence within the Chamber. They failed on the night of the murder. Examining them reveals they weren't broken — they were deactivated from inside, using a method that requires knowledge of their construction.

## Points of Interest

- **The Pose of Accord marking**: Faint chalk lines on the floor where Caudressô's body was found. The Pose of Accord — arms crossed, palms up — matches the founder depicted in the north mural.
- **Scratch marks**: Faint scratches on the interior of the Seal's display case. As if someone placed something inside, then removed it hastily. Not consistent with theft — consistent with concealment.
- **The east wall**: Thicker than the others by six inches (detectable with Stonecunning or careful measurement). The original architectural plans show a compartment here that does not appear in the current building records.

## Secrets & Hidden Things

- The Founding Seal is hidden in a compartment in the east wall. Caudressô placed it there using knowledge from the original architectural plans that the Dorêve Circle thought were their exclusive property.
- A faint alchemical residue on the Fifth Principal's chair — Veilsleep, detectable with DC 15 Investigation + alchemist's supplies or Arcana check. The Veil's sweep "missed" this.
- One of the founding murals has been subtly altered — a sixth figure, partially painted over, is visible under the north panel if examined with Detect Magic or careful physical inspection. A founder who was written out of history.

## History

Built in Year One of the Accord. The five faction leaders gathered on this plateau, signed the founding document in blood and ink, and swore that this chamber would be inviolate. For three hundred years, it has been. Councils have shouted, threatened, and walked out — but no one has drawn a weapon, cast a hostile spell, or harmed another person inside these walls.

The wards were added in Year Twelve, after a threat that the histories describe as "an external attempt on Council security." They've been maintained by the Veil ever since — inspected annually, tested monthly, and never activated in response to actual violence. Until now.

## GM Notes

The Accord Chamber is the campaign's central crime scene and its most symbolically loaded location. Every discovery here carries emotional weight — the players should feel the violation of the space.

Key investigation beats in this location:
1. **First visit (Day 3-4)**: General examination. Discover the body position, the missing Seal, the deactivated wards. The Veil's "clean" report should feel suspicious but not provably wrong.
2. **Second visit (Day 6-8)**: Deeper examination with better tools/knowledge. Discover the alchemical residue, the scratch marks, the wall thickness discrepancy.
3. **Third visit (Act 2)**: Armed with the architectural plans (from Zubeldia), find the hidden compartment and the Founding Seal. This is a turning-point scene.

The hidden mural detail (the painted-over sixth founder) is a deep-lore bonus for observant players. It connects to the original history of the Accord — before the founding, there were six factions, not five. The sixth was absorbed into what became the Mint. This is a Dorêve Circle secret.
