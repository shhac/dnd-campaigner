# Session 1: Beat Sheet

**Opening State**: Day 3 of the Thirty-Day Clock. The four faction operatives have been summoned to the Pallisette's Salle de Consigne to receive their joint investigation mandate. They have not met before. The tension between factions is palpable.

---

## Beat 1: The Mandate

**Goal**: Introduce the PCs to each other, establish the stakes, deliver the investigation mandate

### What Happens
- The four Council members are seated at one end of the briefing table. The four operatives at the other.
- Each Council member introduces their operative with a carefully neutral statement that manages to communicate factional pride and territorial warning simultaneously.
- Marshal Aitzolenne reads the formal mandate: full access to the Accord Chamber and Caudressô's office, authority to question any Pallisette staff, a temporary office in the Pallisette for the team, and a thirty-day deadline that matches the Constitutional Clock.
- Director Pallenne adds, almost as an afterthought: "The Veil's preliminary sweep of the Accord Chamber found nothing unusual. I trust you'll want to verify that independently."
- The Council departs. The four operatives are alone together for the first time.

### Party Opportunities
- Introduce themselves (or not — silence is a choice)
- Ask the Council members questions before they leave
- Establish working dynamics — who takes the lead? Who trusts whom?
- Read the mandate document carefully (it contains specific legal authorities and also specific limitations)

### Tone
Formal and charged. Four professionals assessing each other. Every word is a chess move.

---

## Beat 2: The Accord Chamber

**Goal**: First examination of the crime scene. Establish physical evidence and raise questions.

### What Happens
- Custodian Arrô Thémelisse unlocks the Accord Chamber with visible reluctance. He's been custodian for twenty years and personally found the body.
- The room is preserved as it was found. Chalk outlines mark where Caudressô's body lay. The Seal's display case stands empty.
- The Veil's "clean" report is available but sparse — three pages for a crime scene that should warrant thirty.

### Physical Evidence Available
- **The body position**: Chalk marks show the Pose of Accord. Someone arranged him — this wasn't how he fell.
- **The Seal's display case**: Glass intact, lock functional, no signs of forced entry. DC 13 Investigation: faint scratch marks inside the case, inconsistent with the Seal being removed — consistent with something being placed inside and then taken out again.
- **The Fifth Principal's chair**: DC 15 Investigation with alchemist's supplies or DC 18 Arcana: faint chemical residue. Colorless, odorless, almost undetectable. An alchemist would recognize it as the signature of a refined poison.
- **The wards**: DC 14 Arcana: the protective wards weren't broken — they were deactivated cleanly, from inside, by someone who understood their construction.
- **The walls**: DC 20 Investigation or DC 15 with Stonecunning: the east wall is six inches thicker than the others. This is not in the current building records.

### Key Questions to Raise
- If the wards were deactivated from inside, someone with ward access was involved
- The Seal wasn't stolen through the case — was it even in the case when Caudressô died?
- The body was arranged. This was theatrical. Someone was sending a message.

### Custodian Thémelisse Can Provide
- He found the body at dawn. The Chamber was locked from outside — he used his key.
- Caudressô often worked late in the Chamber. This wasn't unusual.
- The Seal was in its case when Thémelisse locked up the previous evening (or so he believes)
- He noticed nothing unusual about the wards, but he's not trained to assess them

---

## Beat 3: Caudressô's Office

**Goal**: Establish the victim's final activities. Introduce the cipher journal as a missing MacGuffin.

### What Happens
- Caudressô's office in the Pallisette has been searched — but professionally, not ransacked. Drawers are closed but their contents have been shifted. Books are on shelves but in the wrong order.
- His desk calendar shows meetings for the weeks before his death: routine Council business, but also several unmarked entries (times and locations with no names)
- A locked desk drawer has been forced open. Whatever was inside is gone.

### Evidence Available
- **The calendar**: DC 12 Investigation: the unmarked meetings correspond to locations outside the Pallisette — a tavern in the old city, the Rampart archives, a bookshop near the Academy.
- **The desk**: DC 14 Investigation: the drawer was forced with a tool — pry marks. But the rest of the search was done by hand, carefully. Two different people? One careful, one in a hurry?
- **Personal effects**: Spectacles (on the desk, not on his body), ink bottles, correspondence (routine), a half-written letter to someone called "Z." (Zubeldia) that breaks off mid-sentence: "The notation in the eastern folio confirms what we suspected. The original text read—"
- **Books**: DC 15 Investigation: one shelf contains histories of the Accord's founding. Several books have pages marked with small notes. The notes are in cipher.

### Key Questions to Raise
- What was in the locked drawer? (The cipher journal — now missing)
- Who is "Z." and what were they working on together?
- Why was Caudressô researching the Accord's founding text?
- Who searched the office — and was it before or after the murder?

---

## Beat 4: The First Loyalty Test (Optional)

**Goal**: Each faction privately contacts their operative with "supplementary instructions." This beat can happen at the end of Session 1 or the beginning of Session 2.

### What Happens
- Each PC receives a private message from their faction through appropriate channels:
  - **Vélissande (Mint)**: Orrenne Thoraudenne (her mother) sends word: "If the investigation touches the Mint's financial records, delay access until I've reviewed them. This is a precaution, not an obstruction."
  - **Goitxarri (Rampart)**: Marshal Aitzolenne's sealed orders, opened now: "Find the truth. If it implicates the Rampart, bring it to me first. I will decide what to do. Trust your judgment, Lieutenant."
  - **Kes (Commons)**: Speaker Brûveisse's runner delivers a note: "Caudressô was looking into the Accord's history. So am I. Whatever you find about the original document, bring it to me before you share it with the others."
  - **Théodarrhe (Veil)**: A dead-drop message in Pallenne's cipher: "The poison is called Veilsleep. It's ours. A batch went missing six months ago. Find out who took it. Tell no one."

### Party Opportunities
- Share these messages with the group (risky but trust-building)
- Conceal them (safe but creates information asymmetry)
- Selectively share — reveal the existence of faction instructions without revealing the content

### Key Decisions
- Each PC must decide: faction first, or investigation first?
- This decision will shape the rest of the campaign

---

## Session End States

### Strong Ending Points
1. **After the Accord Chamber examination** — the crime scene has raised more questions than it answered
2. **After Caudressô's office** — the missing journal and the letter to "Z." give the investigation its first leads
3. **After the loyalty test** — each PC has a secret, and the real game has begun

### Open Threads for Session 2
- Who is "Z." and where are they? (Zubeldia Galarreta — in hiding)
- What was in the cipher journal?
- Who has ward access in the Accord Chamber?
- What does the chemical residue tell them about the murder method?
- What does the east wall's thickness mean?
- Will anyone share their faction's private instructions?

## GM Notes

### Pacing
- Don't rush the introductions. Four professionals who don't trust each other, forced to work together — let the tension breathe.
- The crime scene should feel methodical. Let them explore, ask questions, examine details. Reward thoroughness.
- If the session runs long, save Beat 4 (loyalty test) for Session 2's opening. It works well as a between-scenes development.

### Combat Considerations
- No combat in Session 1. This is an investigation. If someone tries to start a fight, the Pallisette guards intervene.
- The danger in this session is social, not physical. Wrong words, misplaced trust, missed clues.

### Character Spotlights
- **Vélissande**: Natural fit for examining financial records and the office search (Investigation, forensic analysis)
- **Kes**: Natural fit for talking to Custodian Thémelisse (Persuasion, puts people at ease)
- **Goitxarri**: Natural fit for the Accord Chamber's physical evidence (Stonecunning for the wall, military eye for the ward deactivation)
- **Théodarrhe**: Natural fit for the arcane evidence (Arcana for wards, familiar for scouting, knowledge of Veil methods)

### The Veil's Report
Pallenne's "preliminary sweep found nothing" is a lie of omission. The Veil found the Veilsleep residue and identified it immediately. Pallenne suppressed this because it implicates the Veil. If the PCs discover the residue independently, Théodarrhe will know (from Pallenne's dead-drop) that the Veil already knew. This creates an immediate loyalty crisis.

### Custodian Thémelisse
A genuinely traumatized civilian who found his employer dead in the room he's maintained for twenty years. Not a suspect. Not a source of hidden information. Just a human being dealing with the worst morning of his life. Treat him with dignity. If the PCs are kind to him, he'll remember and be helpful later.
