# World Primer: Môrivôs

What any inhabitant of Môrivôs would know.

---

## The City

Môrivôs is a free port on the Alcyone Coast — beholden to no kingdom, governed by its own Accord. It sits where three rivers meet the sea, built on a natural harbor that made it the busiest trading port on the continent within a generation of its founding. The city climbs from the waterfront up terraced hills to the Pallisette, the government plateau at its center.

## The Gilded Accord

Three hundred years ago, five factions that had been killing each other over control of the port signed the Gilded Accord — a binding pact to share governance. Each faction holds one seat on the Quintenary Council. Decisions require three of five votes. No faction may maintain armed forces within the Pallisette. The Accord is the closest thing Môrivôs has to a constitution, and most citizens treat it with near-religious reverence.

## The Five Factions

- **The Mint** (La Fraternité Dorêve): The bankers and trade houses. They control the currency, the port fees, and most of the city's wealth. Their council seat speaks for commerce.
- **The Rampart** (Errialde Mendizabal): The military order that defends the walls and gates. Old families with Euskaran names, martial traditions, and a code of honor they take seriously.
- **The Commons Assembly** (L'Assemblée Ghalliverre): The guilds and working people. The newest faction — added to the Accord eighty years after the founding. Represents everyone who isn't rich, armed, or secretive.
- **The Veil** (La Chambre Éteinte): The intelligence service. Everyone knows it exists. Nobody knows exactly what it does. They keep the Accord safe from external threats and, some whisper, from internal ones.
- **The Fifth Seat**: Rotates among independent voices — scholars, clergy, retired diplomats. Meant to be a tiebreaker and moral compass. Currently vacant.

## The Quintenary Council

Five Principals govern the city. Each serves until death, retirement, or formal recall by their faction. When a seat is vacant, the remaining four have thirty days to confirm a replacement or the Accord enters crisis. This has happened twice in three hundred years. Both times, the city nearly tore itself apart.

## Daily Life

Môrivôs is loud, crowded, and cosmopolitan. Fifty languages on the docks. Street food from every coast. The rich live on the upper terraces; the poor live in Salthook and the Spillways near the waterfront. Guild membership matters — it determines your vote in the Assembly, your access to work, and your standing in disputes. Religion is personal and varied; the city has no state faith.

## Currency

The Gilded Tôt — small gold-washed coins stamped with the Accord seal. Managed by the Mint. Trusted across the Alcyone Coast because Môrivôs guarantees their weight. Counterfeiting is one of the few crimes punishable by exile.

## What Everyone Knows Right Now

Councillor Caudressô is dead. Found in the Accord Chamber three days ago. No one is saying how. The Council has invoked the Thirty-Day Clock. The factions are circling. And the people of Môrivôs are holding their breath.
