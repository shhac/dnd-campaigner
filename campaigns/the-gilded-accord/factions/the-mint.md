# The Mint (La Fraternité Dorêve)

**Type**: Financial Oligarchy / Central Bank
**Influence**: Dominant (local), Significant (regional — the Gilded Tôt is traded across the coast)
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Overview

The financial backbone of Môrivôs. The Mint controls the city's currency, manages port fees, and underwrites the trade agreements that make the free port viable. It is simultaneously the institution that keeps the lights on and the institution that profits most from the way those lights are wired. Every transaction in Môrivôs passes through the Mint's ledgers, and the Mint never forgets a debt.

## Goals

**Public Goal**: Maintain Môrivôs's position as the premier free port on the Alcyone Coast. Stable currency, favorable trade terms, prosperity for all.
**True Goal**: Preserve and expand the Mint's financial control. The altered Accord gives the Mint hidden structural advantages — dormancy powers, preferential debt treatment, and veto authority over currency policy — that have compounded over three centuries.

## Methods

- Financial leverage: The Mint holds debt over every other faction and most major institutions
- Information through transactions: Every trade, every purchase, every loan passes through Mint systems
- Political patronage: Funding campaigns, sponsoring guild events, endowing Academy chairs
- Quiet property acquisition through intermediaries in the lower wards

## Structure

**Leadership**: The Principal (Rôsamunde Ghalliverre) chairs a board of twelve Directors, each representing a major trade house
**Hierarchy**: Principal → Directors → Senior Auditors → Auditors → Clerks → Runners
**Membership**: Roughly 400 direct employees, plus hundreds of contracted agents. Membership in the Fraternité's inner circle requires family connections or exceptional financial talent.

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Vast — they literally control the money supply
- **Military**: None directly, but can hire mercenaries and have funded Rampart operations in exchange for favors
- **Magic**: Limited — a few contracted diviners for fraud detection
- **Political**: Dominant — money buys influence, and they have all the money

## Hooks & Opportunities

- The Mint's financial records contain clues about Caudressô's movements and meetings
- Orrenne Thoraudenne (Vélissande's mother) is a potential source or obstacle
- The dormancy clause in the altered Accord is the Mint's nuclear option
- Mint debt records could reveal who funded what — including, potentially, a murder

## GM Notes

The Mint is the campaign's institutional antagonist. Not because its members are evil — most are pragmatic professionals who believe they're keeping the city running — but because the system they maintain is built on a three-century-old fraud. The altered Accord gave the Mint advantages that compounded over generations until the Mint became indispensable by design, not by merit.

Rôsamunde is the face of this system. She didn't create it, but she maintains it, and she'll invoke the dormancy clause to protect it. The PCs can potentially separate Rôsamunde from the Mint's institutional interests — but only if they can offer an alternative that addresses her genuine fear that the city will collapse without financial stability.

The Mint's internal investigation division (run by Orrenne Thoraudenne) is a parallel threat. They're investigating the murder too — and if they find evidence that threatens the Mint, they'll bury it.
