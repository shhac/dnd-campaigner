# The Rampart (Errialde Mendizabal)

**Type**: Military Order / City Defense
**Influence**: Significant (local), Limited (regional — their mandate is the city's walls)
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Overview

The shield of Môrivôs. The Rampart defends the walls, gates, and harbor approaches. It maintains the garrison, patrols the streets, and serves as the city's police force for anything beyond petty crime. Founded by dwarven clans who built the original fortifications, it retains a strong Euskaran cultural identity even as its ranks have diversified over centuries.

## Goals

**Public Goal**: Defend the city from external threats and maintain internal order. The walls stand; the gates hold; the people are safe.
**True Goal**: Same as public, with a complication: the Rampart's founding families have accumulated political and economic influence that goes beyond their military mandate. Previous generations were complicit in the Accord's alteration — a secret that current leadership doesn't know.

## Methods

- Military presence: Patrols, garrison rotations, gate inspections
- Honor-bound service: Officers swear oaths that are taken seriously — discipline is enforced from within
- Institutional tradition: The Rampart's procedures are centuries old, documented in Euskaran, and followed precisely
- Quiet influence through the founding families' social networks

## Structure

**Leadership**: The Marshal (Etxebarria Aitzolenne) commands all Rampart forces and holds a Council seat
**Hierarchy**: Marshal → Captains → Lieutenants → Sergeants → Soldiers → Cadets
**Membership**: Approximately 800 active-duty soldiers, plus 200 reserves and a cadet corps of 50. Open to all races, but the officer corps remains predominantly dwarven.

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Moderate — funded by the city treasury (which the Mint manages, creating a dependency)
- **Military**: Strong — the only faction with a standing army. Well-trained, well-equipped, professional.
- **Magic**: Limited — a small corps of runic engineers who maintain the city's defensive wards
- **Political**: Moderate — respected but constrained by their military mandate

## Symbols & Recognition

**Symbol**: A mountain gate — two pillars supporting an arch, with a star above
**Colors**: Copper and stone gray
**Recognition Signs**: Rank insignia in the Euskaran tradition (copper rings on braids, shoulder markings on armor). Officers salute with a fist to the chest. The Euskaran phrase "Atea zabalik" ("The gate stands open") is both a greeting and a statement of readiness.

## Hooks & Opportunities

- The Rampart archives contain the institutional records that show gaps — documents removed by Dorêve Circle members over generations
- Zubeldia Galarreta (archivist) is the party's key witness and a Rampart member
- The sealed predecessor letter in the Marshal's possession contains a confession about the altered Accord
- The Mendizabal Steps vaults contain the original Accord — accessible only with the Founding Seal

## GM Notes

The Rampart is the campaign's most sympathetic faction and the one most damaged by the truth. Unlike the Mint, the Rampart's current leadership is genuinely honorable. Marshal Aitzolenne is exactly what he appears to be. But his predecessors were complicit in the Accord's alteration — and that complicity secured the Rampart's permanent garrison rights, which are the foundation of everything the Rampart has become.

When the truth comes out, the Rampart faces an existential question: if their founding bargain was corrupt, is their authority legitimate? Goitxarri's personal arc mirrors this institutional crisis. The Rampart can survive the truth — but only if it chooses to be defined by its principles rather than its privileges.

The Marshal's sealed letter is a ticking bomb. He hasn't opened it because the condition hasn't been met ("when the Accord is threatened"). The investigation may prompt him to open it early, or the PCs may learn of its existence and ask him to. Either way, the confession inside will change everything for the Rampart.
