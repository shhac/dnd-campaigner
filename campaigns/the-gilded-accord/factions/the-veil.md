# The Veil (La Chambre Éteinte)

**Type**: Intelligence Service / Secret Police
**Influence**: Pervasive (local), Unknown (regional — that's the point)
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Overview

The Veil sees everything and says nothing. Established by the Accord to protect the city from external threats, it has evolved into something more ambiguous — part intelligence agency, part secret police, part institutional memory. Everyone knows it exists. No one knows exactly what it does. That uncertainty is the Veil's most potent weapon.

## Goals

**Public Goal**: Protect the Accord and the city from threats both foreign and domestic. Gather intelligence, neutralize dangers, maintain stability.
**True Goal**: The institution's survival above all else. Director Pallenne believes the Veil is essential. Dorêve Circle members within the Veil believe it's their tool. These two objectives are on a collision course.

## Methods

- Surveillance: Networks of informants, agents, and sympathizers throughout the city
- Counter-intelligence: Monitoring other factions' activities, disrupting external threats
- "Quiet solutions": Disappearances, reputational destruction, and — rarely — assassination
- Information brokering: Knowing secrets and choosing when to reveal them
- Ward maintenance: The Veil maintains the magical wards on key government buildings

## Structure

**Leadership**: The Director (Lôcren Pallenne) answers only to the full Council — no single faction controls her
**Hierarchy**: Director → Section Chiefs → Senior Operatives → Operatives → Contacts → Assets
**Membership**: Unknown. Estimated at 150-200 active operatives, plus an unquantifiable network of informants. Many Veil agents maintain cover identities in other factions or civilian life.

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Significant — funded directly from the city treasury through an opaque budget
- **Military**: Elite — small numbers, but highly trained in covert operations, infiltration, and targeted violence
- **Magic**: Significant — ward maintenance, divination assets, and a stock of alchemical tools including Veilsleep
- **Political**: Immense — through information control rather than direct power

## Symbols & Recognition

**Symbol**: None official — the Veil doesn't advertise. Informally: a closed door.
**Colors**: None — Veil agents dress to blend
**Recognition Signs**: Code phrases that change monthly. Dead drop protocols. A specific way of folding correspondence that identifies the sender's clearance level. Agents who need to identify each other in public use a sequence of three seemingly casual gestures.

## Hooks & Opportunities

- The missing Veilsleep batch is a critical piece of evidence — if the PCs can prove it was used
- Pallenne's willingness to burn the Veil to save it is a potential alliance opportunity
- The Dorêve Circle members within the Veil are the investigation's most dangerous obstacles
- The Veil's historical archives may contain records of every Dorêve Circle operation — if they can be accessed

## GM Notes

The Veil is the campaign's most internally divided faction. Director Pallenne is fighting a shadow war against infiltrators she can barely identify. The Dorêve Circle members within the Veil have been maintaining their cover for so long that they've risen to senior positions — Section Chiefs, analysts, ward maintainers. The person who deactivated the Accord Chamber's wards was a Veil agent.

Gattô Théveresse deserves special attention. He's the campaign's physical antagonist — a retired Veil operative reactivated by the Dorêve Circle to handle "complications." He killed Caudressô. He killed the Pallisette clerk. He will attempt to kill the PCs if they get too close. He's methodical, patient, and very good at his job. His combat stats should be high enough to be genuinely threatening to the party.

**Gattô Théveresse — Quick Stats**:
- **Use Stat Block**: Assassin (high CR, modified)
- **AC**: 17 (studded leather, DEX) | **HP**: 78
- **Notable Abilities**: Assassinate, Sneak Attack (6d6), Evasion, expertise in Stealth and Deception, proficiency with poisoner's kit, carries Veilsleep doses
- **Tactics**: Never confronts directly. Uses ambush, poison, and environmental hazards. If cornered, fights with lethal efficiency and escapes if possible.

The Veil's complicity in the Accord Chamber ward failure is the investigation's most explosive evidence. The wards weren't broken from outside — they were deactivated by someone with maintenance access. This points directly at the Veil, which is why Pallenne is so afraid and so desperate to find the truth before it finds her.
