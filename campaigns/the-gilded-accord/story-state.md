## Recent Events Summary

Three days ago, Councillor Îsombré Caudressô was found dead in the Accord Chamber by the morning custodian. His body was arranged in the Pose of Accord — arms crossed, palms up, the position depicted in the founding murals. The Founding Seal was gone from its display case. His office, when checked, had been thoroughly searched. The Veil's preliminary sweep found no signs of forced entry, no poison residue, and no magical traces — which itself is suspicious.

The remaining four Council members convened an emergency session and invoked the Thirty-Day Clock. They also agreed, after bitter argument, to a joint investigation staffed by one operative from each faction. This is unprecedented. No one trusts it to work.
