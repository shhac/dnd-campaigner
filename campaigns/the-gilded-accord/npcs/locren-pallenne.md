# Director Lôcren Pallenne

**Role**: Faction Leader / Spymaster / Ambiguous Ally
**Location**: Unknown (officially: the Pallisette, Veil offices; practically: she moves)
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Half-Elf |
| **Occupation** | Director of the Veil (La Chambre Éteinte), Quintenary Council member |
| **Age** | 73 (appears mid-40s) |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

Unremarkable by design. Medium height, medium build, brown hair fading to gray, features that slide out of memory the moment you look away. She cultivates forgettability the way others cultivate presence. The only distinctive feature: her hands, which are scarred across the knuckles from a career that didn't begin behind a desk. She wears no insignia, carries no badge, and changes her clothing style daily.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Calm, precise, and deeply tired in a way she rarely lets show
**Motivation**: Protect the Veil as an institution. She believes the Veil is the only thing standing between Môrivôs and the chaos that other cities suffer. She'll sacrifice anything to keep it functional — including her own people.
**Fear**: That the Veil is already compromised beyond saving. That the rot is too deep.

**Personality Traits**:
- Speaks as if reading from a report — facts first, analysis second, opinion never. When she does offer an opinion, it means the situation is desperate.
- Treats every person as a potential asset or a potential threat. The categories are not mutually exclusive.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Quiet, precise, with pauses that feel loaded. Never uses two sentences when one will do. Asks questions she already knows the answers to, then watches for the lie.

**Mannerisms**: Maintains exactly the right amount of eye contact — enough to be engaged, not enough to be memorable. Sits with her back to the wall. Never writes anything down in front of others.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "The Veil's preliminary analysis found nothing. I want you to understand how unusual that is."
- "I chose Théodarrhe because they ask difficult questions. I trust you'll extend them the same courtesy."
- "If I knew who killed Caudressô, I wouldn't need an investigation. I'd need a quieter room."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Director for 11 years — appointed after her predecessor died of "natural causes"
- Reputation for efficiency and ruthlessness in equal measure
- The Veil under her leadership has prevented two assassination attempts on Council members (that the public knows of)
- She's the youngest Director in Veil history

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- A batch of Veilsleep (the poison that killed Caudressô) went missing from Veil stores six months ago
- She suspects at least two senior Veil operatives are compromised — working for an interest beyond the Veil
- She knows more about the other factions' internal operations than any of them would be comfortable with

### Secrets (GM only)
- She identified the poison immediately but did not share this with the Council
- She knows the Veil has Dorêve Circle members in its ranks but not how many or how high they go
- She assigned Théodarrhe specifically as a double agent — investigating the murder AND investigating the Veil's own people
- She has a contingency plan: if the Veil is fully compromised, she will burn it down herself and rebuild from the ashes
- She does not know about the altered Accord. The Veil members of the Dorêve Circle have kept it from her.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Cooperative in a way that invites suspicion. She provides exactly enough information to be useful and withholds exactly enough to maintain leverage.

**Current Disposition**: Opaque (deliberately)

**History with Party**: Issued the joint mandate. Maintains private contact with Théodarrhe through dead drops.

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Veilsleep poison, internal Veil corruption, Théodarrhe's loyalty arc, the Dorêve Circle

**Potential Uses**:
- Source of intelligence resources — surveillance, analysis, historical records
- The person who can identify every Dorêve Circle member when she finally commits to action
- Her decision to "burn or save" the Veil is a critical Act 3 moment
- Can provide the PCs with the missing piece they need — if she trusts them enough

## GM Notes

Pallenne is the most isolated faction leader. She runs the intelligence service and yet she's the one being kept in the dark. The Dorêve Circle members within the Veil have been very careful to keep the altered Accord's existence from their own Director — because they know she'd destroy them if she found out.

She's playing a dangerous game with Théodarrhe. She needs an operative she can trust, but she can't tell them the full scope of what she suspects without compromising her position. So she gives Théodarrhe enough rope to investigate and hopes they pull the right threads.

Her endgame depends on what the investigation reveals. If the Veil is salvageable, she'll purge it and rebuild. If it's not, she'll expose everything — including things that will end her career and possibly her life — because the alternative is an intelligence service in the hands of people who've been lying for centuries.
