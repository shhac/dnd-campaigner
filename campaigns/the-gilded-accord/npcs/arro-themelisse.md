# Custodian Arrô Thémelisse

**Role**: Witness / Civilian
**Location**: The Pallisette — the Accord Chamber and its adjacent offices
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Custodian of the Accord Chamber |
| **Age** | 63 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

A small, neat man with the careful posture of someone who has spent a lifetime maintaining precious things. Thinning white hair combed precisely. Clean-shaven. Hands that tremble slightly since the morning he found the body. He wears the custodian's traditional charcoal coat with brass buttons — the same style the position has worn for two centuries — and keeps it immaculate despite everything. His eyes are red-rimmed and tired. He has not been sleeping well.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Nervous but cooperative. Wants desperately to be helpful and equally desperately wants all of this to be over.
**Motivation**: Duty to the Accord Chamber itself — not the politics, but the physical space. He has maintained it for over forty years and considers it sacred.
**Fear**: That someone will blame him. That the wards failed because of something he missed. That the Chamber will never feel safe again.

**Personality Traits**:
- Meticulous about details — remembers exactly where every object belongs, which tiles were replaced when, how the light falls at different hours. This precision makes him a surprisingly good witness.
- Apologizes constantly, even when he hasn't done anything wrong. A lifetime of serving powerful people has taught him that apologies are cheaper than consequences.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Formal, slightly old-fashioned diction. Speaks in complete sentences even under stress. Voice goes quiet when he's frightened, which is most of the time right now. Uses honorifics reflexively — "ser," "councillor," "officer." Tends to over-explain, circling back to correct minor details in his own statements.

**Mannerisms**: Wrings his hands when anxious. Straightens objects that are already straight. Stands when others sit, unless explicitly told to sit down. Looks at the floor when addressing anyone he perceives as authority.

**Sample Dialogue**: "I locked the Chamber at half-past the ninth bell, as I always do, ser. I checked the display case — the Seal was there, I'm certain of it. Or — I believe I'm certain. I looked at the case. I always look at the case. It was there. I would have noticed if it wasn't."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- He found the body at dawn on Day 1, when he arrived to open the Chamber for the morning session
- The Chamber was locked from outside — he used his key as he does every morning
- Councillor Caudressô often worked late in the Chamber; this was not unusual
- He has been custodian for forty-two years, inheriting the position from his uncle

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- The Seal was in its case when he locked up the previous evening — he is almost certain, but the doubt eats at him
- He noticed nothing unusual about the wards, but he has no training to assess them
- Caudressô had been working later than usual in the weeks before his death, often alone
- He heard voices in the Chamber one evening, about a week before the murder — Caudressô and someone else, arguing. He didn't recognize the second voice. He didn't report it because councillors are entitled to private meetings.

### Secrets (GM only)
- He is genuinely innocent. He has no connection to the conspiracy.
- The voices he heard were Caudressô and Zubeldia Galarreta arguing about what to do with their discovery about the Accord's founding text.
- He possesses a spare key to the Chamber that he has never reported — his uncle gave it to him as a backup. It has never been copied, and he keeps it in his quarters. This key is not relevant to the murder (the wards, not the lock, were the real barrier) but its existence would make him look suspicious if discovered.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Frightened, cooperative, grateful for anyone who treats him with basic decency. Will open up significantly to anyone who shows patience and kindness.
**Current Disposition**: Neutral (trending Friendly with decent treatment)
**History with Party**: Will meet them in Beat 2 when he unlocks the Accord Chamber for their examination.

## Combat Stats

**Use Stat Block**: Commoner
**AC**: 10 | **HP**: 4

**Notable Abilities**: None combat-relevant. Expert knowledge of the Accord Chamber's physical layout, maintenance history, and daily routines. Can identify when objects have been moved or disturbed.

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Caudressô murder (found the body), the Accord Chamber examination, the overheard argument
**Potential Uses**:
- Key witness for establishing timeline and physical evidence
- The overheard argument is a lead to Zubeldia Galarreta
- If treated well, he becomes a reliable ongoing source — he notices things in the Pallisette that others overlook
- The spare key is a red herring that could waste investigation time or, if handled carefully, build trust (he'll confess it if asked directly and treated kindly)

## GM Notes

Arrô is not a suspect, a plant, or a hidden asset. He is a traumatized civilian who found his employer dead in the room he has lovingly maintained for four decades. Play him with dignity. His nervousness is real. His guilt is survivor's guilt, not criminal guilt. His precision about details makes him valuable, and his willingness to help should feel earned by the PCs' treatment of him.

If Kes is the one who primarily interviews him, Arrô will respond well — Kes's directness and working-class background put him at ease more than Vélissande's precision or Théodarrhe's questions-behind-questions. Goitxarri's military formality is neutral — Arrô is used to it from years of dealing with Rampart guards.

The overheard argument is the most important piece of information Arrô possesses. He won't volunteer it immediately — he's afraid of implicating himself (why didn't he report it?). A DC 12 Persuasion check with sympathetic framing, or a DC 15 Insight check to notice he's holding something back, will draw it out.
