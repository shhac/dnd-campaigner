# Marshal Etxebarria Aitzolenne

**Role**: Faction Leader / Authority Figure / Potential Ally
**Location**: The Pallisette, Rampart headquarters (Irrintzi Hall)
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Dwarf (Mountain) |
| **Occupation** | Marshal of the Rampart (Errialde Mendizabal), Quintenary Council member |
| **Age** | 147 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

Built like the walls he defends — broad, immovable, weathered by decades of service. His beard is iron-gray, braided in the traditional Euskaran military pattern, bound with copper rings that denote rank. He wears the Rampart's formal armor even in the Pallisette — not for protection but for principle. The armor is old, maintained with obsessive care, and bears the marks of actual combat from campaigns most humans don't remember.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Stern but not cold. Speaks with the weight of someone who has earned the right to be listened to.
**Motivation**: Honor and duty. The Rampart exists to protect the city. That mission is sacred, and he will not allow it to be compromised by politics.
**Fear**: That the Rampart's honor has already been compromised — that the institution he's given his life to has been complicit in things he doesn't know about.

**Personality Traits**:
- Holds silence like a weapon. In meetings, his quiet commands more attention than anyone else's speech.
- Judges people by what they do, not what they say. Has no patience for rhetoric without action.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Low, gravelly, with a strong Euskaran accent. Short declarative sentences. Uses military terminology instinctively. Never cusses — considers it undisciplined.

**Mannerisms**: Clasps his hands behind his back when listening. Nods once to indicate agreement — no wasted gestures. Inspects rooms the way soldiers inspect fortifications.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "The Accord Chamber was breached. That is a military failure. I take responsibility."
- "Lieutenant Zumarratz. You will find the truth. You will bring it to me. Is that understood?"
- "I have served seven Council members in my lifetime. Caudressô was the best of them. He deserved better than this."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Served as Marshal for 31 years — the longest tenure in Rampart history
- Fought in the Corsair War and the Harbor Riots
- Known as incorruptible — three separate factions have tried to buy him over the decades, all failed
- His personal loyalty to the Accord is absolute

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- The Accord Chamber's wards were Veil-maintained, and they failed. He suspects Veil involvement.
- He's aware that the Rampart's founding families have accumulated political influence beyond their military mandate
- He chose Goitxarri specifically because the younger officer has no political connections to leverage

### Secrets (GM only)
- He has begun to suspect that previous Rampart Marshals were involved in something they didn't record — gaps in the institutional records going back generations
- His predecessor left a sealed letter to be opened "when the Accord is threatened." He hasn't opened it yet.
- He is not a member of the Dorêve Circle and has no knowledge of the altered Accord. Learning the truth will shatter his worldview.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Formal, professional. He chose Goitxarri, tolerates the other faction operatives, and expects results.

**Current Disposition**: Neutral

**History with Party**: Issued the joint mandate alongside the other Council members.

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Rampart's honor, the sealed predecessor letter, Goitxarri's loyalty arc

**Potential Uses**:
- Authority figure who can open (or close) doors for the investigation
- The person the PCs bring the truth to when they need someone with the power and will to act on it
- His reaction to learning about the altered Accord is a major emotional beat
- Can order the city gates closed or opened — a critical Act 3 decision

## GM Notes

Aitzolenne is the campaign's moral anchor. Unlike every other faction leader, he's exactly what he appears to be: a soldier who believes in duty, honor, and the Accord. He is not part of the conspiracy. He is not playing a long game. He will do the right thing when shown the right thing.

This makes him both the PCs' strongest potential ally and their most dangerous liability. If they bring him the truth too early, he'll act on it with military directness that may trigger the crisis it was supposed to prevent. If they wait too long, he'll act on incomplete information and make things worse.

The sealed letter from his predecessor contains a confession: "The Rampart agreed to the altered Accord in exchange for permanent garrison rights. We told ourselves it was for the city's protection. It was for ours." Reading this will break something in Aitzolenne that may never fully heal.
