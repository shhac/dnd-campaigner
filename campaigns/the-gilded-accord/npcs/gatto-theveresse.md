**Role**: Antagonist / Assassin
**Location**: Unknown (moves between safe houses in the lower wards and the old city)

## Appearance

Lean and weathered, with the economy of movement that comes from decades of professional violence. Close-cropped gray hair, deep-set eyes the color of wet slate, and a face that registers nothing unless he wants it to. His hands are his most notable feature — large, steady, with scarred knuckles and nails kept short. He dresses like a mid-tier merchant: unremarkable clothes in muted colors, well-maintained boots, nothing that catches the eye or stays in memory.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Patient, precise, utterly calm. Speaks rarely and means every word.
**Motivation**: Loyalty to the Dorêve Circle — specifically, loyalty to an ideal of Môrivôs that predates the Accord. He believes the city was stronger before the compromise.
**Fear**: Irrelevance. He retired once and it nearly destroyed him. The Circle's reactivation gave him purpose again, and he'll do anything to keep it.

**Personality Traits**:
- Takes professional pride in clean work. Caudressô's death was arranged with deliberate theatricality — the Pose of Accord was Gattô's touch, a message from the Circle to anyone who understood it.
- Genuinely courteous in casual interactions. Tips well, remembers names, never raises his voice. The courtesy is real. So is the capacity for violence underneath it.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Sparse, deliberate, with a faint old-city accent that surfaces on certain vowels. Never uses contractions. Favors short declarative sentences. When he does speak at length, it's because he's decided you're worth the effort — or he's buying time.

**Mannerisms**: Sits with his back to walls. Enters rooms and identifies exits before looking at people. Drinks slowly. Eats slowly. Does everything with a measured patience that makes rushing feel foolish.

**Sample Dialogue**: "I am not a complicated man. I was given a task. I completed it. What you do with that information is your concern."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Retired Veil operative, served with distinction for thirty years
- Known to frequent the old city's quieter taverns
- Has no family, no visible income source beyond a modest pension

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- Still maintains contacts within the Veil's operational network
- Has access to Veilsleep and knows its preparation
- Was seen in the Pallisette district in the days before Caudressô's death

### Secrets (GM only)
- Murdered Councillor Caudressô using Veilsleep — a Veil-proprietary poison that leaves no trace without alchemical analysis
- Arranged the body in the Pose of Accord on Dorêve Circle instructions
- Stole the Founding Seal from the display case (using ward access provided by a Veil insider)
- Killed the Pallisette clerk who noticed his presence that night
- Knows the full membership of the Dorêve Circle's active cell in Môrivôs
- The ward deactivation codes were provided by a senior Veil Section Chief loyal to the Circle

## GM Notes

Gattô is the campaign's primary physical antagonist in Acts 2-3. He should feel like a genuine threat — the party is level 3, and he fights like a level 5-6 assassin with years of field experience. He won't seek confrontation, but if the PCs get close to the truth, he'll move to eliminate them. His preferred method is isolation and ambush, not open battle.

His loyalty to the Dorêve Circle is ideological, not mercenary. He believes the Accord weakened Môrivôs and that the city needs to be remade. This makes him dangerous because he can't be bought off — only stopped.

The one crack in his armor: he respects the Veil as an institution, even as he works against its current leadership. If Théodarrhe appeals to him as a fellow operative, there's a narrow window where professional pride might override mission discipline. This is a DC 20+ Persuasion check with very specific framing.
