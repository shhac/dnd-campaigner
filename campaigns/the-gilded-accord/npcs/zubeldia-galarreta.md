# Zubeldia Galarreta

**Role**: Key Witness / Information Source / Endangered
**Location**: In hiding (last known: the old city, near the Mendizabal Steps)
**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Dwarf (Hill) |
| **Occupation** | Rampart Archivist (senior), Archive of Errialde Mendizabal |
| **Age** | 112 |
| **Status** | Alive, in hiding |

## Appearance

Small even for a dwarf, with the hunched posture of someone who has spent a century bent over documents. Thick spectacles. Fingers permanently stained with archival ink and preservation oils. She wears her hair in a single practical braid and dresses in the Rampart archivist's traditional charcoal and copper. Currently disguised in common dockworker's clothes — badly. She looks exactly like an archivist pretending to be a dockworker.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Nervous, precise, and stubborn in the specific way of someone who has dedicated their life to accuracy
**Motivation**: The truth. She helped Caudressô because the historical record had been falsified, and that offended her on a level deeper than politics.
**Fear**: Being found by whoever killed Caudressô. Also: being responsible for the city's collapse if the truth she helped uncover destroys the Accord.

**Personality Traits**:
- Corrects factual errors compulsively, even when it's socially inappropriate or physically dangerous
- Catastrophizes — imagines the worst possible outcome of every situation, then prepares for it. This has kept her alive so far.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Fast, detailed, with academic precision. Provides citations for claims. Interrupts herself with parenthetical clarifications. When frightened, speaks even faster.

**Mannerisms**: Adjusts her spectacles constantly. Wrings her hands when nervous (which is always, currently). Touches documents with reverent care, like a priest handling relics.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "The notation in the margin — third folio, second page, left column — doesn't match the ink formulation used in the founding year. It's newer by at least fifty years. Fifty years, do you understand what that means?"
- "I am not leaving this room until you explain how you plan to keep me alive."
- "Caudressô was right. I wish he'd been wrong. Being wrong is much safer."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Senior archivist of the Rampart for 40 years
- Considered the foremost expert on pre-Accord Môrivôs history
- Has not been seen at her post since Caudressô's death

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- She made copies of every document Caudressô accessed through her
- She can identify the specific alterations to the Accord text (ink analysis, marginalia dating, handwriting comparison)
- She knows the location of architectural plans that show the Accord Chamber's hidden compartments

### Secrets (GM only)
- She has a partial copy of Caudressô's cipher journal — he gave it to her as insurance
- She knows the Founding Seal is hidden inside the Accord Chamber but not exactly where
- She has identified three Dorêve Circle members through archival records but doesn't know the full membership
- She sent word to Goitxarri's clan (the Zumarratz family) through old connections, hoping for protection

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Terrified, suspicious, desperate. Will demand proof of identity and intent before sharing anything. May attempt to flee if approached clumsily.

**Current Disposition**: Unknown (hasn't met the party yet)

**History with Party**: None, but may contact Goitxarri through Rampart channels

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: Caudressô's research, the altered Accord, the cipher journal, the Founding Seal's location

**Potential Uses**:
- The primary source of historical evidence for the investigation
- Her protection becomes a secondary quest — she's a target
- Her expertise can decode portions of the cipher journal
- She knows where to find the architectural plans that reveal the Seal's hiding place
- Her testimony is needed if the PCs want to present evidence publicly

## GM Notes

Zubeldia is the campaign's most vulnerable NPC and the key to unlocking Act 2. She has the knowledge the PCs need, but getting to her and keeping her alive is a challenge. The Dorêve Circle knows she helped Caudressô and has people looking for her.

She should make contact around Day 12-15 of the Thirty-Day Clock, through a message left for Goitxarri at a Rampart garrison. She'll insist on meeting in a secure location and will test whoever comes with questions only a genuine Rampart officer could answer.

Once found, she's an information firehose — detailed, exhaustive, and prone to tangents. The PCs will need to ask the right questions to extract what matters. She's also a liability: she can't fight, she can't hide effectively, and she's terrified enough to make bad decisions under pressure.
