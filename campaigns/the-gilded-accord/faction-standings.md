## Standing Scale
- **Allied** (+3): Active partnership, shared resources, mutual defense
- **Friendly** (+2): Willing to help, share information freely, offer favorable terms
- **Sympathetic** (+1): Positively inclined, will assist if convenient
- **Neutral** (0): No strong opinion, default for new factions
- **Wary** (-1): Cautious, less forthcoming, higher DCs for social checks
- **Unfriendly** (-2): Actively unhelpful, may obstruct, significantly higher DCs
- **Hostile** (-3): Active opposition, may attack on sight

## Reputation Effects on Gameplay
- **Social check DC modifier**: Standing value * -2 (Allied = -6 to DC, Hostile = +6 to DC)
- **NPC initial attitude**: Faction-affiliated NPCs start conversations at the faction's standing level
- **Information access**: Friendly+ factions share rumors and warnings. Unfriendly+ factions withhold or mislead.
- **Quest availability**: Some quests only available at certain standing thresholds

## Special Rule: Cross-Faction Tension
When a PC takes an action that benefits their own faction at the expense of another, the benefiting faction gains +1 and the harmed faction loses -1. When a PC acts against their own faction's interests for the investigation's sake, their faction drops -1 but all other factions gain +1 (respect for integrity). These shifts are cumulative and permanent.
