# Campaign Preferences

**Campaign**: The Gilded Accord

## Narrative Style

**narrative_style**: hybrid

Uses clear speaker labels with flowing prose. Dialogue is attributed but woven into descriptive narrative rather than bare script format. Descriptions maintain literary quality while keeping conversation easy to follow.

## Tone Notes

- Political thriller with noir undertones
- Tension through dialogue and subtext, not action
- Every NPC has an agenda; every conversation is a negotiation
- Violence is rare, sudden, and consequential
- The city itself is a character — its moods, its rhythms, its factions
