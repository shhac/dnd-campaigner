# Session 1: Beat Sheet

**Opening State**: Four strangers in a back room at Hagos's shop in Teff Row. Paavai Sundaram is about to make her pitch. The Festival of Sealed Words is two days away.

---

## Beat 1: The Pitch

**Goal**: Establish the crew, let characters meet, and deliver the job offer.

### What Happens
- Paavai enters the back room, nervous but prepared. She has a leather folio of documents.
- She introduces herself: senior clerk at Selvan House, eighteen years of service, access to the upper vault levels.
- She explains what she found: the Ādhāra Protocol — a plan to make debt-marks hereditary across bloodlines. Children born to debtors would carry the marks from birth. No escape. Ever.
- She needs a crew to break into the Nallur Vaults and retrieve the Protocol documents — both to prove what Selvan House is planning and to prevent it.
- Payment: She has access to the vault's collateral chamber. Valuable items. But more than that — she can help each of them with what they actually need. She's done her research.

### Party Opportunities
- Ask Paavai questions (she's forthcoming but doesn't know everything)
- Assess each other — first impressions, sizing up the crew
- Negotiate terms, express concerns, push back on the plan
- Accept, refuse, or request more information before committing

### Tone
Tense, conspiratorial. The room is small, the stakes are enormous, and nobody trusts each other yet.

### Key Questions Paavai Can Answer
- What is the Ādhāra Protocol? (She knows the broad strokes, not the technical details)
- How does she know it's real? (She found the documents while misfiling — stumbled into a restricted section)
- Why not go to the Echoguard? (They serve the Counting Houses. They'd bury it.)
- Why not the Unbound? (She has been talking to them, but they want to demolish the vaults, not infiltrate them. She needs precision, not explosions.)
- What's in it for her? (She believes in the original system — fair contracts, freely entered. The Protocol perverts everything Kanakkur was supposed to be.)

---

## Beat 2: Getting to Know You

**Goal**: Character introductions, relationship establishment, first hints of hidden agendas.

### What Happens
- After Paavai leaves (she can't stay long — she needs to return to Selvan House before her absence is noted), the crew is left alone.
- Hagos brings drinks — on the house, which means someone's paying and it isn't them.
- The four characters have time to introduce themselves, establish their cover stories, and begin the process of deciding whether to work together.

### Party Opportunities
- Share (or withhold) personal motivations for taking the job
- Assess each other's skills and personalities
- Establish early dynamics — who leads, who listens, who pushes back
- Discuss initial reactions to the job and Paavai's credibility

### Watch For
- **Dawit**: Will he mention Mesfin? His debt? He has every reason to keep quiet about who sent him.
- **Arul**: The face will naturally take the social lead. Watch how much charm he deploys vs. how much he reveals.
- **Sparks**: The Nallur Vaults are his former project. Will he reveal his connection immediately, or hide it?
- **Kavitha**: The ghost watches and evaluates. What does she share about her "foreign scholar" cover? What questions does she ask?

### Tone
Cautious, probing. The conversational equivalent of circling.

---

## Beat 3: The Complication

**Goal**: Introduce immediate pressure and establish that the clock is ticking.

### What Happens
- A messenger arrives at Hagos's shop — not for the crew, but for Hagos. He steps out.
- He returns with news: Mesfin the Quiet wants to see Dawit. Tonight. At Warka Hall.
- Hagos delivers this with his usual cheerful neutrality — "Not my business, friend. But Mesfin doesn't like waiting."

### Party Opportunities
- Dawit must decide: go to Mesfin now (learning what the crime lord wants) or delay (risking Mesfin's displeasure)
- The crew can discuss whether to accompany Dawit, let him go alone, or use the meeting as an intelligence opportunity
- If Dawit goes to Warka Hall: Mesfin has a "small job" — rough up a debtor in the Grain Market who's been skimming. Payment: one week's interest forgiven. The job would raise Heat +1 if done publicly, and it conflicts with keeping a low profile.

### Possible Outcomes
- **Dawit goes alone**: He meets Mesfin, gets the job, faces a moral dilemma. The crew doesn't see this interaction.
- **Crew accompanies**: Mesfin evaluates them. His ward ring may detect Kavitha (DC 14 Investigation vs. Kavitha's CHA-based shapeshifting DC 14 — this could go either way). He's cordial but clearly in control.
- **Dawit refuses/delays**: Mesfin sends a reminder — the debt-mark burns. This can be defused but makes Mesfin wary.

### Tone
Suddenly real. The heist is theoretical; Mesfin's summons is immediate.

---

## Beat 4: Planning Begins (Optional)

**Goal**: If time permits, the crew starts their first concrete planning steps.

### What Happens
- The crew reconvenes (with or without Dawit, depending on Beat 3 choices)
- First planning questions emerge: What do they know about the vaults? What do they need? Where do they start?
- Sparks may begin sharing vault knowledge (carefully — how much does he reveal about his personal connection?)

### Party Opportunities
- Identify information gaps: vault layout, guard schedules, ward systems, the Echo Key
- Discuss approach: stealth vs. social engineering vs. force vs. some combination
- Assign initial reconnaissance tasks based on skills
- Decide where to meet going forward (Hagos's back room is compromised — Mesfin knows they're there)

### Key Early Planning Decisions
- Do they tell each other why they're really doing this? (Personal stakes vs. operational security)
- Do they approach the Unbound for the Echo Key component? (Gains a resource, risks losing control)
- Do they case Kovil Plaza before the Festival? (Useful intel but increases exposure)
- How do they communicate securely? (The echo-wards record everything in public spaces)

### Tone
Constructive energy mixed with lingering distrust. The job is starting to feel possible — and dangerous.

---

## Session End States

### Strong Ending Points
1. **After Paavai's pitch** — the crew has the offer but hasn't committed. Strong cliffhanger.
2. **After character introductions** — the crew has decided to work together (or is close). Natural pause.
3. **After Mesfin's summons** — immediate pressure applied, stakes established. The crew is in motion.
4. **After initial planning** — the crew has a direction and tasks. Ready for reconnaissance in Session 2.

### Open Threads for Session 2
- Mesfin's "small job" — did Dawit accept? What are the consequences?
- First reconnaissance of Kovil Plaza and the vault entrance
- The Echo Key — how to learn about it, where to find the components
- The Festival of Sealed Words — opportunity or trap?
- Arul's next dead drop to Senthil — what does he report?
- Establishing a secure meeting location outside Hagos's shop

---

## GM Notes

### Pacing
- Beat 1 (The Pitch) should take the most time — let Paavai be thorough and let the crew ask questions
- Beat 2 (Introductions) is the heart of the session — character dynamics established here carry through the campaign
- Beat 3 (Complication) should feel abrupt — an interruption to the planning that grounds the story in immediate danger
- Beat 4 is optional overflow — if the session is running long, end after Beat 3

### Combat Considerations
- No combat expected in Session 1. The tension is social and conspiratorial.
- If the crew accompanies Dawit to Warka Hall, a confrontation with Mesfin's enforcers is possible but should be avoidable.
- If violence occurs in Teff Row, Heat increases by 1 and Mesfin's disposition shifts.

### Character Spotlights
- **Dawit**: Mesfin's summons is his moment — the crew sees what he's carrying
- **Arul**: The pitch meeting is his natural stage — watch how he handles the group
- **Sparks**: Paavai's description of the vaults should trigger visible reactions — the GM should note these for other characters to notice
- **Kavitha**: The quietest character in a room full of strangers — what does she observe that others miss?

### Paavai's Pitch Details
She has the following documents in her folio:
- A partial copy of an Ādhāra Protocol design document (theoretical, not the implementation)
- A vault map showing Upper Vault levels 1-3 (her access area)
- A guard rotation schedule (current but subject to change for the Festival)
- Access codes for Level 1 and Level 2 (rotated weekly; she knows the pattern)
- A list of valuable items in the Collateral Chamber that could serve as payment

She does NOT have:
- Access to Deep Vault levels 4-6 or the Foundation Level
- Knowledge of the echo-memory's full capabilities
- Information about the Echo Key (she doesn't know it exists)
- Awareness that one of the crew is a Ledgerkeeper agent
