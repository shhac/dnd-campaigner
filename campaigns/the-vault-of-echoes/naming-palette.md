# Naming Palette: The Vault of Echoes

## Primary: Tamil (Dravidian)

Used for the city of Kanakkur and its institutions — Counting Houses, the Echoguard, the Ledgerkeepers, elite families, civic infrastructure, and characters embedded in the city's power structure.

**Reasoning**: Tamil's polysyllabic richness and classical weight evoke bureaucracy, contract law, and ancient mercantile tradition. The sounds feel monumental — fitting for a city built on binding agreements.

### Examples in Use

| Element | Name | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| City | Kanakkur | "City of ledgers" — from *kanakku* (account) |
| Primary Counting House | Selvan House | Ruling house; head is Thirunavukkarasu Selvan |
| Other Houses | Kovil House, Nallur House, Pandyan House | Major competing houses |
| Elite district | Marai Quarter | Where the Counting Houses keep their estates |
| Civic plaza | Kovil Plaza | Central square, seat of the Echoguard |
| The vaults | Nallur Vaults | Underground contract archive, the heist target |
| Echoguard commander | Murugan Velan | Head of the city's magical law enforcement |
| Ledgerkeeper handler | Senthil Arunachalam | Intelligence operative who runs agents |
| Clerk NPC | Paavai Sundaram | Disgruntled Selvan House employee |
| Bard PC (face) | Arulchelvan Maran | Refined name befitting the city's elite |
| Rogue PC (ghost) | Kavitha Nair | Local-sounding use-name for a Changeling |
| Unbound lieutenant | Deepa Kuzhali | Tamil name signals she came from the establishment |

## Secondary: Amharic (Ethiopian Semitic)

Used for the criminal underworld, debtor classes, street-level characters, and the Unbound resistance. Also applies to older traditions that predate Kanakkur's current power structure.

**Reasoning**: Amharic's distinct phonology creates an audible divide between the legitimate city and its shadow economy. Characters with Amharic names are immediately coded as outsiders to the Counting House system — by choice or by circumstance.

### Examples in Use

| Element | Name | Notes |
|---------|------|-------|
| Underworld market | Teff Row | Black market district |
| Crime lord's base | Warka Hall | Named for the fig tree — deep roots, wide shade |
| Fighter PC (muscle) | Dawit Tessem | Street-level name; everyone calls him "Tessem" |
| Artificer PC (breaker) | Fikru | Goes by "Sparks" — nickname-heavy underworld culture |
| Crime lord | Mesfin the Quiet | Dawit's creditor |
| Inspector | Yonas Bekele | Law enforcement from the debtor class |
| Fence | Hagos | Moves stolen goods through Teff Row |
| Unbound leader | Bereket Zewde | Revolutionary with deep-root Amharic name |

## Cross-Palette Characters

Some characters straddle both worlds. Their names signal which world they *came from*, not necessarily where they stand now.

- **Deepa Kuzhali** (Tamil) is the Unbound's lieutenant — her establishment-origin name hints at her defection from the system
- **Yonas Bekele** (Amharic) is a lawful inspector — his underclass name explains his sympathy for debtors even as he enforces the law
- **Fikru / Sparks** (Amharic) was once a wardsmith for the Counting Houses — cast out, his Amharic name marks where he landed, not where he started

## Guidelines for New Names

When introducing new NPCs during play:

- **Counting House affiliates, city officials, merchants**: Tamil palette
- **Criminals, debtors, street contacts, resistance members**: Amharic palette
- **Characters who've crossed between worlds**: Name from their origin; let the dissonance tell the story
