# The Echoguard

**Type**: Magical Law Enforcement
**Influence**: Local (Kanakkur) — total within the city
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Overview

The Echoguard enforces contract law and maintains civic order in Kanakkur. They patrol the streets, guard the Nallur Vaults, investigate contract breaches, and serve as the visible arm of the Counting Houses' authority. Their signature capability — echo-reading, the ability to "listen" to the ward network and detect breaches, illusions, and unauthorized magic — makes them uniquely effective and uniquely dangerous.

## Goals

**Public Goal**: Protect Kanakkur's citizens and enforce the rule of law.
**True Goal**: Same, mostly. The Echoguard genuinely believes in its mission. The problem is that "the law" is written by the Counting Houses.

## Methods

- Patrol all tiers (with decreasing frequency and attention toward the lower tiers)
- Echo-reading for contract breaches, unauthorized magic, and wanted persons
- Garrison the Nallur Vaults with dedicated security teams
- Investigate crimes against Counting House property and personnel with priority
- Maintain ward-spires and the city's enchantment infrastructure

## Structure

**Leadership**: Commander Murugan Velan — reports to the Counting Houses' Joint Security Committee
**Hierarchy**: Commander > Captains (4, one per tier/district) > Lieutenants > Inspectors > Officers
**Membership**: Approximately 200 active officers. Recruitment from all tiers, though advancement favors upper-tier backgrounds.

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Moderate — funded by the Counting Houses
- **Military**: Significant — 200 trained officers with magical equipment
- **Magic**: Significant — echo-reading, ward-staves, detection enchantments
- **Political**: Moderate — they enforce but don't write the law

## Hooks & Opportunities

- Inspector Yonas Bekele can be turned into an ally if given proof of the Ādhāra Protocol
- The Guard's patrol patterns are predictable — they can be mapped and exploited
- Commander Murugan's Teff Row origins make him potentially sympathetic to the crew's goals, but only if presented with irrefutable evidence
- The Guard's echo-reading has blind spots — older wards, underground areas, and deliberately dampened zones

## GM Notes

The Echoguard is the crew's most persistent antagonist. They're not evil — they're cops doing their jobs in a system that happens to be corrupt. This makes them harder to deal with than a straightforward enemy.

Heat directly translates to Echoguard response:
- Heat 0-1: Random patrols, no awareness of the crew
- Heat 2-3: Directed investigation, descriptions circulating, checkpoints
- Heat 4-5: Full mobilization, ward-net tracking, Commander Murugan personally involved

The Echoguard's echo-reading is the crew's biggest ongoing challenge. It detects: active magic (including Changeling shapeshifting if the reader is skilled enough), contract breaches, ward tampering, and illusions. It does NOT detect: mundane disguises, non-magical stealth, or conversations in ward-blind zones.
