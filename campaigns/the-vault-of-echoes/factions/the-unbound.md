# The Unbound

**Type**: Revolutionary Movement
**Influence**: Local (Kanakkur lower and middle tiers)
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Overview

The Unbound are Kanakkur's revolutionary resistance — a clandestine network dedicated to destroying the contract system that they see as legalized slavery. Born fifty years ago in Teff Row, the movement has grown from a handful of angry debtors into an organized cell structure with operatives across the city's lower and middle tiers. They sabotage ward-spires, forge free-contract documents, and dream of the day the debt-marks stop burning.

## Goals

**Public Goal**: Freedom from the contract system for all citizens of Kanakkur.
**True Goal**: Total destruction of the contract framework — not reform, not replacement, destruction. The system cannot be fixed because it was never broken; it works exactly as designed. It must be unmade.

## Methods

- Cell-based structure to limit exposure from infiltration
- Sabotage of ward-spires and echo-ward infrastructure
- Propaganda distribution (pamphlets, graffiti, coded messages)
- Debt-mark removal services (underground, imperfect, dangerous)
- Intelligence gathering through sympathizers in every tier
- Planning for a decisive strike against the Nallur Vaults

## Structure

**Leadership**: Bereket Zewde — charismatic leader, never seen by the Echoguard
**Hierarchy**: Bereket > Cell Leaders (6-8) > Cell Members (5-10 per cell) > Sympathizers
**Membership**: 50-80 active members, hundreds of sympathizers in the lower tiers

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Poor — funded by member contributions and sympathizer donations
- **Military**: Limited — members are motivated but poorly equipped
- **Magic**: Limited — one Echo Key component, basic ward-disruption alchemicals, some knowledge from Deepa's Counting House background
- **Political**: None — they are hunted criminals

**Symbol**: A broken chain drawn in debt-mark ink — deliberate provocation
**Colors**: None formal. Members sometimes wear copper-colored thread woven into clothing.
**Recognition Signs**: Coded phrases referencing "the old weight" and "the breaking." A specific handclasp — wrist to wrist, covering the debt-mark location.

## Hooks & Opportunities

- The Echo Key component: the crew needs it, and the Unbound will trade it for partnership
- Bereket's demolition plan creates urgency — if the crew doesn't act, the Unbound will, destructively
- Deepa Kuzhali is the reasonable contact — routing negotiations through her produces better outcomes
- The Unbound's cell network provides safe houses, lookouts, and distraction capabilities for the heist
- The compromised cell is a ticking time bomb — if the Ledgerkeepers learn of the heist through this channel, everything accelerates

## GM Notes

The Unbound are the crew's most natural allies — and the most dangerous ones. Their goals overlap with the crew's (break into the vaults, expose the Protocol) but their methods diverge (destroy vs. steal, collateral damage vs. precision).

Bereket's demolition plan is the campaign's ticking clock. The Unbound will act regardless of whether the crew is ready. This creates urgency without railroading — the crew chooses whether to race the Unbound, partner with them, or find a way to slow them down.

The compromised cell is a slow-burn threat. The Ledgerkeepers are receiving intelligence about Unbound operations but not about the crew specifically — yet. If the crew works too closely with the Unbound without operational security, the Ledgerkeeper infiltration becomes a direct threat.

The Unbound's idealism is both their strength and their limitation. They inspire loyalty and sacrifice, but their refusal to compromise means they can't adapt when the situation requires pragmatism. The crew's flexibility is their advantage; the Unbound's conviction is theirs.
