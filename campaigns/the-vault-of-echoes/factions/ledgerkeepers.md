# The Ledgerkeepers

**Type**: Intelligence Apparatus
**Influence**: Local (Kanakkur) — pervasive but hidden
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Overview

The Ledgerkeepers are the Counting Houses' intelligence service — a network of embedded agents, informants, and analysts who ensure the Houses always know more than everyone else. They operate in the shadow of legitimate institutions, gathering information through surveillance, social engineering, and cultivated assets. Most citizens don't know they exist. Those who do know better than to discuss them.

## Goals

**Public Goal**: The Ledgerkeepers have no public presence. Officially, they don't exist.
**True Goal**: Maintain the Counting Houses' information monopoly. Control what is known, by whom, and when. The contract system runs on trust — the Ledgerkeepers ensure that trust is managed.

## Methods

- Embed agents in every major faction and social stratum
- Recruit vulnerable individuals (orphans, debtors, outcasts) and train them as operatives
- Monitor dead drops, coded communications, and ward-network traffic
- Feed intelligence to the Counting Houses' Joint Security Committee
- Occasionally manipulate events to prevent threats from materializing

## Structure

**Leadership**: A committee of senior handlers who report to the Joint Security Committee. No single leader — by design.
**Hierarchy**: Senior Handlers > Handlers > Embedded Agents > Informants > Contacts
**Membership**: Perhaps 40-50 operatives, plus hundreds of informants and contacts who don't know they're serving an intelligence network.

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Moderate — funded through House budgets, disguised as administrative costs
- **Military**: None — they avoid direct action
- **Magic**: Limited — basic encryption and counter-surveillance enchantments
- **Political**: Significant — their intelligence shapes House policy

## Hooks & Opportunities

- Arul's dual loyalty is the primary hook — will he serve the Ledgerkeepers or the crew?
- Senthil's dead-drop schedule can be intercepted or exploited
- The Ledgerkeepers' intelligence files on the Unbound could be valuable to the resistance (or dangerous if released)
- If the crew discovers the Ledgerkeepers' existence, they can be played against the Echoguard — the two organizations don't trust each other

## GM Notes

The Ledgerkeepers are the invisible faction. They don't patrol streets or hold territory — they hold information. Their threat is subtle: they know things, and they use what they know to manipulate events.

Arul's connection to the Ledgerkeepers is the crew's most dangerous internal vulnerability. Every report Arul makes (or fails to make) to Senthil affects what the Ledgerkeepers know about the heist. If Arul stops reporting, Senthil will escalate — first with pressure, then with threats, and finally by burning Arul to the Echoguard.

The Ledgerkeepers do NOT want the Ādhāra Protocol exposed. They benefit from the contract system and would benefit even more from the Protocol's implementation — hereditary debt means hereditary intelligence leverage. If Senthil learns what Paavai found, he'll try to bury it, not report it.
