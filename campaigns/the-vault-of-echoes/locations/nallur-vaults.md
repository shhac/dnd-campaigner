# The Nallur Vaults

**Type**: Underground Vault Complex
**Region**: Beneath Kovil Plaza, Kanakkur
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Overview

The Nallur Vaults are Kanakkur's heart and its deepest secret — a vast underground archive containing every binding contract signed in the city's three-hundred-year history, along with magical collateral, confiscated artifacts, and records the Counting Houses would rather forget. Protected by layered ward systems, Echoguard garrison, and the echo-memory — a semi-sentient magical construct that records and replays everything that happens within. The heist target.

## Atmosphere

**Sights**: Corridors of pale stone lit by ward-light that responds to movement — it brightens where people walk and dims where they don't, creating a constant sense of being followed by illumination. The walls are lined with contract shelves extending into darkness. Everything is immaculate. Dust doesn't settle here — the wards prevent it.

**Sounds**: The echo-hum is loudest here — a deep, resonant vibration felt in the chest more than heard. Footsteps replay faintly from other corridors. Occasionally, a whispered conversation from decades ago surfaces and fades, like a radio briefly catching a distant signal.

**Smells**: Old parchment, copper ink, and ozone. The air is temperature-controlled by the wards — always cool, always dry, always slightly charged. Deeper levels have a mineral smell, like underground rivers.

**Feeling**: Being remembered. The echo-memory is not surveillance — it's memory. The vaults don't watch you; they remember you. The distinction is felt viscerally by anyone with magical sensitivity.

## Key Features

### The Entrance
A single fortified doorway in Kovil Plaza, flanked by Echoguard and sealed with a combination of physical locks and ward-seals. The entrance registers everyone who passes through — name, time, authorization level. There is no legitimate way in without Echoguard clearance.

### The Upper Vaults (Levels 1-3)
Active contract storage. Clerks (including Paavai) access these levels daily. Well-lit, organized, staffed during business hours. Standard ward protection — echo-recording, unauthorized access alerts, anti-theft enchantments on documents.

### The Deep Vaults (Levels 4-6)
Historical archives and high-security storage. Access restricted to Counting House principals and their designated agents. Heavier wards, fewer people, longer corridors. The echo-memory is stronger here — replays are more vivid and more frequent.

### The Foundation Level (Level 7)
Pre-dates the contract system. Ancient stone, ley-line convergences, the infrastructure that makes Kanakkur's magic possible. This is where the Ādhāra Protocol is stored. Access restricted to the Composer and his immediate circle. The echo-memory here is not just strong — it's interactive. It responds to intruders.

## Points of Interest

- **The Registry Room** (Level 1): Where all vault access is logged. Bypassing or falsifying the registry is the first challenge of the heist.
- **The Contract Hall** (Level 2): Main storage for active contracts. Thousands of shelves, organized by House and date. Paavai's workspace.
- **The Collateral Chamber** (Level 4): Where magical items held as contract collateral are stored. Some are valuable; some are dangerous.
- **The Sealed Archive** (Level 5): "Composer's Eyes Only" section. Contains the Ādhāra Protocol documentation — or so Paavai believes.
- **The Ley-Line Nexus** (Level 7): The convergence point that powers the city's ward network. The true Protocol is here, integrated into the ley-line infrastructure.

## Encounters

### Likely Encounters
- **Echoguard patrols**: 2-guard teams on 15-minute rotations (Upper Vaults) to 30-minute rotations (Deep Vaults). Foundation Level has no regular patrol — it's considered inaccessible.
- **Echo-memory replays**: Phantom images and sounds from past events. Usually harmless; disorienting.
- **Ward triggers**: Unauthorized access activates escalating responses: alert chimes, door seals, and eventually paralysis wards.

### Possible Encounters
- **The echo-memory's defense response**: If intruders are detected on Level 5+, the echo-memory manifests phantom guards — echoes of previous defenders that can physically interact with intruders. They're not real, but the damage is.
- **Sparks' past**: The echo-memory contains recordings of Sparks installing the wards. These will replay if triggered, potentially exposing his connection.
- **Other intruders**: If the Unbound are running their own operation simultaneously, the crew may encounter Bereket's people in the vaults.

## Secrets & Hidden Things

- **Sparks' backdoor**: A maintenance access point on Level 4 that bypasses the echo-memory for a 30-second window. Sparks built it; he doesn't know it still exists.
- **The decoy documents**: The Composer has placed false Protocol documents on Level 5 as a trap. Anyone who steals them thinks they've succeeded while the real Protocol remains on Level 7.
- **The pre-foundation tunnels**: Ancient passages beneath Level 7 that connect to the city's sewer system. Unwarded but physically dangerous — collapsed sections, underground water, and creatures that have adapted to the magical environment.
- **The Ley-Line Nexus's vulnerability**: The nexus is both the Protocol's power source and its weakness. Disrupting the nexus would destroy the Protocol — and collapse the city's ward infrastructure entirely.
- **The echo-memory's awareness**: On the Foundation Level, the echo-memory behaves less like a recording and more like an entity. It asks questions. It offers deals. It remembers everything that was ever promised in its presence.

## History

The Nallur Vaults began as a simple contract archive — a secure room where signed agreements were stored. As the contract system grew, so did the vaults, extending deeper into the hill beneath Kovil Plaza. Nallur House designed and maintained the ward systems, with each generation of wardsmith adding new layers.

The Foundation Level was discovered (not built) during a deep expansion sixty years ago. The pre-existing infrastructure — ley-line convergences, ancient stonework, residual enchantments — was adapted into the vault's deepest security layer. Sparks' echo-memory system was the most recent addition, installed fifteen years ago as the capstone of the entire ward structure.

## GM Notes

The Nallur Vaults are the campaign's centerpiece location. Every aspect of the heist revolves around understanding and defeating this environment. Present it in layers:

**Act 1**: The crew learns about the vaults from Paavai's descriptions, Sparks' memories, and reconnaissance. The vaults should feel impossibly well-defended.

**Act 2**: The crew enters and discovers that the reality is both better and worse than they imagined. The echo-memory is stranger and more dangerous than anyone expected. The decoy documents are a genuine threat to the plan.

**Act 3**: The Foundation Level reveals the vaults as something more than a building — the echo-memory as a quasi-entity, the ley-line nexus as the city's magical heart, the Ādhāra Protocol as a cancer growing in the infrastructure.

The vault's defense layers (from easiest to hardest):
1. Physical security (guards, doors, locks) — Dawit and Kavitha's domain
2. Ward systems (echo-recording, access control, alerts) — Sparks' domain
3. Social access (credentials, authorization, bluffing) — Arul's domain
4. The echo-memory (phantom defenders, replay traps, the Foundation Level entity) — everyone's problem
