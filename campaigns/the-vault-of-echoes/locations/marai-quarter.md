# The Marai Quarter

**Type**: District (Elite)
**Region**: Upper tier, Kanakkur
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Overview

The Marai Quarter is where power lives in Kanakkur — a district of elegant estates, private gardens, and Counting House headquarters that sits atop the city like a crown. Here, the contract system is not a burden but a privilege: the air hums with protective wards, the streets are clean and quiet, and the only debt-marks visible are the ornamental kind — patterns of ownership rather than obligation. Access is controlled, movement is monitored, and everything is exactly as the Counting Houses intend.

## Atmosphere

**Sights**: Wide boulevards lined with ward-glass lamps that cast prismatic light. Counting House estates behind wrought-iron fences, their grounds manicured by contracted labor. The ward-spires are visible from everywhere in the Quarter, their amber glow reflected in polished stone. Architecture is deliberate and beautiful — every building is a statement of wealth and permanence.

**Sounds**: Quiet. The echo-wards here absorb ambient noise, creating an artificial hush that contrasts sharply with the rest of the city. Footsteps sound oddly clear. Conversations carry further than expected. Music from salons drifts through evening air.

**Smells**: Clean stone, cultivated flowers, expensive perfumes. The ozone of active wards is strongest here — a persistent charge in the air that makes hair stand on end. Underneath: the subtle chemical signature of contract ink, so pervasive it's become the Quarter's signature scent.

**Feeling**: Curated perfection. The Marai Quarter feels like a stage set — beautiful, controlled, and slightly unreal. Residents move with the practiced ease of people who have never worried about a debt-mark. Visitors feel watched, evaluated, and found wanting.

## Key Features

### The Counting House Estates
Four grand compounds, each housing a Counting House's primary family, administrative offices, and private vaults:
- **Selvan House**: The largest. Burgundy and gold. The Composer's residence and the political center of Kanakkur.
- **Kovil House**: Traditional architecture, deep blue trim. Conservative, respectable, slightly fading.
- **Nallur House**: Ward-glass construction that shifts with the light. The magical heart of the city.
- **Pandyan House**: Newest, most ostentatious. Glass and steel. Hungry for status.

### The Promenade
A public (but heavily monitored) walkway connecting the estates to Kovil Plaza. During the Festival of Sealed Words, it becomes a parade route. The rest of the year, it's where the wealthy see and are seen.

### The Salon Circuit
Private gatherings in Counting House residences where deals are made, alliances formed, and social standing determined. Entry by invitation only. Arul's cover as a performer gives him access to some of these events.

## Points of Interest

- **The Collector's Gallery**: A private art gallery in the Nallur House district that also deals in magical curiosities. One of the three Echo Key components was sold to a collector here.
- **The Sealed Garden**: A public garden maintained by Selvan House as a civic gift. Beautiful, open to all tiers' residents — and saturated with echo-wards that record every conversation within.
- **The Contract Registrar**: The elite contract office where high-value agreements are signed. More ornate than the public offices in Kovil Plaza but functionally identical.

## Encounters

### Likely Encounters
- **Echoguard elite patrols**: 4-guard teams, better armed and more alert than lower-tier patrols. They stop anyone who doesn't look like they belong and check identification.
- **Social gatekeeping**: Servants and functionaries who assess visitors before granting access. Dress, speech, and bearing are evaluated.
- **Contract ink detectors**: Ward-enchanted doorways at estate entrances that detect debt-marks and unauthorized enchantments.

### Possible Encounters
- **Salon invitations**: If Arul has built sufficient social capital, he may receive invitations to private gatherings where intelligence can be gathered.
- **Counting House rivalries**: Overheard arguments, tense negotiations, and subtle power plays between House representatives.
- **The Echo Key collector**: A wealthy Nallur House patron who purchased the component as a curiosity. Retrieving it requires social engineering, theft, or negotiation.

## Secrets & Hidden Things

- The estates' private vaults are connected to the Nallur Vaults via a sealed tunnel system. Only House principals know the access codes. This is a potential infiltration route — but the tunnel wards are keyed to specific individuals.
- Pandyan House is quietly desperate. Their finances are stretched thin, and Selvan House's contract acquisitions have weakened them. A well-placed offer might buy Pandyan cooperation.
- The Sealed Garden's echo-wards extend further than officially documented — they capture conversations on adjacent streets. The Ledgerkeepers use this for surveillance.
- The ward-glass used in Nallur House construction has a flaw: under specific conditions, it resonates with the echo-memory and produces visible replays. This has been happening more frequently.

## History

The Marai Quarter was established when the four Counting Houses formalized their dominance over Kanakkur's economy. Each House claimed a section of the hilltop and built outward. The name comes from the Tamil word for "hidden" or "secret" — an honest name for a place where the city's real decisions are made behind closed doors.

## GM Notes

The Marai Quarter is hostile territory for the crew. Everything is watched, everything is recorded, and everyone who enters is evaluated. The crew can't operate here the way they do in Teff Row. Social engineering (Arul's domain) is the primary tool; stealth and force are dangerous last resorts.

Use the Quarter's aesthetic perfection to highlight the contrast with Teff Row. When the crew visits both in the same session, the juxtaposition should make the injustice visceral: the same city, the same system, radically different experiences.

The Echo Key collector in the Nallur House district provides a heist-within-the-heist: acquiring the component is a smaller-scale operation that tests the crew's teamwork before the main event.
