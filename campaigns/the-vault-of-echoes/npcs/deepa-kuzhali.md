# Deepa Kuzhali

**Role**: Potential Ally / Contact
**Location**: Unbound operations (rotating safe houses)
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Half-Elf |
| **Occupation** | Unbound lieutenant, former Nallur House clerk |
| **Age** | 29 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

A sharp-featured half-elf woman with light brown skin and dark hair cut short and practical. She wears working clothes that could belong to any middle-tier laborer, but her posture and speech betray her Counting House origins. A faded Nallur House guild pin is hidden inside her collar — she can't quite bring herself to throw it away. Her left forearm shows the ghost of a removed debt-mark: the scar tissue is visible, but the magical ink was burned out by an Unbound alchemist. The removal was painful and imperfect.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Precise, impatient, and pragmatic. Where Bereket inspires, Deepa organizes. She's the one who turns revolutionary fervor into operational plans.
**Motivation**: Build something better. Unlike Bereket, Deepa doesn't just want to destroy the system — she wants to replace it with something fair. Her Counting House background means she understands how institutions function, which makes her both the Unbound's greatest asset and its most frustrated member.
**Fear**: That the Unbound will destroy the system and replace it with nothing — or with something equally corrupt.

**Personality Traits**:
- Speaks in logistics: timelines, resources, contingencies. The revolution runs on spreadsheets.
- Distrusts idealism without structure. Bereket's speeches make her nervous.
- Fiercely protective of Unbound operatives — she knows what they've sacrificed.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Crisp, educated, with the clipped efficiency of someone trained in Counting House administration. She catches herself using formal Tamil business phrases and switches to street-Amharic self-consciously. The code-switching reveals her split identity.

**Mannerisms**: Counts on her fingers when listing priorities. Unconsciously straightens things — papers, tools, people's collars. Rubs the scar on her forearm when stressed.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "Bereket gives the speeches. I make sure there are chairs and exits."
- "I worked for Nallur House for six years. I know exactly how the wards function — and exactly who they're designed to hurt."
- "If you want the Unbound's help, you come to the table with a real plan. Not hope. Plans."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- The Echoguard knows the Unbound has a "coordinator" with Counting House training but hasn't identified Deepa
- She appears in the lower tiers as a day laborer — no one connects her to her former position

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- She designed the Unbound's cell structure based on Nallur House security protocols
- She knows the ward schedules for the lower and middle tiers from her House days (somewhat outdated but useful)
- She has contacts still inside Nallur House — former colleagues who are sympathetic but too scared to act
- She's the one who sourced the Echo Key component for the Unbound

### Secrets (GM only)
- She doesn't fully trust Bereket's judgment — his willingness to accept civilian casualties frightens her
- She's been considering reaching out to the crew independently, bypassing Bereket, if his demolition plan moves forward
- The debt-mark removal left a vulnerability: ward-detection magic reads her scar as "anomalous," which could identify her if an Echoguard with strong echo-reading examines her closely

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Professional assessment. She'll evaluate the crew's capabilities with the same analytical eye she once applied to Nallur House operations.

**Current Disposition**: Unaware

**History with Party**: None

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Scout (modified)
**AC**: 14 (leather) | **HP**: 33
**Notable Abilities**:
- Multiattack (2 shortsword attacks)
- Ward knowledge: Can identify ward types and weaknesses with an INT check (DC varies)
- Keen Hearing and Sight: Advantage on Perception checks relying on hearing or sight

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Unbound, the Echo Key, Nallur House intelligence, the crew's alliance negotiations

**Potential Uses**:
- More reasonable negotiation partner than Bereket for Unbound alliance terms
- Can provide ward intelligence and Nallur House contacts
- If Bereket's plan becomes too destructive, Deepa might defect to the crew's approach
- Her Tamil name signals her Counting House origins, creating automatic rapport with Tamil-palette NPCs

## GM Notes

Deepa is the Unbound member most likely to work productively with the crew. She shares their preference for precision over destruction. If the crew needs to negotiate with the Unbound, routing through Deepa (rather than directly to Bereket) produces better results.

Her internal conflict — loyalty to Bereket vs. concern about collateral damage — mirrors the campaign's central question: what are you willing to destroy to build something better?
