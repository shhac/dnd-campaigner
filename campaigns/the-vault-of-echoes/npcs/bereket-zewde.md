# Bereket Zewde

**Role**: Potential Ally / Complication
**Location**: Unbound safe houses (rotating)
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Appearance

A wiry, intense man with dark skin, a close-cropped beard, and deep-set eyes that burn with conviction. His left forearm bears three overlapping debt-marks — two paid, one active — that he displays openly as a badge of survival. He dresses in working-class clothes, deliberately undistinguished, and carries no weapons visibly. His hands are calloused from manual labor (he still works as a mason to maintain cover). A broken chain is tattooed at his wrist in debt-mark ink — the Unbound's symbol.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Charismatic, committed, and dangerously idealistic. Bereket inspires loyalty through genuine belief, not manipulation — which makes him more dangerous than any cynic.
**Motivation**: Destroy the contract system entirely. Not reform it, not replace it — destroy it. Bereket has seen what debt-marks do to families, to communities, to generations. He wants it gone.
**Fear**: That the system will outlast him. That his sacrifice — and everyone's sacrifice — will be for nothing.

**Personality Traits**:
- Speaks about the future with absolute certainty, as if liberation is inevitable and he's just accelerating the timeline
- Treats every debtor he meets as a potential recruit — not aggressively, but with the assumption that everyone who's been marked should be angry
- Can be ruthless about means while remaining sincere about ends

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Passionate, rhythmic, almost sermonic when addressing followers. One-on-one, he's warmer and more practical. Uses Amharic proverbs naturally. Calls everyone "friend" until they prove otherwise.

**Mannerisms**: Traces his debt-marks when making a point. Makes eye contact that's uncomfortably sincere. Clasps both hands around yours when sealing an agreement.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "The chain doesn't care if the link is gold or iron. It still holds."
- "I'm told I'm a radical. As if wanting people free is radical. As if the alternative is reasonable."
- "We have our own plan for the vaults. We would prefer to work together. But we will act regardless."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- The Echoguard has been hunting the Unbound leader "Bereket" for five years — they've never seen his face
- The Unbound operate in cells across Kanakkur's lower and middle tiers
- They've conducted sabotage operations against ward-spires, debt-mark registries, and Counting House property

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- Paavai Sundaram has been feeding the Unbound information about Selvan House through a cutout
- The Unbound have one of the three Echo Key components (acquired from a sympathetic Nallur House ward-crafter)
- Bereket knows the Festival of Sealed Words creates vulnerabilities in the ward network
- He's planning his own operation against the Nallur Vaults — demolition, not stealth

### Secrets (GM only)
- One of his cell leaders has been compromised by the Ledgerkeepers (not Deepa — a lower-ranking operative)
- His demolition plan for the vaults would cause structural damage to Kovil Plaza, potentially collapsing buildings above. He considers this acceptable collateral damage.
- He's aware that Paavai hired an independent crew and is angry about it — he expected to be her primary partner
- He will betray the crew in Act 3 if their goals diverge from total destruction of the contract system

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: When the crew approaches (or the Unbound approach the crew), Bereket will be cautiously receptive — allies against the Counting Houses are welcome. But he'll push to control the operation.

**Current Disposition**: Unaware (of crew) / Frustrated (with Paavai)

**History with Party**: None directly

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Unbound, the Echo Key, Paavai's backup plan, the vault operation

**Potential Uses**:
- Holds one Echo Key component — the crew needs it
- His demolition plan creates time pressure and an alternative approach
- Alliance with the Unbound provides muscle and distraction but surrenders control
- His idealism contrasts with the crew's more pragmatic approach, creating philosophical tension
- In Act 3, his willingness to accept collateral damage forces a choice

## GM Notes

Bereket is the revolution made flesh — inspiring, dangerous, and unable to compromise. He's the crew's potential ally whose goals overlap but don't align. He wants to destroy the system; the crew might want to change it, profit from it, or simply survive it.

His Echo Key component is a bargaining chip. He'll trade it for partnership — a real partnership, where the Unbound have a voice in the heist plan and a claim on the outcome. The crew can negotiate, but Bereket won't be sidelined.

His demolition plan is the ticking clock: if the crew doesn't act soon, the Unbound will act on their own, loudly and destructively. This creates urgency without railroading.
