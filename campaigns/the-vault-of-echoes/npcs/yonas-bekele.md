# Yonas Bekele

**Role**: Potential Ally / Obstacle
**Location**: Lower-tier Echoguard precinct
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Inspector, Echoguard (lower-tier district) |
| **Age** | 38 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

A lean man with deep brown skin and worry lines that belong on someone twenty years older. Wears the Echoguard blue, but his uniform is perpetually rumpled — buttons done wrong, collar slightly open, badge a fraction off-center. His eyes are sharp despite the tired face. He carries his ward-staff like a walking stick rather than a weapon, which makes the Echoguard leadership uncomfortable.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Weary, sardonic, and quietly furious about the system he enforces. Covers it with dark humor and professional competence.
**Motivation**: Do the job, feed the family, don't think too hard about whether the job is just. (He thinks about it constantly.)
**Fear**: That his children will inherit his debts — both financial and moral.

**Personality Traits**:
- Makes cynical jokes about the contract system that walk right up to the line of insubordination
- Notices everything but reports selectively — what the Echoguard doesn't know can't hurt people he'd rather not hurt
- Keeps a flask of cheap spirits in his belt pouch. Doesn't drink on duty. Drinks heavily off it.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Informal, slightly slurred cadence even when sober. Uses street-Amharic idioms that his Echoguard superiors find uncouth. Addresses suspects by first name, which throws them off balance.

**Mannerisms**: Rubs his temples when frustrated (constantly). Leans against walls instead of standing at attention. Chews his lip when he's decided to look the other way.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "Let me explain how this works. You did something. I know you did something. Now we decide whether I write it down or forget I saw it."
- "The law says one thing. My stomach says another. Guess which one keeps me up at night."
- "I'm not a hero, friend. I'm a tired man with a badge. Don't make me use it."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Competent inspector assigned to the lower tiers — the district no one else wants
- Known for closing cases and for being lenient on petty debtor violations
- Not promoted despite his record, which everyone understands without discussing

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- He's aware of Mesfin's operation and maintains an informal non-aggression pact: major crimes get investigated, minor operations get ignored
- He knows several Unbound members by face and has "failed" to arrest them multiple times
- He suspects the Counting Houses use the Echoguard as muscle rather than justice — and resents it
- His wife works as a seamstress under a Pandyan House contract with unfavorable terms

### Secrets (GM only)
- He's passed information to the Unbound twice — nothing operational, just warnings about upcoming raids. He tells himself it was practical (avoiding bloodshed), not political.
- Commander Murugan suspects Yonas's sympathies and has him under soft surveillance
- His debt-mark (modest — a housing contract) was issued by Selvan House. This gives them theoretical leverage over him, though they've never used it.
- If shown proof of the Ādhāra Protocol, he would turn. Not just sympathize — actively turn.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: If the crew draws attention in the lower tiers, Yonas is the inspector who shows up. He'll be professional, observant, and inclined to give benefit of the doubt — once.

**Current Disposition**: Unaware

**History with Party**: None

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: Lower-tier law enforcement, Unbound sympathies, potential crew ally

**Potential Uses**:
- If Heat rises, Yonas might investigate — and might decide to look the other way
- Can provide inside information about Echoguard patrol patterns and operations
- His conversion from reluctant enforcer to active ally is a significant story beat
- His family provides emotional stakes — helping Yonas means protecting his wife and children

## GM Notes

Yonas is the "good cop" in a bad system. He enforces laws he knows are unjust because the alternative is losing his position (and his family's protection). He's the kind of man who could be a hero if the cost weren't so high.

If the crew approaches him carefully — proving they're not just criminals but people trying to fix the system — he becomes invaluable. His Echoguard access, knowledge of patrol patterns, and willingness to "lose" paperwork can reduce Heat and open doors.

The key to turning Yonas is his children. The Ādhāra Protocol would make debt hereditary. His children would inherit his mark. That knowledge will push him past the line he's been standing on.
