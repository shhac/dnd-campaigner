# Paavai Sundaram

**Role**: Quest Giver / Ally
**Location**: Selvan House (day job) / Teff Row (meetings)
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Senior clerk, Selvan House contract archives |
| **Age** | 34 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

A compact woman with dark hair pulled back in a severe bun, wearing the grey-and-gold uniform of a Selvan House clerk. Her hands are stained with contract ink — an occupational mark that never fully washes out. She has sharp, watchful eyes behind wire-framed spectacles, and the particular pallor of someone who spends their days in underground archives. She moves with the controlled precision of someone who is terrified but has decided to act anyway.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Tightly wound, precise, and earnest. She presents information the way she'd present a contract filing — organized, thorough, with supporting documentation.
**Motivation**: Destroy the Ādhāra Protocol and expose Selvan House's plan. She believes in the original contract system — fair, consensual, limited — and sees the Protocol as a perversion of everything Kanakkur was supposed to be.
**Fear**: Being caught before the job is done. Being wrong about what she found. Being right about it.

**Personality Traits**:
- Apologizes before and after giving bad news
- Keeps a mental inventory of every document she's handled — near-eidetic memory for written text
- Fidgets with her spectacles when lying (which she's bad at)

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Formal, clerkish, but with an underlying intensity that breaks through when she talks about what she found. Uses precise terminology — "pursuant to," "as documented in," "subsection" — then catches herself and translates into plain speech.

**Mannerisms**: Adjusts her spectacles constantly. Checks exits when entering a room (newly acquired habit). Her hands tremble slightly when she talks about the Protocol — adrenaline, not weakness.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "I'm sorry — I should start from the beginning. I'm sorry, I've never done anything like this before."
- "The documents I found aren't just irregular. They're an abomination wearing the language of law."
- "I know what I'm asking you to do. I'm asking you to rob the most powerful institution in the city. I'm also asking you to save it."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- She's a Selvan House clerk with eighteen years of service
- She has access to the contract archives in the upper vault levels
- She's considered reliable, thorough, and unremarkable by her superiors

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- The upper vault access codes change weekly — she knows the rotation pattern
- Selvan House has been commissioning unusual ward modifications in the deep vaults
- Several filing categories have been reclassified to "Composer's Eyes Only" in the past year
- She found the Ādhāra Protocol documents while misfiling a routine contract — they were in a section she wasn't supposed to access

### Secrets (GM only)
- She's made copies of key Protocol documents and hidden them outside Selvan House
- She's been feeding information to Bereket Zewde (the Unbound) through a cutout, hoping they'd act first — they didn't move fast enough
- Her access to the upper vaults has already been flagged as "anomalous" by the Ledgerkeepers' monitoring system — she has weeks, not months, before someone investigates
- She has a younger sister working as a servant in Kovil House. If Paavai is exposed, her sister will be punished.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Desperate but organized. She's rehearsed this pitch. She's also clearly out of her depth in Teff Row and trying not to show it.

**Current Disposition**: Hopeful but frightened

**History with Party**: Recruited each member separately through intermediaries

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Commoner (with expertise in Investigation)
**AC**: 10 | **HP**: 4
**Notable Abilities**: None — Paavai is not a combatant. Her value is knowledge and access.

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Nallur Vaults, the Ādhāra Protocol, Selvan House internal politics

**Potential Uses**:
- Primary source of vault intelligence — guard rotations, ward schedules, access codes
- Moral anchor for the heist — she reminds the crew why they're doing this
- Vulnerability — she can be captured, compromised, or killed, raising stakes dramatically
- If the crew needs a way into Selvan House functions, her clerk credentials provide access

## GM Notes

Paavai is the heart of Act 1. She provides the mission briefing, the moral justification, and the practical intelligence the crew needs. She's also the most vulnerable person in the story — no combat skills, no powerful allies, no escape plan beyond "the heist works."

Her sister at Kovil House is a pressure point the Composer can exploit. If Selvan House suspects Paavai, they'll threaten the sister first.

Paavai's connection to the Unbound should surface mid-Act 1 — it complicates the crew's relationship with both factions. The Unbound expected Paavai to work with them exclusively. Learning she hired an independent crew will anger Bereket.
