# Thirunavukkarasu Selvan ("The Composer")

**Role**: Primary Antagonist
**Location**: Selvan House estate, Marai Quarter
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Head of Selvan House, de facto ruler of Kanakkur's economy |
| **Age** | 62 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

A tall, lean man with silver-streaked black hair worn long and tied back. His face is all angles — sharp cheekbones, deep-set dark eyes, a thin mouth that rarely smiles but never frowns. He dresses immaculately in deep burgundy and gold, the Selvan House colors. His hands are his most notable feature: long-fingered, always moving — gesturing, tapping, tracing patterns in the air as if conducting invisible music. His debt-mark ink is tattooed in ornamental patterns across both forearms, visible when his sleeves shift — not debts owed, but debts *held*. A living ledger.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Measured, precise, and terrifyingly calm. Speaks as if every sentence has been composed in advance.
**Motivation**: Complete the Ādhāra Protocol and make the contract system permanent across generations. He genuinely believes this will bring stability and prosperity to Kanakkur.
**Fear**: Disorder. The Composer has seen what happens when contracts fail — the chaos, the violence, the suffering. His solution is worse than the disease, but he can't see that.

**Personality Traits**:
- Refers to the contract system as "the harmony" and to disruptions as "dissonance"
- Treats every interaction as a negotiation, even casual conversation
- Genuinely kind to his household staff — pays well, never raises his voice. The monster is in the system, not the man.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Formal, melodic cadence. Never raises his voice. Uses musical metaphors naturally — "discordant," "in tempo," "resolution." Asks questions he already knows the answers to, giving people a chance to tell the truth before he reveals he knows they're lying.

**Mannerisms**: Conducts with his right hand when thinking — small, precise gestures as if shaping invisible sound. Reads contracts the way a musician reads a score, his lips moving slightly.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "Every great composition requires structure. Remove the structure and you don't have freedom — you have noise."
- "I don't punish disloyalty. I simply ensure that loyalty is the more elegant choice."
- "You think I'm the villain. I understand. But chaos is not the alternative you imagine it to be."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- He's the head of Selvan House, the most powerful Counting House in Kanakkur
- He's held the position for thirty years, longer than any predecessor
- He's credited with modernizing the contract system and expanding Kanakkur's trade reach
- He sponsors the Festival of Sealed Words and is its ceremonial conductor

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- He's been quietly acquiring contracts from other Counting Houses at generous rates
- He's commissioned special ward-work in the Nallur Vaults — beyond normal maintenance
- He personally selects the debt-mark ink formulations used in high-value contracts
- He has informants in every major faction, including the Echoguard

### Secrets (GM only)
- The Ādhāra Protocol: a ritual to bind the contract system to bloodlines, making debt hereditary at the magical level
- He's already tested it on debtor families in Teff Row — their children were born with faint marks
- He knows about the Unbound, the Ledgerkeepers' covert operations, and most of what happens in Teff Row
- He designed the Protocol not from cruelty but from a genuine — if monstrous — belief that permanent order prevents greater suffering

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: The Composer doesn't know the party exists at campaign start. When he learns of the heist attempt, his reaction will be intellectual curiosity before anger — who are these people, and what do they think they've found?

**Current Disposition**: Unaware

**History with Party**: None

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Archmage (modified — contract magic specialist)
**AC**: 15 (ward-woven robes) | **HP**: 99
**Notable Abilities**:
- Contract magic: Can activate any debt-mark within line of sight, causing pain, compulsion, or paralysis
- Echo command: Can trigger the Nallur Vault echo-memory remotely, replaying events
- Ward manipulation: Can reshape existing wards within Selvan House properties
- Always has 2 bodyguards (use Veteran stat block) within earshot

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Ādhāra Protocol, the Nallur Vaults, the entire contract system

**Potential Uses**:
- The final boss — but a negotiation boss, not just a combat boss
- He'll offer deals. They'll be genuine. The terms will be terrible.
- He's the face of the system the crew is trying to break — understanding his perspective makes the moral questions harder

## GM Notes

The Composer is not a cackling villain. He's a man who saw suffering and decided that control was the answer. Every terrible thing he's done follows logically from his premise: that disorder causes more harm than order, even when order requires coercion. He's wrong — but he's wrong in a way that's hard to argue with when you've lived in a city where unpaid debts lead to starvation.

Play him as the reasonable man in the room. His offers will be fair. His logic will be sound. The horror is in the implications, not the delivery.

His contingency plan (Act 3): if the Protocol is stolen, he can activate a partial version using existing debt-marks. This makes him dangerous even after the heist succeeds.
