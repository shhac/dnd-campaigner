# Senthil Arunachalam

**Role**: Hidden Antagonist / Handler
**Location**: Ledgerkeeper offices (concealed within Kovil House administrative building)
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

|---|---|

## Appearance

A nondescript man who could disappear in any crowd — medium height, medium build, grey-streaked brown hair, unremarkable features. His clothes are always appropriate for wherever he is: clerk's attire near the Counting Houses, trader's garb in the markets, laborer's roughcloth near Teff Row. His eyes are the only remarkable thing — warm brown, attentive, and far too intelligent for the forgettable face they're set in.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Avuncular, patient, and manipulative without seeming it. Senthil makes people feel heard, valued, and safe — which is how he recruits and controls his assets.
**Motivation**: Protect the Counting Houses' information monopoly. Knowledge is power; the Ledgerkeepers ensure the Houses always know more than everyone else.
**Fear**: Losing control of an asset. Specifically, losing Arul — his finest operative and his most dangerous liability.

**Personality Traits**:
- Asks about your family, your health, your aspirations — and files every answer for future use
- Never gives a direct order. Everything is a suggestion, a request, a favor between friends.
- Genuinely believes he's helping his agents by giving them purpose and structure. The fact that the purpose serves the Counting Houses is, to him, a happy alignment.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Warm, conversational, unhurried. Uses names frequently. Asks questions that feel like genuine interest and are actually intelligence gathering. Sprinkles in self-deprecating humor that makes him seem harmless.

**Mannerisms**: Drinks tea constantly — always has a cup. Leans forward when listening, creating intimacy. His handshake lingers a half-second longer than comfortable.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "Arul, my boy. You look tired. Sit, have tea. Tell me what you've learned — in your own time."
- "I'm not asking you to betray anyone. I'm asking you to protect the city. Sometimes those things overlap."
- "Information is neither good nor evil. It's what people do with it that matters. We ensure the right people have it."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Outwardly, Senthil is a mid-level administrator in Kovil House's bureaucracy
- He's known as a pleasant, unremarkable man who's been in the same position for decades

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- He runs a network of twelve embedded agents across Kanakkur's factions
- He knows about the unusual ward modifications in the Nallur Vaults
- He suspects a major theft is being planned but doesn't know the target or the crew
- He's been feeding Arul's reports to the Counting Houses' Joint Security Committee

### Secrets (GM only)
- He recruited Arul from an orphanage at age fourteen. He considers this a gift; it was grooming.
- If Arul becomes a liability, Senthil will burn him — extract what intelligence he can and then hand Arul to the Echoguard as a criminal
- He does NOT want the Ādhāra Protocol exposed. The Ledgerkeepers benefit from the contract system and would benefit more from the Protocol's implementation.
- He has a contingency file on every agent: their secrets, their vulnerabilities, their extraction cost. Arul's file is the thickest.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Senthil doesn't know the crew exists as a unit. He knows Arul is embedded in something and is pushing for more detailed reports.

**Current Disposition**: Unaware (of crew) / Proprietary (toward Arul)

**History with Party**: Arul's handler for the past eight months. Recruited Arul as a teenager.

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Spy
**AC**: 12 | **HP**: 27
**Notable Abilities**:
- Sneak Attack (2d6)
- Cunning Action
- Always has an escape route planned — carries a smoke bomb and knows every secret passage in the Kovil House district
- His true weapon is information: he can mobilize the Echoguard with a single message

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: Arul's loyalty crisis, the Ledgerkeepers, Counting House intelligence operations

**Potential Uses**:
- Creates ongoing pressure on Arul to report and maintain cover
- His "fatherly" relationship with Arul makes the betrayal personal, not just professional
- If the crew discovers Arul's handler, Senthil becomes a negotiation target or a threat
- In Act 3, he represents the "reform from within" argument — maybe the system just needs better people running it (he's wrong, but persuasively so)

## GM Notes

Senthil is the face of institutional evil — not a monster, just a man who serves a machine that grinds people. He genuinely cares about Arul the way a handler cares about a prize asset. The care is real; the context makes it monstrous.

His dead drop schedule with Arul: every four days, at rotating locations in the market district. Arul has missed two drops recently (deliberately vague reports). Senthil is getting impatient.

If the crew captures or confronts Senthil, he'll negotiate. He always negotiates. His information is his weapon and his shield — he knows things the crew needs to know, and he'll trade access for survival.
