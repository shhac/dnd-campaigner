# Hagos

**Role**: Neutral / Mercenary Contact
**Location**: Hagos's shop, Teff Row
**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Halfling |
| **Occupation** | Fence, pawnbroker, information trader |
| **Age** | 44 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

A stout halfling with copper-brown skin, a bald head that he keeps polished, and a gold tooth that catches the light when he grins — which is often. His shop is an organized chaos of mismatched goods: confiscated ward-components, second-hand jewelry, unregistered weapons, and a surprising collection of rare books. He wears a leather apron over merchant's clothes and smells faintly of machine oil and pipe tobacco.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Cheerful, mercenary, and completely without illusions about the world he operates in. Hagos treats everything as a transaction and is honest about it, which makes him paradoxically trustworthy.
**Motivation**: Profit and survival. Hagos has no cause, no loyalty beyond self-interest, and no ambitions beyond keeping his shop running and his neck intact.
**Fear**: Mesfin deciding he's no longer useful. The Echoguard deciding he's worth the paperwork.

**Personality Traits**:
- Assigns a price to everything, including abstract concepts. "How much is your dignity worth? Because I can get you a discount."
- Genuinely enjoys the art of the deal — haggling is entertainment, not just business
- Keeps a scrupulously clean shop despite the questionable origins of its merchandise

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Fast, cheerful, peppered with merchant's patter. Uses rhetorical questions as punctuation. Switches between street-Amharic and formal Common depending on who he's selling to.

**Mannerisms**: Polishes things while talking — whatever's nearest gets buffed. Weighs objects in his hand reflexively, estimating value. Grins before delivering bad news, which is disconcerting.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "You want a meeting room? I've got a meeting room. You want privacy? That's a different price."
- "I don't ask questions. You don't ask where things come from. Everyone's happy, yes?"
- "Mesfin sends his regards. No, I'm joking. Mesfin never sends regards. He sends reminders."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Runs a pawnshop in Teff Row that buys and sells without asking questions
- Maintains neutrality between Teff Row's competing operators
- Known for fair prices and reliable discretion (within limits)

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- He reports unusual activity to Mesfin — it's the price of operating in Teff Row
- He knows most of the underworld's active operators and their specialties
- He can source hard-to-find items: ward-components, alchemical supplies, forged papers
- He's heard rumors about "something big" being planned against the Counting Houses

### Secrets (GM only)
- He'll sell the crew out if the price is right — but his reputation depends on discretion, so the price would have to be very high
- He's been skimming from Mesfin's cut for years. Small amounts, carefully hidden. Enough to fund an emergency exit from Kanakkur if things go bad.
- He has a contact in Nallur House who supplies him with "surplus" ward-components — some of which are actually functional echo-ward bypasses

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Business as usual. Four strangers need a room; he has a room. The clerk who arranged it paid in advance.

**Current Disposition**: Neutral (transactional)

**History with Party**: Providing the meeting space for Paavai's recruitment

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Spy (modified — halfling)
**AC**: 13 (leather) | **HP**: 22
**Notable Abilities**:
- Halfling Luck
- Brave (advantage on saves vs frightened)
- Naturally Stealthy
- Carries a concealed hand crossbow under the counter

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: Teff Row underworld, equipment acquisition, information flow

**Potential Uses**:
- Source for specialized heist equipment (ward-components, disguises, tools)
- Information broker — knows what's happening in Teff Row and can find out about other districts
- Mesfin pipeline — anything told to Hagos reaches Mesfin eventually
- His Nallur House contact could provide ward-bypass components (for a significant price)

## GM Notes

Hagos is a utility NPC — useful, colorful, and reliably self-interested. He adds flavor to Teff Row scenes and provides a transactional interface for the crew's shopping and information needs. Don't make him too helpful or too obstructive; he's a merchant, and merchants serve whoever's buying.

His reporting to Mesfin is a known quantity — the crew should eventually realize that anything they discuss in Hagos's shop reaches Mesfin. This creates a useful information management challenge.
