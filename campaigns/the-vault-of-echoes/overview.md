# The Vault of Echoes

## Setting

**World**: Homebrew — a single city and its underworld
**Era/Time Period**: Late mercantile era, magic intertwined with contract law
**Starting Location**: Kanakkur, the City of Binding Contracts

## Tone & Themes

**Tone**: Tense, stylish, morally gray — a caper film played at the table
**Core Themes**:
- Debt as bondage — who owns your future when your past is collateral
- The machinery of "legitimate" power vs. the freedom of illegitimacy
- Trust among thieves — the crew is the only contract that matters
- What you're willing to burn to get free

## The Hook

Everyone in Kanakkur owes something to someone. The Counting Houses — ancient mercantile dynasties that fused magic with contract law — hold the city in a web of binding agreements. Break a contract and the magic enforces the penalty: debt-marks that burn on your skin, compulsions that drag you back to your creditors, echo-wards that track you through the city's streets.

Four people, each trapped by a different kind of debt, discover that the most powerful Counting House — Selvan House — is hiding something in the Nallur Vaults beneath Kovil Plaza. Something that could shatter the contract system entirely. A disgruntled clerk named Paavai Sundaram has the proof and the access codes, but she needs a crew willing to break into the most heavily warded vault in the city.

The job is impossible. The reward is freedom. And every faction in Kanakkur — the Counting Houses, the Echoguard, the Ledgerkeepers, the criminal underworld, and the revolutionary Unbound — has their own reason to want what's inside.

## Major Factions

### The Counting Houses
- **Type**: Mercantile oligarchy with magical enforcement
- **Goals**: Maintain the contract system that makes them wealthy and powerful
- **Relationship to Party**: Antagonist (Selvan House) / varied (others)

### The Echoguard
- **Type**: Magical law enforcement
- **Goals**: Enforce contract law, maintain civic order
- **Relationship to Party**: Antagonist — they protect the vaults

### The Ledgerkeepers
- **Type**: Intelligence apparatus
- **Goals**: Protect the Counting Houses' interests through surveillance and covert operations
- **Relationship to Party**: Hidden antagonist — one PC is secretly working for them

### The Underbelly (Criminal Network)
- **Type**: Organized crime syndicate
- **Goals**: Profit, territory, leverage over the legitimate economy
- **Relationship to Party**: Complicated — Mesfin the Quiet holds Dawit's debt

### The Unbound
- **Type**: Revolutionary movement
- **Goals**: Destroy the contract system and free all debtors
- **Relationship to Party**: Potential ally with their own agenda

## Key Locations

### Kanakkur
A tiered city built on ancient foundations. The upper tiers hold the Counting Houses, the Marai Quarter's elegant estates, and Kovil Plaza where the Echoguard keeps watch. The lower tiers descend into markets, tenements, and eventually Teff Row — the underworld's beating heart. Beneath it all, the Nallur Vaults hold centuries of binding contracts, magical collateral, and secrets the Houses would kill to protect.

### The Nallur Vaults
The target. A vast underground archive beneath Kovil Plaza, warded with echo-magic that records and replays everything that happens within. Every footstep, every whispered word, every spell cast — the vaults remember. Breaking in means defeating not just locks and guards, but the building's memory itself.

### Teff Row
The underworld market district. Stolen goods, black-market enchantments, forged identities, and information — all available for the right price. Mesfin the Quiet runs the district from Warka Hall, and nothing moves through Teff Row without his knowledge.

## The Threat

**Main Antagonist/Threat**: Thirunavukkarasu Selvan ("The Composer"), head of Selvan House
**Their Goal**: Completing the Ādhāra Protocol — a ritual that would make the contract system permanent and inescapable, binding not just individuals but bloodlines across generations
**Timeline**: Weeks — the ritual requires a specific celestial alignment
**What Happens If They Succeed**: Kanakkur's debt system becomes eternal. No one born into a debtor family can ever escape. The Counting Houses rule forever.

## Campaign Arc (GM Eyes Only)

### Act 1: The Crew Assembles
The party meets through Paavai's recruitment, discovers the scope of the job, and begins planning. Each PC's personal stakes become clear. The crew must build trust while hiding their secrets from each other. Gathering intelligence, recruiting allies, acquiring tools.

### Act 2: The Heist
Infiltration of the Nallur Vaults. Multiple approaches, multiple things going wrong, improvisation required. The crew discovers that what's hidden in the vaults is bigger than any of them expected — not just evidence of corruption, but the Ādhāra Protocol itself.

### Act 3: The Reckoning
The aftermath. Every faction reacts. Betrayals surface. The crew must decide what to do with what they've stolen — and who they really are when the job is done. The Composer makes his move. The city burns or is reborn.

## Notable Items

### The Echo Key
- **Type**: Magical artifact (warding bypass)
- **Location**: Must be assembled from components held by different factions
- **Significance**: The only way to enter the Nallur Vaults without triggering the echo-wards

### Debt-Mark Ink
- **Type**: Alchemical substance
- **Location**: Produced exclusively by the Counting Houses
- **Significance**: Used to inscribe binding contracts on skin. Removal requires either the creditor's consent or destruction of the original contract.

## House Rules

- **Heat Mechanic**: Party Heat tracks how much attention they've drawn from the Echoguard and Counting Houses (0-5 scale). Heat increases from failed stealth, public incidents, and faction conflicts. High Heat means more patrols, surveillance, and complications.
- **Contract Magic**: Binding agreements carry magical weight. Breaking a contract triggers automatic penalties unless the original document is destroyed or the creditor releases the debt.
- **Downtime Between Heist Phases**: Between major operations, the crew can spend downtime gathering intel, acquiring gear, building relationships, or reducing Heat.

## Session Zero Notes

- **Content boundaries**: No torture scenes. Debt and coercion are themes but should not be gratuitous. The tone is Ocean's Eleven, not Saw.
- **Player expectations**: Planning and social encounters are as important as combat. Creative problem-solving is rewarded. There is no single correct approach to the heist.
- **Character connections**: The crew is assembled by Paavai Sundaram. They don't know each other well at first — trust is earned through the job, not assumed.
