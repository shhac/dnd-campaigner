# Campaign Preferences

**Campaign**: The Vault of Echoes

## Narrative Style

**narrative_style**: hybrid

Uses clear speaker labels with flowing prose. Dialogue is attributed but woven into descriptive narrative rather than bare script format. Descriptions maintain cinematic quality while keeping conversation easy to follow.

## Tone Notes

- Heist-film tension: plans going wrong is more interesting than plans going right
- Moral grayness — no one is purely good or evil, including the party
- Style matters — the crew should look competent even when improvising
- The city is a character: Kanakkur's contracts, echoes, and wards are always present
- Humor lives in the cracks between tension, not in slapstick
