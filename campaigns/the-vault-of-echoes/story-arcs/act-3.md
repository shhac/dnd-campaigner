# Act 3: The Reckoning

## Status
LOCKED

## Key Secrets
- The Kanakkur Compact, if destroyed, wouldn't just free debtors — it would collapse the magical infrastructure of the entire city. Wards, protections, civic enchantments, even the water purification system — all are anchored to the contract framework. Freedom has a devastating cost.
- The Composer has a contingency: if the Protocol is stolen, he can activate a partial version using the debt-marks already inscribed on the city's population. Less powerful, but enough to demonstrate the concept and force the other Houses to join him.
- Kavitha's foreign handlers have sent extraction orders — they want the Protocol (or knowledge of it) brought out of Kanakkur. Kavitha must choose between the mission and the crew.
- Bereket Zewde's Unbound have been infiltrated by a Ledgerkeeper agent (not Arul — someone else). Their next move will be anticipated.
- The echo-memory of the vaults, once disturbed, begins "leaking" throughout the city — past events replaying in public spaces, revealing secrets that powerful people have buried. This affects everyone, not just the Counting Houses.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **The Composer**: Desperate but calculating. He'll offer the crew a deal — return the Protocol and he'll release their debts. It's a genuine offer. He needs the Protocol more than he needs revenge. But he'll kill them if they refuse.
- **Bereket Zewde**: If the contract system can be destroyed, the Unbound don't care about collateral damage. Bereket will push for total destruction of the Compact, regardless of consequences.
- **Senthil Arunachalam**: The Ledgerkeepers want the Protocol preserved but transferred to their control. They approach Arul with a final offer: deliver the Protocol and earn a full pardon plus a permanent position.
- **Mesfin the Quiet**: Sees the chaos as an opportunity to become the new power in Kanakkur. He's been positioning assets throughout Acts 1-2. He'll back whichever side is winning.
- **Yonas Bekele**: Forced to choose between law and justice. If the crew can convince him, he becomes a powerful inside ally. If not, he'll do his duty.

## Planned Events
- The city fractures as echoes leak: old deals exposed, buried crimes replayed in the streets, the illusion of the "fair contract system" shattered
- The Composer activates his contingency — debt-marks across the city flare as he demonstrates the Ādhāra Protocol's power
- Faction war: Counting Houses turn on each other, Unbound launch open rebellion, Echoguard splits between loyalists and those horrified by what they've been protecting
- The crew must decide: destroy the Compact (freedom + chaos), preserve it (stability + oppression), or find a third option (rewrite the system)
- Personal reckoning for each PC: Dawit confronts Mesfin, Arul chooses crew or handlers, Sparks faces what they built, Kavitha decides who they really are
- The final confrontation with The Composer in the heart of the Nallur Vaults

## Foreshadowing
- Contract ink on old documents bleeds through their pages — the magic is destabilizing
- The Echoguard report "impossible" ward readings — echoes appearing where no echoes were recorded
- Old debts surface: merchants discover contracts they settled years ago are somehow active again
