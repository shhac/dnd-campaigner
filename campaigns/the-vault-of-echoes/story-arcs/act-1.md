# Act 1: The Planning Phase

## Status
UNLOCKED

## Key Secrets
- Paavai Sundaram is genuine but desperate — she discovered the Ādhāra Protocol by accident while filing routine contracts and is terrified of what Selvan House will do if they learn she knows
- The Ādhāra Protocol is a ritual that would bind the contract system to bloodlines permanently — children born to debtors would inherit the debt-marks, making escape across generations impossible
- Thirunavukkarasu Selvan ("The Composer") has been quietly buying contracts from the other Counting Houses, consolidating power. The other Houses don't yet realize the pattern.
- The Echo Key — the only tool that can bypass the Nallur Vault wards without triggering the echo-memory — was broken into three components decades ago by the wardsmith who built the vault's final layer. That wardsmith was Fikru (Sparks). He doesn't know two of the three pieces still exist.
- Senthil Arunachalam (Arul's Ledgerkeeper handler) suspects a major theft is being planned but doesn't know the target. He positioned Arul in the underworld to gather intelligence. He does NOT want the Ādhāra Protocol exposed — the Ledgerkeepers benefit from the contract system.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Paavai Sundaram**: Wants the Protocol destroyed. She's a true believer in Kanakkur's original contract system (fair, consensual, limited) and sees the Ādhāra Protocol as a perversion. She will sacrifice herself if necessary but would prefer to survive.
- **Mesfin the Quiet**: Knows something big is happening with Selvan House. Wants to position himself to profit from the chaos. He sent Dawit to the meeting deliberately — if the heist succeeds, he benefits. If it fails, Dawit takes the fall and Mesfin's debt is "resolved."
- **Hagos (the fence)**: Neutral profiteer. He's providing the meeting space and will fence any secondary goods from the heist. He'll sell the crew out if the price is right, but his reputation depends on discretion.
- **Murugan Velan (Echoguard commander)**: A loyalist who genuinely believes in the contract system. Not corrupt — worse, he's principled. He'll pursue the crew with righteous conviction.
- **Yonas Bekele (Inspector)**: A debtor-class inspector who enforces laws he knows are unjust. Secretly sympathetic to the Unbound but has a family to feed. Could be turned into an ally — or could be the one who catches them.

## Planned Events
- **The Pitch**: Paavai lays out what she knows about the Nallur Vaults and the Ādhāra Protocol. She reveals her partial access and what she needs: a crew with specific skills.
- **Mesfin's Squeeze**: Within 1-2 sessions, Mesfin demands Dawit do a "small job" — rough up a debtor who's late on payments. This creates a moral dilemma and a timing conflict with heist planning.
- **Reconnaissance Phase**: The crew needs to scout the Nallur Vaults, the Echoguard patrol patterns, and the ward layout. Multiple approaches possible.
- **Echo Key Hunt**: Learning the Key exists, locating its components, acquiring them. One piece is in Sparks' old workshop (now sealed by the Echoguard). One is held by the Unbound. One was sold to a collector in the Marai Quarter.
- **Festival of Sealed Words**: A major civic holiday (in 2 days) that creates both opportunity (crowds, distraction, reduced vault traffic) and risk (increased Echoguard patrols, city-wide ward activation for the celebrations).
- **Senthil Makes Contact**: Arul's handler pushes for a report. The Ledgerkeepers are getting nervous about underworld activity. Arul must decide what to share — and what to withhold.
