## Act 2: The Heist
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- Party has agreed to the job and completed reconnaissance of the Nallur Vaults
- Party has acquired at least two of three Echo Key components
- Heat is at 2 or below (or the party has made a deliberate choice to go loud)
- Minimum sessions: 3
**Foreshadowing hints**: The echo-wards hum louder at night. Selvan House servants have been seen carrying sealed boxes into the vaults at unusual hours.
**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-2.md

## Act 3: The Reckoning
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- Party has entered (or attempted to enter) the Nallur Vaults
- Party has discovered the Ādhāra Protocol's true nature
- At least one major betrayal or secret has been revealed
**Foreshadowing hints**: The Composer has been absent from public functions. Debt-marks across the city flare simultaneously, just for a moment — then fade. The old wards in the city foundations pulse with a rhythm that wasn't there before.
**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-3.md
