# Act 2: The Heist

## Status
LOCKED

## Key Secrets
- The Nallur Vaults have a living echo — not just a recording system but a semi-sentient magical construct that "remembers" everything within. It can replay past events and will actively resist intruders by manifesting echoes of previous vault defenders.
- The Ādhāra Protocol is further along than Paavai realized. The Composer has already tested it on a small population of debtors in Teff Row — their children were born with faint debt-marks. No one connected the dots because debtor families don't get attention.
- The vault contains not just the Protocol but the original Kanakkur Compact — the founding agreement that established the contract system. Destroying it would unmake all contracts in the city simultaneously. Stealing it gives leverage over every faction.
- Sparks built a backdoor into the ward system — a maintenance access he never told anyone about. It still exists, but using it will alert anyone attuned to the ward network that something is wrong.
- One of the crew's allies (determined by Act 1 events) has been compromised. The Ledgerkeepers know the heist is coming but not the exact timing.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **The Composer**: Has prepared for a breach attempt. He's planted false documents in the outer vaults as a trap — anyone who steals them thinks they've succeeded while the real Protocol is deeper within.
- **Murugan Velan**: Will personally lead the Echoguard response if the wards are tripped. He's stationed additional forces during the Festival.
- **Bereket Zewde (Unbound leader)**: Has their own team ready to move on the vaults. If the crew doesn't act soon, the Unbound will — and their approach is demolition, not stealth.
- **Mesfin the Quiet**: Has positioned enforcers near the vault district. If the crew succeeds, he plans to intercept them and take a "finder's fee" from whatever they extract.

## Planned Events
- The heist itself — multiple entry points, ward layers, guard rotations, and the echo-memory to contend with
- The Festival of Sealed Words provides crowd cover but also ward-strengthening ceremonies
- Mid-heist complication: the Unbound launch their own operation simultaneously, creating chaos
- The vault's echo-memory activates, replaying past events — including Sparks' original ward installation, forcing a confrontation with his past
- Discovery of the Ādhāra Protocol's true scope and the evidence of its testing on Teff Row families
- Extraction under pressure — the crew must choose what to take and what to leave

## Foreshadowing
- The air in the lower city tastes like old paper and copper — the wards are charged for the Festival
- The echo-memory in the vaults sometimes leaks: passersby near Kovil Plaza hear faint whispers of decades-old conversations
- Echoguard patrols have doubled but their pattern has gaps — deliberate? A trap? Or just bureaucratic incompetence?
