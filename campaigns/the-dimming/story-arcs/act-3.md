# Act 3: The Choice

## Status
LOCKED — GM reads this file only when unlocked

## Key Secrets
- The nanomachine network can be reconfigured, not just preserved or destroyed — a third path exists
- The original builders of the network left safeguards that the Eth'koru can access, but using them requires understanding and cooperation between species
- Hessa's research, while dangerous, has also produced genuine insights that could help if redirected
- The "Dimming" of ancient myth was a previous partial shutdown — the Vel'ashi survived but lost much of what they were

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Hessa Tolvren-Nakamura**: Reaches a crisis point. If confronted with proof that the network sustains life, she faces a genuine moral dilemma — her desire to protect humans vs. the reality that her actions would kill millions
- **Keth'vorah leadership**: Some are willing to sacrifice the human settlements to protect the network. Others see this as the moment to finally bridge the species divide.
- **The Eth'koru itself**: As a diagnostic tool, it has been guiding the party toward the information they need to make an informed choice — it does not have an agenda beyond completing its function

## Planned Events
- The party must stop the researchers, but force isn't the only option
- Climax forces a choice about Keth'toh's future: preserve comfortable ignorance, embrace dangerous knowledge, or find a third path
- Consequences ripple outward depending on the party's approach — who they allied with, who they alienated, what they learned

## Foreshadowing
- The Eth'koru begins showing visions of the past — the world before, the world during the last Dimming, the world after
- Ancient carvings depict figures from two different peoples working together over something that glows
