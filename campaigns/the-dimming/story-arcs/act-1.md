# Act 1: The Compass Points

## Status
UNLOCKED

## Key Secrets
- Nethara'kora was attacked by human mercenaries hired by Hessa Tolvren-Nakamura's team to recover the Eth'koru; they used a trained glintcat to track and wound her
- The Eth'koru awakened weeks ago when the Second Anchor researchers ran their first full-scale scan of the nanomachine network
- The artifact is itself a piece of the original system — a diagnostic and navigation tool
- "The Dimming" is what the ancient Vel'ashi called a network shutdown. It's in their oldest myths, but no one alive knows what it really means.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Constable Koresh'rath**: Will investigate the death professionally, but has quiet concerns about increasing human activity in the region. Not corrupt, but may be pressured by local interests to downplay the incident.
- **Morvethri (Veth)**: Owner of the Layered Rest. Witnessed the death and wants trouble out of her establishment. Will cooperate with the constable but won't stick her neck out for strangers.
- **Keth'vorah envoy** (not yet arrived): The Keth'vorah know more than they'll initially share. Their envoy will test the party before offering real information about the energy fluctuations.

## Planned Events
- Constable Koresh'rath arrives to investigate the death — first opportunity for the party to be questioned about what happened
- The Keth'vorah send an envoy within days once they learn of Nethara'kora's fate
- If the party delays too long, the mercenaries who attacked Nethara'kora may come looking for the artifact
- The Eth'koru leads the party through ancient sites, revealing glimpses of what the Vel'ashi once were

## Foreshadowing
- The bioluminescent growths in Dustmeet flicker slightly when the Eth'koru pulses — most people wouldn't notice
- Old-timers mention that "the light used to be steadier" in the deep caverns
- Travelers from the west report odd silences in the forests near the abandoned human settlements
