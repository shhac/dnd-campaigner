## Act 2: The Nature of Things
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- Party has followed the Eth'koru to at least one ancient site and discovered the energy source is artificial
- Party has made contact with the Keth'vorah envoy and learned about the energy fluctuations
- Minimum sessions: 3
**Foreshadowing hints**: Strange vibrations in places where the "magic" runs strongest. Vel'ashi who spend time near ancient sites report vivid dreams of geometric patterns.
**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-2.md

## Act 3: The Choice
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:

- Act 2 is complete or nearly complete
**Foreshadowing hints**: The Eth'koru grows warmer, its pulse quickening as if counting down. Ancient Vel'ashi carvings show a world divided by a single decision.
