# Act 2: The Nature of Things

## Status
LOCKED — GM reads this file only when unlocked

## Key Secrets
- Keth'toh's "magic" comes from an ancient nanomachine network that has been maintaining the planet for millennia
- Humans at Second Anchor have discovered the network and are trying to shut it down, believing it to be dangerous alien technology
- The nanomachines maintain the Vel'ashi's shape-shifting abilities, the bioluminescent ecosystem, and countless other systems
- Disabling the network would cause ecological collapse

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Keth'vorah factions**: Internal disagreement on how to respond. Some want aggressive action against the human researchers. Others counsel patience and diplomacy. A secretive faction may know more about the nanomachines than they admit.
- **Hessa Tolvren-Nakamura**: Not evil — genuinely afraid of what she doesn't understand. Believes the nanomachine network is a threat to human colonists. Her fear is reasonable from her perspective, but her solution is catastrophic.
- **Research team members**: Not all aligned with Hessa. Some have doubts. One or two may be sympathetic to the party if approached carefully.

## Planned Events
- The party discovers the true nature of Keth'toh's "magic" through the ancient sites the Eth'koru leads them to
- Growing tensions between Vel'ashi and humans as the researchers' activities cause noticeable environmental effects
- The party must navigate Keth'vorah internal politics — choosing allies and making enemies
- First direct encounter with the research team or their agents

## Foreshadowing
- Shape-shifting becomes slightly harder or unpredictable in areas near the researchers' scanning equipment
- Animals and plants behave strangely near ancient sites — drawn toward them, or fleeing from them
