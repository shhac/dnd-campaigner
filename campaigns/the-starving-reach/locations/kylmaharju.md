# Kylmaharju

**Type**: Frontier Outpost / Settlement
**Region**: Southern edge of the Nalkamaa
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Overview

A frontier outpost of roughly 200 souls — trappers, traders, soldiers, and the stubborn. Built where the northern road ends and the wilderness begins, Kylmaharju exists because someone has to live at the edge of the map. Low stone buildings with sod roofs hunker against the wind. Smoke from peat fires hangs in the cold air. The settlement feels temporary despite having stood for sixty years.

## Atmosphere

**Sights**: Grey stone buildings, frost-covered timber palisade, a single muddy street running north-south. Caribou hides stretched on drying racks. A Crown banner above the Reeve's Hall, faded and fraying. The dark line of the boreal forest beginning a hundred yards north.
**Sounds**: Wind. Always wind. The creak of timber, the distant howl of frost wolves at dusk, the rhythmic thud of someone splitting firewood. Lately: unease in the voices at the trading post. Arguments that end too quickly.
**Smells**: Peat smoke, cured leather, pine resin, animal fat. The sharp clean cold of the subarctic morning.
**Feeling**: Precarious. A settlement clinging to existence not out of hope but out of habit. The recent unease — the dreams, the silent owls, the lost expedition — has turned habitual stubbornness into something closer to dread.

## Key Features

### The Reeve's Hall
A two-story stone building that serves as administrative center, courtroom, and emergency shelter. Reeve Harkka runs the settlement from a desk cluttered with Crown paperwork and hunter reports. The upper floor has rooms for official visitors — including the party.

### The Trading Post (Kettukauppa)
The settlement's commercial heart. A long timber building where trappers sell furs, traders stock supplies, and everyone shares news. Run by a taciturn half-elf named Yrjo who charges fair prices and asks no questions. The best source of local gossip and wilderness intelligence.

### The Palisade
A timber and earth wall surrounding the settlement's core. Not military-grade — meant to discourage wolves and bears, not armies. The north gate faces the Nalkamaa and is barred at night. Guards on the wall have reported hearing the hum on still nights.

## Points of Interest

- **The Shrine of the Road**: A small stone cairn at the north gate where travelers leave tokens for safe return. The tokens have been piling up since Seppanen's expedition departed. No one collects their token until they come back.
- **The Kennels**: Military sled dogs, maintained for patrol use. The dogs have been restless — barking at nothing, refusing to face north. The kennelmaster blames the hum.
- **Old Pentti's Cabin**: At the settlement's northern edge, closest to the forest. Pentti claims he heard the hum once before, fifty years ago. It stopped after a few days. This time it hasn't stopped.

## Encounters

### Likely Encounters
- **Nervous settlers**: People asking about the expedition, the hum, the dreams. Looking for reassurance the party can't honestly provide.
- **Provisioning challenges**: Winter supplies are finite. The trading post has what the party needs but prices are climbing.

### Possible Encounters
- **Old Pentti's warning**: If the party talks to Pentti, he'll share his previous experience with the hum and his belief that "the old bones are waking up."
- **Restless dogs**: The sled dogs react aggressively to anyone who's been near Mtskhuri sites. Foreshadowing for Drift detection.

## Secrets & Hidden Things

- Reeve Harkka received fragmentary reports from northern hunters about abandoned camps — equipment neatly arranged, no bodies, no tracks leading away. She was ordered by the Crown not to share this with the follow-up party.
- Beneath the Reeve's Hall is a root cellar that contains a crate of Crown correspondence. Some letters reference "previous incidents" at Mtskhuri sites in other regions.
- The Shrine of the Road's oldest token — a carved bone disc at the cairn's base — bears Mtskhuri script. No one in the settlement can read it.

## History

Founded sixty years ago as a waystation for Crown surveys of the northern frontier. Originally a seasonal camp, it became permanent when the fur trade proved profitable enough to justify year-round habitation. The settlement has survived harsh winters, wolf attacks, and Crown neglect, but the current unease is something new. The old-timers say the land feels different. The young people are having dreams they can't explain.

## GM Notes

Kylmaharju is the party's last touch of civilization. Everything they don't buy or learn here, they'll have to find in the wilderness. The settlement should feel like a place people are holding together through willpower — the hum, the dreams, and the lost expedition have shaken confidence.

The party's departure should feel consequential. When they walk through the north gate, they're leaving the last safe place behind. The Shrine of the Road's growing pile of uncollected tokens makes this visual.
