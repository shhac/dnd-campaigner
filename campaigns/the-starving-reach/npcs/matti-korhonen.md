# Sergeant Matti Korhonen

**Role**: Ally / Survivor Leader
**Location**: Northern Nalkamaa, with Seppanen's surviving group
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Sergeant, Crown's Northern Expeditionary Force |
| **Age** | 35 |
| **Status** | Alive — exhausted, at Drift 2, holding the survivors together |

## Appearance

Built like a wall — short, broad, thick-necked. His face is a map of frontier service: broken nose, frostbite scars on both ears, a white scar across his chin from a bar fight he won't discuss. His uniform is in better condition than it should be — he maintains it as a matter of discipline, even when everything else is falling apart. His eyes are tired but watchful.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Blunt, practical, professionally angry
**Motivation**: Get his soldiers home alive. He doesn't care about the ruins, the mission, or the Crown's objectives.
**Fear**: That he'll lose another soldier before help arrives. Every day in the wilderness is another day something can go wrong.

**Personality Traits**:
- Expresses concern through profanity-laced competence: "Get that wound wrapped, you stupid bastard, before I have to carry you."
- Maintains routines (watch schedules, equipment checks, meal times) with religious dedication — routine is what separates soldiers from refugees
- Trusts people who do their jobs and distrusts everyone else

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Barking, clipped, northern frontier accent thick enough to spread on bread. Doesn't soften his language for rank or company. Swears constantly but precisely — each profanity serves a grammatical function.

**Mannerisms**: Counts heads. Always counting heads. Chews on a piece of dried leather when stressed. Stands when others sit.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "Fourteen. I've got fourteen breathing soldiers and enough rations for five days if we stretch. You want to play explorer? Do it after I've fed my people."
- "The commander's a good man. He's also been hearing things for a week. Make of that what you will."
- "You want my trust? Get us south. That's the only currency I'm accepting."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Career soldier, fifteen years of frontier service
- Known as reliable, blunt, and unbreakable under pressure

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- The expedition fell apart faster than the official version suggests. Arguments started on the third night, not the eighth. The hum got to some people faster than others.
- Commander Seppanen made the right call retreating, but he hesitated too long — some soldiers who might have followed were already too deep in the Chorus's influence by the time the order came
- He's been managing Drift symptoms among the survivors without knowing what Drift is — he calls it "the fading" and treats it with cold water, slaps, and forced activity

### Secrets (GM only)
- He's at Drift 2 himself. The dreams are constant. He hasn't told anyone because the survivors need him steady.
- He mercy-killed a soldier who reached Drift 4 — the man was speaking in a voice that wasn't his own. Matti considers this the worst thing he's ever done and the most necessary.
- He suspects Ilmari and Esko were spies but didn't confront them because he needed every able body. Now that they've separated from the group, he considers them enemies.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Suspicious but desperate. Four people is not a rescue force. But four people with supplies and the ability to navigate are better than nothing.
**Current Disposition**: Pragmatic — will cooperate as long as the party's goals align with getting his soldiers south
**History with Party**: None

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Veteran
**AC**: 15 (chain shirt + shield) | **HP**: 45 (currently ~35 from exhaustion)
**Notable Abilities**:
- Multiattack (2 longsword attacks)
- Tough as nails — advantage on CON saves against exhaustion
- Rally (1/short rest): As a bonus action, allies within 30 ft gain temp HP equal to Matti's level + CON mod (8)

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: Seppanen's survivors, expedition history, Drift consequences
**Potential Uses**:
- Provides ground-truth account of the expedition's collapse from a NCO's perspective
- His soldiers add numbers to the party but also add mouths to feed and people to protect
- His mercy-kill of a Drifted soldier foreshadows what happens at Drift 4-5
- He can identify Ilmari and Esko's cover identities if shown their real faces
- His pragmatic loyalty can be directed — he'll follow whoever gets his people out

## GM Notes

Matti is the human cost of the expedition made personal. He's not a named commander or a specialist — he's the NCO who kept people alive by being too stubborn to let them die. His fourteen survivors are his entire world right now.

His mercy-kill of the Drifted soldier is a powerful revelation that should be held for the right moment. It establishes that Drift 4+ is genuinely terrifying and that the party may face impossible choices about their own people.

He should feel like someone Toivo immediately respects — a fellow soldier doing the job.
