# Nino Kartvelishvili

**Role**: Ally / Alternative Voice within the Wardens
**Location**: K'ldekari, the Warden Fortress
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Half-elf (human/wood elf) |
| **Occupation** | Mkhartveli Ranger / Scholar |
| **Age** | 42 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

Lean, weathered, with the angular features of her elven heritage softened by human warmth. Dark hair braided tight against her skull, threaded with small bone beads. Her skin is olive-toned, wind-chapped, and marked with geometric tattoos on her forearms — Mkhartveli tradition, each mark representing a sealed site she's sworn to protect. She moves with the quiet economy of someone who spends most of her life in wild places.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Intense, questioning, impatient with dogma
**Motivation**: Find a better way. Sulkhan's plan — sacrifice to renew the binding — is a temporary fix that has been temporary for millennia. She wants to understand the Mtskhuri mechanism well enough to repair it permanently.
**Fear**: That Sulkhan is right and there is no better way. That the only answer is an endless chain of people feeding themselves to a machine.

**Personality Traits**:
- Challenges authority with evidence, not attitude — she's not a rebel, she's a researcher who happens to disagree with the consensus
- Keeps detailed journals in a mix of Common and Kartvelian, filled with diagrams and theories
- Forms quick attachments to people she respects; this makes her passionate but vulnerable

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Quick, engaged, with the Kartvelian accent of the Wardens but lighter — she's spent more time among humans than most of the order. She argues the way scholars argue: with citations and hypotheticals. When excited, she talks with her hands.

**Mannerisms**: Sketches in the air when explaining spatial concepts. Taps her braids when thinking. Crouches rather than sits.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "Sulkhan says the binding requires sacrifice. Sulkhan has been saying that for three hundred years. What if he's wrong? Has anyone checked?"
- "I've been in the archives. The records are fragmentary, but look — here and here — the original Mtskhuri didn't think sacrifice was the only option. They chose it because they ran out of time."
- "I don't want to die for a seal. I want to fix the lock."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Within the Mkhartveli, known as the youngest full Warden and the most vocal advocate for new approaches
- Skilled ranger who patrols the widest territory of any current Warden

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- She's been studying the forbidden archives at K'ldekari — texts Sulkhan considers too dangerous for general access
- Her research suggests the Sinatl'e was designed with a repair mechanism, not just a renewal cycle
- She's found partial schematics for a device at Dzmar-Etseri that might allow the containment to be reinforced without sacrifice

### Secrets (GM only)
- She communicated with the Mtskhuri consciousness within a standing stone. Briefly, fragmentarily, but enough to confirm they're aware and can share information. She hasn't told Sulkhan because he would forbid further contact.
- Her plan has a flaw she hasn't solved: the repair mechanism at Dzmar-Etseri requires someone to interface directly with the Sinatl'e — not permanent dissolution, but temporary merging. The risk of permanent absorption is significant.
- She's developing feelings for the idea of the party before she's met them — outsiders who aren't bound by the Wardens' traditions might see solutions the order can't.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Eagerly curious, immediately friendly. She sees the party as potential allies in her argument against Sulkhan's fatalism.
**Current Disposition**: Sympathetic — especially toward Vieno (a fellow researcher) and Tamar (whom she considers a friend despite their disagreements)
**History with Party**: None directly, but she trained alongside Tamar.

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Collapse option (Act 3), Mtskhuri lore, internal Warden politics, the repair mechanism
**Potential Uses**:
- Provides an alternative perspective to Sulkhan's fatalism
- Her research opens the third path (repair/communion) that the party might not discover otherwise
- Her relationship with Tamar creates internal drama within the Warden faction
- She can serve as guide to Dzmar-Etseri if the party earns her trust
- Her forbidden research reveals that the Mtskhuri are still conscious — a critical lore revelation

## GM Notes

Nino is hope in a story full of grim pragmatism. She represents the possibility that there's a better answer than sacrifice or destruction. Whether she's right is the central question of Act 3.

Her dynamic with Sulkhan is respectful disagreement — she loves the old man but thinks he's trapped by tradition and guilt. Their arguments should feel like a family disagreement, not a factional split.

She should bond quickly with Vieno over shared scholarly passion. This friendship makes Nino's potential risk in Act 3 (interfacing with the Sinatl'e) more personal for the party.
