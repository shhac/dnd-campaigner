# Sulkhan Mtskhureli

**Role**: Quest Giver / Moral Authority / Potential Sacrifice
**Location**: K'ldekari, the Warden Fortress
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Firbolg |
| **Occupation** | Mkhartveli-Deda (Elder of the Warden Order) |
| **Age** | 387 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

Ancient even by Firbolg standards. Nearly eight feet tall but slightly stooped, as if bowing under the weight of centuries. His skin is bark-brown, deeply lined, with patches of grey lichen that grows naturally on very old Firbolgs. His eyes are amber, clear, and terrifyingly alert. He wears layered robes of undyed wool and caribou hide, belted with a cord of braided Mtskhuri metal that has not corroded in four centuries. His hands are massive, gentle, and trembling.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Patient, absolute, sorrowful
**Motivation**: Maintain the containment at any cost. The seals must hold. He has spent 387 years preparing for the possibility that they won't, and his contingency plan is himself.
**Fear**: That the Mkhartveli will die before the containment fails, leaving no one to renew it. That his sacrifice will be wasted because there's no one left to maintain what he buys.

**Personality Traits**:
- Speaks as if every word has been considered for decades (some have)
- Treats younger beings with a kindness that borders on grief — he's watching children play in a burning house
- Will not compromise on the containment. Will compromise on everything else.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Slow, deep, resonant. Heavy Kartvelian accent. Uses archaic turns of phrase. His Common is grammatically perfect but sounds translated — because it is. He thinks in Kartvelian.

**Mannerisms**: Places both hands flat on surfaces when speaking, as if grounding himself. Closes his eyes when listening carefully. Hums a single low note when troubled — it harmonizes with the subsonic hum of the ruin.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "You carry good intentions into a place that eats intention. I have buried many well-meaning visitors."
- "Tamar was sent to watch you. I will not apologize for this. We protect what must be protected."
- "I have lived long enough. If my death purchases another millennium, that is more than fair."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- The Mkhartveli know him as their leader, the oldest living Warden, the one who remembers the most

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- The full history of the Mtskhuri civilization as the Wardens understand it
- The location and status of every sealed site in the Nalkamaa
- Warden rituals for suppressing Drift and warding against the Chorus
- The containment is failing. The hum is proof. He has perhaps months, not years.

### Secrets (GM only)
- He plans to sacrifice himself to renew the binding. He's been preparing the ritual for decades. He has not told anyone except the ancestors' shrine.
- The Wardens' guilt: his order's founders refused to join the Mtskhuri binding. They chose to survive and guard the seals instead of dissolving into the Sinatl'e. Sulkhan considers this the original sin of the Mkhartveli and believes his sacrifice will atone for it.
- He knows that the Mtskhuri consciousness still exists within the Sinatl'e — they are not dead but dissolved. The standing stones sometimes whisper in Mtskhuruli. He has heard them.
- He suspects the Chorus can be reasoned with but considers this too dangerous to attempt. The risk of failure is extinction.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Guarded respect. They survived the Nalkamaa to reach K'ldekari. That earns them an audience. Whether it earns them trust depends on what they want.
**Current Disposition**: Wary — especially toward Vieno (scholars exploit) and Toivo (the Crown takes)
**History with Party**: He sent Tamar to shadow them.

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: Mkhartveli faction, Mtskhuri history, containment system, Act 3 sacrifice option
**Potential Uses**:
- Primary source of lore about the Mtskhuri and the Chorus
- His sacrifice offer is one of three Act 3 endings
- His relationship with Tamar is a lever — she respects him but may disagree with his fatalism
- His Warden wards are mechanically useful for reducing Drift
- His revelation about the Mkhartveli's guilt adds moral complexity to the Wardens

## GM Notes

Sulkhan should feel like a figure of immense gravity. He has been carrying this burden for nearly four centuries. He's tired, he's resolved, and he's made peace with his death. The party may find this noble or horrifying, and both reactions are correct.

His dynamic with Tamar is key. She is the future of the order — if there is one. Sulkhan has been preparing her for leadership but hasn't told her about his sacrifice plan. When she learns, it will feel like a second betrayal (the first being his order to deceive the party).

Sulkhan should be genuinely helpful with information and rituals. He's not withholding out of malice but out of the same pragmatism that has kept the Wardens alive for centuries: share only what is needed, when it is needed.
