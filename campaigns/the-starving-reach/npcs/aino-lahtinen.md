# Aino Lahtinen

**Role**: Key Informant / Potential Ally
**Location**: Northern Nalkamaa, surviving alone between Kylmaharju and Tsikhe-Dzveli
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Cartographer (Crown's Cartographic Survey) |
| **Age** | 29 |
| **Status** | Alive — barely. Malnourished, paranoid, carrying the Humming Stone. |

## Appearance

Gaunt and weathered beyond her years. Dark hair hacked short with a knife for practicality. Fingers stained with charcoal and ink — she hasn't stopped mapping, even while running. Her clothes are layers of salvaged expedition gear, patched and repatched. She moves like prey: quick, low, always checking behind her. Her eyes are sharp but sunken. She carries a leather map case like a lifeline.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Wary, brittle, competent beneath the trauma
**Motivation**: Get the Humming Stone to someone who understands what it is. Survive long enough to warn people.
**Fear**: Being found by whatever followed her from the ruin. Being forced to go back.

**Personality Traits**:
- Maps everything compulsively — terrain, camps, ruin layouts. It's how she processes fear: if she can map it, she can understand it.
- Trusts actions over words. She'll watch what the party does before deciding whether to reveal what she knows.
- Dark, sudden humor that catches people off guard — a survival mechanism from weeks alone.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Rapid, clipped, prone to tangents when stressed. She starts sentences in the middle of thoughts, assuming the listener has been following her internal monologue. When she calms down, she's precise and articulate — the training shows through.

**Mannerisms**: Draws in the dirt while talking. Touches the map case repeatedly. Flinches at subsonic vibrations.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "You're from the Crown. How many did they send? Four. They sent four. That's — that's not a rescue party. That's a scouting party. They don't want us back, they want data."
- "I can show you where the camp was. I can show you where they went in. I will not go back inside."
- "The stone hums louder when you face north. I've been walking south. Draw your own conclusions."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Skilled cartographer attached to Seppanen's expedition
- Known in the Cartographic Survey as meticulous and stubborn about accuracy

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- The expedition split on the eighth night. She was with the group that went deeper initially but turned back when the architecture stopped making sense — rooms connecting to places they shouldn't, corridors that curved upward but felt level
- She broke the Humming Stone from the central pillar in a moment of panicked vandalism — she was trying to break line-of-sight from something she could feel watching her
- Commander Seppanen ordered the retreat but was visibly struggling. She thinks he was hearing things before anyone else.
- Two "soldiers" in the expedition — Ilmari and Esko — had separate communications equipment and were mapping different things than the rest of the survey team

### Secrets (GM only)
- She is at Drift 1 and doesn't know the concept exists. She attributes her unease and broken sleep to normal trauma.
- The Humming Stone is partially protecting her — its resonance creates a small zone of disruption that the Chorus has difficulty penetrating. She doesn't know this consciously but has noticed she sleeps better holding it.
- Her maps of Tsikhe-Dzveli's interior are the most accurate record in existence. They show structural impossibilities that suggest the ruin reconfigures itself.
- She saw Eero Pellervo among those who descended deeper. Eero was lucid but insistent — said the voices "made sense" if you listened long enough. She hasn't shared this because she doesn't know Ruska is in the party.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Deep suspicion. The Crown sent four people. That's a sacrifice, not a rescue. She'll observe from a distance before approaching, and may need to be found rather than met.
**Current Disposition**: Hostile (defensive, not aggressive) — will warm to Cautious if the party demonstrates competence and genuine concern for the expedition survivors
**History with Party**: None

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Humming Stone, expedition history, Tsikhe-Dzveli interior maps, Eero Pellervo's fate
**Potential Uses**:
- Provides the most detailed account of what happened inside the ruin
- Her maps are essential for navigating Tsikhe-Dzveli
- The Humming Stone she carries is a Sinatl'e node with enormous significance
- She saw Eero — this information will devastate and motivate Ruska
- Can identify Ilmari and Esko as Crown's Shadow if she encounters them again

## GM Notes

Aino is the party's first real witness to the horror of the expedition's collapse. She should be sympathetic but difficult — traumatized, suspicious, and not immediately helpful. Earning her trust should feel like an accomplishment.

Her maps are a genuine treasure. Tsikhe-Dzveli's interior shifts, but Aino mapped patterns in the shifting — not a static layout but a catalog of configurations. This is invaluable and she knows it.

The Humming Stone reveal should be significant. When she produces it, anyone near Mtskhuri-related knowledge (Vieno, Tamar) should recognize its importance. It's a piece of the containment network, and carrying it is both protective and dangerous.

Her information about Eero should be delivered carefully. If Ruska is present, Aino's casual mention that she "saw the big one's sibling go deeper" becomes the emotional pivot of Act 1.
