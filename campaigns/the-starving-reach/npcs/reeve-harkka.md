# Reeve Harkka

**Role**: Quest Giver / Authority Figure
**Location**: Kylmaharju, the Reeve's Hall
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Reeve (Crown-appointed administrator) of Kylmaharju |
| **Age** | 52 |
| **Status** | Alive |

## Appearance

A broad-shouldered woman with iron-grey hair pulled back in a practical braid. Her face is weathered from decades on the frontier — deep lines around the eyes, wind-burned cheeks. She wears a fur-trimmed official coat over practical frontier clothing, the Crown's seal pinned at her collar. Her hands are calloused; she was a trapper before she was an administrator.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Measured, direct, carrying a weight she won't name
**Motivation**: Protect Kylmaharju and its people. Fulfill her obligations to the Crown without sacrificing the settlement.
**Fear**: That the Crown sent the expedition knowing what would happen — and that she helped.

**Personality Traits**:
- Makes decisions quickly and doesn't revisit them publicly, even when she privately doubts. A frontier reeve who second-guesses herself out loud doesn't stay reeve for long.
- Watches people more than she talks to them. Reads competence in how someone handles their gear, not in what they say about themselves.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Clipped northern frontier dialect. Drops unnecessary words. Formal when representing the Crown, blunt when speaking as herself. Her voice is low and steady, the kind that carries across a hall without raising.

**Mannerisms**: Stands with her hands clasped behind her back when delivering official information. Drums her fingers on the table when thinking. Looks people in the eyes longer than is comfortable.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "Seppanen took forty. You are four. I won't pretend the math is encouraging."
- "The Crown says find them. I say bring yourselves back. Both would be ideal."
- "There are things I've been told not to share. I'm telling you that so you know the silence isn't mine."

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- Commander Seppanen departed six weeks ago with forty soldiers heading north toward a Mtskhuri ruin site
- The expedition's planned route and supply cache locations (she provides maps)
- Kylmaharju settlers have reported strange dreams and the subsonic hum is faintly audible even in town
- The whisper owls have gone silent in the northern forest

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- The Crown's briefing to her contained more urgency than the official story suggests — she was told this was a "priority recovery" with language she associates with military operations, not search-and-rescue (Persuasion DC 14)
- Two members of Seppanen's expedition — Ilmari and Esko — reported separately to someone other than Seppanen. She noticed but wasn't told why. (Persuasion DC 16)
- She sent a rider south three weeks ago requesting reinforcements. No response has come. (Persuasion DC 12)

### Secrets (GM only)
- The Crown's briefing included a sealed addendum she was ordered not to open — "for the expedition commander's eyes only." She gave it to Seppanen. She doesn't know what it said, but its existence means the Crown knew more than it told her.
- She suspects the expedition was not purely a rescue/survey mission but has no proof. The two separate reporters (Ilmari and Esko) confirmed her suspicion.
- She's considered evacuating Kylmaharju south but has no authority to do so without Crown approval, and the rider hasn't returned.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Evaluating. She's seen confident people head north before. She wants to believe in these four but has learned what optimism costs on the frontier.
**Current Disposition**: Cautiously Hopeful
**History with Party**: Briefed them on the mission in the Reeve's Hall

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Veteran (reduced — she's older and administrative now)
**AC**: 12 (leather under coat) | **HP**: 38
**Notable Abilities**:
- Proficient in Insight (+5) and Survival (+4)
- Former trapper — competent in the wilderness but hasn't been in the field in years

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: The Crown's hidden agenda, Seppanen's expedition, Kylmaharju's safety
**Potential Uses**:
- Provides the party's mission briefing, maps, and supply information
- Can reveal that the Crown is withholding information (with persuasion)
- Her knowledge of Ilmari and Esko's separate reporting chain is an early clue about Crown's Shadow
- If the party returns to Kylmaharju, she's their primary point of contact and resupply

## GM Notes

Harkka is honest within the constraints the Crown has placed on her. She genuinely wants the expedition found and her settlement protected. She is not an antagonist — she's a competent administrator trapped between duty to the Crown and duty to her people.

Her line "There are things I've been told not to share" is the most honest thing she can say. If players push her, she'll reveal what she's observed (the separate reporters, the sealed addendum, the unanswered rider) but not what she's been directly ordered to withhold — because she doesn't actually know those secrets. The Crown told her very little.

She should feel like a grounded, trustworthy person operating within a system that doesn't trust her back.
