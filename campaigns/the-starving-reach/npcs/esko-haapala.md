# Esko Haapala

**Role**: Secondary Antagonist / Potential Turncoat
**Location**: Northern Nalkamaa, operating with Ilmari near Tsikhe-Dzveli
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Quick Reference

| | |
|---|---|
| **Race** | Human |
| **Occupation** | Crown's Shadow operative (demolitions/engineering) |
| **Age** | 31 |
| **Status** | Alive — increasingly frightened, Drift 2 |

## Appearance

Stocky, strong-handed, with the calluses of someone who works with explosives and tools. Ruddy complexion, reddish-brown hair cropped close. His face is open and expressive — a liability for a spy, which is why he handles materials rather than people. Sweat on his brow even in the cold. His hands shake when he's idle, so he keeps them busy.

## Personality

**Demeanor**: Nervous, competent under pressure, increasingly desperate
**Motivation**: Survive. Complete the mission if possible, but survive first. He signed up for demolitions work, not psychic warfare.
**Fear**: The Chorus. He can hear it. He can't tell Ilmari how bad it's getting.

**Personality Traits**:
- Talks too much when scared — fills silence with technical details about explosives, engineering, anything tangible
- Genuinely good at his job; his hands are steady when they're working, even when the rest of him is falling apart
- Loyal to Ilmari out of habit and dependency, not conviction. That loyalty has a breaking point.

## Voice & Mannerisms

**Speech Pattern**: Fast, technical, with the northern frontier accent he hasn't been able to train away. Uses jargon as a comfort blanket. When truly frightened, his speech fragments.

**Mannerisms**: Fidgets with tools and components. Taps surfaces rhythmically. Checks and rechecks his demolitions kit.

**Sample Dialogue**:
- "The charges are set. Standard configuration, redundant fuses, thirty-second delay. That's the part I'm good at. The part where we're running from a screaming hole in the ground, that's — that's less in my training."
- "Ilmari says it's manageable. Ilmari says a lot of things."
- "I just want to go home. Is that — can we just go home?"

## What They Know

### Public Knowledge
- None — posed as "Private Nurmi," a combat engineer in Seppanen's expedition

### Private Knowledge (requires trust/persuasion)
- He and Ilmari are Crown's Shadow. He'll crack before Ilmari does if pressured.
- He planted charges at Tsikhe-Dzveli's entrance as a contingency — the ruin can be collapsed from outside
- He's heard the Chorus. Not just the hum — actual words. It calls him by his real name.

### Secrets (GM only)
- He is at Drift 2 and climbing. The Chorus has been speaking to him specifically, offering to "quiet the fear" if he opens certain sealed doors in the ruin. He hasn't complied but he's thought about it.
- He knows the charges at the entrance are powerful enough to seal the ruin permanently — but anyone inside would be trapped. Ilmari considers this acceptable. Esko does not.
- He has a secondary set of charges that Ilmari doesn't know about — a personal insurance policy. If everything goes wrong, he can blow the entrance on his own.

## Relationship to Party

**First Impression**: Will follow Ilmari's lead in presenting as a fellow survivor. Less convincing than Ilmari — perceptive party members may notice his discomfort.
**Current Disposition**: Frightened, potentially sympathetic. If separated from Ilmari, he can be reasoned with.
**History with Party**: None

## Combat Stats (if needed)

**Use Stat Block**: Spy (modified for demolitions)
**AC**: 13 (leather) | **HP**: 27
**Notable Abilities**:
- Expertise in thieves' tools and demolitions equipment
- Can set, disarm, and detect explosive charges
- Sneak Attack (1d6, only with advantage or ally adjacent)
- Proficient in Stealth (+4), Sleight of Hand (+4), Investigation (+3)

## Plot Relevance

**Connected To**: Crown's Shadow, ruin collapse contingency, potential party ally
**Potential Uses**:
- His demolitions expertise is useful if the party needs to open or close passages in the ruin
- He's the weak link in Crown's Shadow — turning him provides intel on Ilmari's plans
- His charges at the entrance create a ticking clock if Ilmari decides to seal the ruin
- His Drift progression is a visible warning of what exposure does to a person
- His secondary charges give the party an option they wouldn't otherwise have

## GM Notes

Esko is designed to be sympathizable and turnable. He's not a bad person — he's a frightened specialist in over his head. If the party offers him genuine safety and a way home, he'll break from Ilmari.

His demolitions skills become critical in Act 3 regardless of which ending the party pursues: Renewal may require sealing passages. Collapse requires his expertise. Even Communion benefits from controlling access to the deep ruin.

The Chorus targeting Esko specifically creates dramatic tension — he's carrying enough explosives to reshape the dungeon, and the entity trying to influence him knows it.
