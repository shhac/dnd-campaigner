# Session 1: Beat Sheet

**Opening State**: The party has been assembled and briefed in Kylmaharju's Reeve's Hall. It is early morning, late autumn, first hard frost. They depart today.

---

## Beat 1: The Departure

**Goal**: Establish the party, introduce the world's tone, create the emotional weight of leaving civilization behind

### What Happens
- Final briefing with Reeve Harkka — she provides maps, supply manifests, and Seppanen's planned route
- Opportunity to visit the trading post (Kettukauppa) for last-minute supplies
- The walk through Kylmaharju to the north gate — settlers watching, some hopeful, some pitying
- The Shrine of the Road at the north gate: the pile of tokens left by Seppanen's soldiers, uncollected
- The gate opens. The boreal forest begins.

### Party Opportunities
- Talk to Reeve Harkka for additional details (she'll share what she can, withhold what the Crown told her to)
- Visit the trading post — Yrjo can sell cold weather supplies, basic equipment
- Talk to Old Pentti if they find him (he'll be at the north gate, watching)
- Examine the Shrine of the Road — forty tokens, one for each soldier
- Establish party dynamics: who walks point, who covers the rear, what's the march order?

### Tone
Somber but purposeful. The party has a job to do. The settlement is counting on them. The weight of forty missing people hangs over everything.

---

## Beat 2: The First Day

**Goal**: Establish wilderness survival rhythms, begin party relationship building, introduce the Nalkamaa's beauty

### What Happens
- Travel through boreal forest. Dense pine and birch, game trails, frost on everything
- Navigation is straightforward (Survival DC 10) but the terrain is demanding
- Opportunities for hunting/foraging to supplement rations (Survival DC 12)
- The party makes camp for the first night

### Party Opportunities
- Character introductions during travel — who talks, who stays quiet, who checks the map?
- Tamar's wilderness expertise is immediately evident — she reads the forest like a book
- Vieno notices standing stones at a distance (Mtskhuri markers) — her curiosity sparks
- Ruska recognizes landmarks from her previous, disastrous trip
- Setting up camp: fire, shelter, watch rotation. Toivo's element.

### Environmental Details
- Whisper owls calling at dusk — normal, reassuring
- Frost wolf tracks crossing the trail — fresh, large pack
- The cold is sharp but manageable with gear
- Stars visible through the canopy; no aurora tonight

### Tone
Cautious optimism. The wilderness is beautiful and the party is competent. Let the first day feel achievable. The horror comes later; the first day earns it.

---

## Beat 3: The Night Watch

**Goal**: First character-building conversations, introduce subtle wrongness, establish the night routine

### What Happens
- Camp is set. Fire lit. Rations shared.
- Watch rotation begins — pairs are natural for conversation
- First night in the Nalkamaa. Dreams are normal (no Drift exposure yet).
- One atmospheric detail: the campfire flames lean slightly north, toward the deep Nalkamaa. Barely noticeable. If someone points it out, there's no explanation.

### Party Opportunities
- **Watch conversations**: These are the campaign's first real character moments. Who pairs with whom? What do they talk about?
  - Toivo + anyone: He'll ask about their experience, assess capabilities, keep it professional
  - Vieno + anyone: She'll talk about the Mtskhuri, ask questions, try to learn their stories
  - Tamar + anyone: She'll point out environmental details, share (limited) knowledge, deflect personal questions
  - Ruska + anyone: She'll be guarded but practical. If someone mentions the expedition, she might let something slip about her previous trip
- **Skill checks**: Perception during watch (DC 12 to notice the fire leaning north). Nature check (DC 15) to know that campfires don't lean in still air.
- **Ruska's pendant**: If anyone notices the bone pendant, Ruska will deflect. Pressing earns a flat "It's my sibling's. They're with the expedition." End of discussion.

### Tone
Quiet, intimate, the fragile warmth of a fire in the cold. This is where the party becomes people to each other instead of job titles.

---

## Beat 4: Day Two — The First Sign (Optional)

**Goal**: Introduce the first tangible evidence that something is wrong

### What Happens
Only if the session has time and energy for it:
- Morning of Day 2. The party breaks camp and continues north.
- Mid-morning: they find one of Seppanen's planned supply caches. It's been opened.
- The cache was supposed to be sealed with a military lock. The lock is intact. The supplies are rearranged — some taken, some left, in a pattern that doesn't match foraging or theft.
- Investigation (DC 13): boot prints from Crown-issue boots, heading north. Two sets of tracks that are lighter, more careful — not military stride. These are Ilmari and Esko's tracks, though the party can't know that yet.

### Evidence
- Standard military rations partially consumed
- A notebook page torn out — the remaining pages show charcoal rubbings of Mtskhuri inscriptions (the expedition's scholar was here)
- A carved wooden token from the Shrine of the Road, placed carefully on top of the cache. Someone came back for their token and then continued north instead of going home.

### Tone
Unease. The evidence doesn't fit a clean narrative. The supply cache isn't ransacked — it's curated. Someone came here with a purpose that doesn't match "desperate survivor" or "careless thief."

---

## Session End States

### Strong Ending Points
1. **After the departure** (Beat 1) — short session, strong opening image
2. **After the first night** (Beat 3) — party bonds established, ready for the wilderness
3. **After the supply cache** (Beat 4) — first mystery planted, urgency to continue

### Open Threads for Session 2
- What happened to the supply cache? Who rearranged it, and why?
- The campfire leaning north — what's pulling it?
- The two non-military tracks (Ilmari and Esko, though the party doesn't know)
- The returned shrine token — someone from the expedition came back but chose to go north again
- Ruska's sibling connection to the expedition (planted but not yet explored)
- Tamar's knowledge of the standing stones (noticed by Vieno, not yet interrogated)

---

## GM Notes

### Pacing
- Session 1 is about establishing the world, the party, and the tone. No combat. No horror. The horror earns its impact by having something to contrast against.
- Let travel feel like travel. The Nalkamaa is eight days deep. The party should feel the distance.
- Character conversations during the march and at camp are the session's most important content. Prompt them, give them space, don't rush to the next plot point.

### Dice Rolling Opportunities
- **Navigation** (Survival DC 10-12): Tamar or Ruska
- **Foraging** (Survival DC 12): Tamar, Ruska, or anyone with the skill
- **Perception during watch** (DC 12): Whoever is on watch
- **Nature check for fire anomaly** (DC 15): Anyone who notices
- **Investigation of supply cache** (DC 13): Anyone examining it
- **Tracking** (Survival DC 14): To follow the two non-military tracks from the cache

### Character Spotlights
- **Toivo**: The march order, camp setup, watch rotation — this is his domain. Let him lead.
- **Vieno**: The standing stones are her first taste of the Mtskhuri in person. She'll want to investigate. Let her be excited.
- **Tamar**: She knows more than she's saying about the standing stones. Watch for moments where knowledge leaks through her cover. Vieno will notice.
- **Ruska**: The Nalkamaa is personal for her. Watch for reactions to landmarks, to the forest, to the feeling of going north again. The pendant is a visual anchor.

### The Campfire
The fire leaning north is the session's single supernatural detail. It should be easy to miss, easy to dismiss, and deeply unsettling if noticed. Don't explain it. Let it sit. The party will learn what it means later.
