## Active Quests

### Find the Lost Expedition
- **Objective**: Locate Commander Kaleva Seppanen and his forty soldiers, determine what happened, return with a report
- **Given by**: The Crown, via Reeve Harkka of Kylmaharju
- **What we know**: Seppanen departed six weeks ago heading north toward a Mtskhuri ruin site. He had forty soldiers, a scholar, a cartographer, and three months of supplies. No contact since departure. His planned route followed known landmarks through the boreal forest before entering open tundra.
- **Current progress**: Departing today.

### Investigate the Hum
- **Objective**: Determine the source and nature of the subsonic hum reported near the northern Nalkamaa
- **Given by**: Implicit — the hum is why the expedition was sent in the first place
- **What we know**: The hum has been audible (or rather, felt) for several months. It emanates from somewhere in the northern Nalkamaa, near known Mtskhuri ruin sites. Animals have been migrating south in unusual numbers.
- **Current progress**: Not yet investigated.

## Important Information Learned

- Commander Seppanen's expedition has been missing for six weeks
- The subsonic hum has been present for several months and is growing
- Settlers in Kylmaharju report vivid, repetitive dreams they can't remember on waking
- Whisper owls have gone silent in the northern forest — locals consider this a very bad sign
- Animal migrations are trending south, away from the hum's source
- The Mtskhuri ruins are ancient, of unknown origin, and locals avoid them
- The Mkhartveli are a reclusive druidic order in the deep Nalkamaa; their purpose is unknown
