# Naming Palette: The Starving Reach

## Primary: Suomi (Finnish)

Used for the subarctic human setting, settlements, and most PCs/NPCs.

### Phonetic Character
- Doubled vowels (aa, ii, uu, ee, oo) for length
- Soft consonants, rolling rhythm
- Diphthongs: ai, ei, oi, au, ou, uo, ie, uo
- Special vowels: a, o, u (back); e, i (front)
- Consonant clusters: ts, nk, ht, lv, rv

### Example Constructions
- **People**: Kaleva, Toivo, Ilmari, Vieno, Aino, Ruska, Lumi, Pellervo
- **Places**: Kylmaharju (Cold Ridge), Sarvitalo (Horn Hall), Valkeasuo (White Marsh)
- **Concepts**: talvi (winter), tuuli (wind), pimea (dark), valo (light)

### Names Used in This Campaign

**PCs:**
- **Toivo Rautavaara** - Human Paladin, expedition leader
- **Vieno Kettunen** - Gnome Wizard, scholar
- **Ruska Pellervo** - Goliath Barbarian, sole survivor

**NPCs:**
- **Kaleva Seppanen** - Commander of the lost expedition
- **Aino Lahtinen** - Cartographer survivor
- **Ilmari Niskanen** - Crown's Shadow agent
- **Esko Haapala** - Crown's Shadow demolitions expert
- **Eero Pellervo** - Ruska's sibling (inside the entity)
- **Sergeant Matti Korhonen** - Sergeant of surviving soldiers

**Settlements & Geography:**
- **Kylmaharju** - The frontier outpost (starting location)
- **Harmaakallio** - Grey Rock, the region's capital
- **Valkeasuo** - White Marsh, a wetland between settlements
- **Pimeavesi** - Dark Water, a lake near the Reach
- **Nalkamaa** - "The Starving Land" / the Reach itself (Finnish: nalka = hunger, maa = land)

## Secondary: Kartvelian (Georgian)

Used for the ancient Aethyn civilization (renamed **Mtskhuri**), their ruins, the Warden order, and characters from that tradition.

### Phonetic Character
- Ejective consonants: ts', k', p', t', ch'
- Consonant clusters: mkh, tskh, shv, dz, ts
- Vowel-light, consonant-heavy words
- Suffixes: -uri, -eli, -ani, -eti, -oba

### Example Constructions
- **People**: Daviti, Sulkhan, Nino, Tamar, Giorgi, Mzekha
- **Places**: Mtskheta, Vardzia, Tusheti, Svaneti
- **Concepts**: sinatl'e (light), sibnel'e (darkness), dzmari (root), k'ldekari (fortress)

### Names Used in This Campaign

**The Ancient Civilization:**
- **Mtskhuri** - The ancient civilization (replaces "Aethyn")
- **Mtskhuruli** - Their language/script
- **Tsikhe-Dzveli** - "Old Fortress," largest ruin complex
- **Vardzia-Kari** - "Rose Gate," a sealed ruin entrance
- **Sinatl'e** - The light-network the Mtskhuri built (their great work)

**NPCs:**
- **Tamar Dzvelishvili** - Firbolg Druid, PC (Warden order, Kartvelian name)
- **Sulkhan Mtskhureli** - Warden elder
- **Nino Kartvelishvili** - Young Warden ranger

**The Wardens:**
- **Mkhartveli** - The Warden order's name for themselves ("Guardians")
- **Mkhartveli-Deda** - "Mother Guardian," title for the elder
- **K'ldekari** - The Wardens' hidden fortress

**Ruin Sites:**
- **Tsikhe-Dzveli** - Old Fortress (major ruin, Act 1)
- **Vardzia-Kari** - Rose Gate (sealed entrance, Act 2)
- **Dzmar-Etseri** - Root Cradle (deep ruin, Act 3)

---

## Cross-Palette Notes

- Characters raised across cultures may have mixed naming (e.g., Ruska Pellervo is Goliath-born but raised among Suomi settlers)
- Tamar uses her Kartvelian name openly; the Wardens maintain Kartvelian naming as a link to the Mtskhuri
- Suomi-speaking characters will mangle Kartvelian words; this is intentional flavor
- The Dreaming Chorus has no palette - it communicates in sensations, not language
