# The Mkhartveli (Wardens)

**Type**: Ancient Druidic Order
**Influence**: Regional (the Nalkamaa)
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Overview

An ancient order of druids and rangers who have guarded the sealed Mtskhuri ruin sites for nearly four millennia. They are the descendants (spiritual if not always biological) of the Mtskhuri who refused to join the binding — those who chose to survive and maintain the seals rather than dissolve into the containment network. They consider this choice their greatest shame and their sacred duty.

## Goals

**Public Goal**: Protect the wilderness of the Nalkamaa and maintain the balance of the land (this is what outsiders believe)
**True Goal**: Maintain the Sinatl'e containment network and prevent anyone from disturbing the sealed Mtskhuri sites, at any cost

## Methods

- Patrol the deep Nalkamaa, monitoring sealed sites for signs of disturbance
- Redirect, mislead, or intimidate outsiders who approach the ruins
- Perform suppression rituals to reinforce the containment locally
- Maintain the Kartvelian language and fragmentary Mtskhuri records
- In extreme cases: sabotage expeditions that threaten the seals

## Structure

**Leadership**: Sulkhan Mtskhureli, the Mkhartveli-Deda (Elder). Final authority on all matters.
**Hierarchy**: Flat. The Elder leads; all others are Wardens of equal rank. Seniority earns respect, not authority. Decisions are discussed openly but the Elder's word is final.
**Membership**: ~30 remaining members. Mostly Firbolgs and half-elves, with some humans. Recruitment is rare — the order is dying. New members are typically found among frontier druids, rangers, or communities near the sealed sites.

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Poor — they have no need for money and little access to trade
- **Military**: Militia — 30 capable fighters, specialists in wilderness and guerrilla tactics
- **Magic**: Significant — druidic magic supplemented by fragmentary Mtskhuri rituals
- **Political**: None — they are unknown to the Crown and most frontier settlements

## Symbols & Recognition

**Symbol**: A circle within a circle — representing the containment (inner) and the watchers (outer). Carved on ward-stones and tattooed on Wardens' skin.
**Colors**: Earth tones — brown, grey, moss green. Nothing that stands out against the landscape.
**Recognition Signs**: A specific pattern of three knocks, a Kartvelian greeting phrase, and the ward-stone each Warden carries (a small carved stone that hums near Mtskhuri sites).

## Relationship to Party

**Current Standing**: Wary
**History**: Tamar is a Warden sent to shadow the party. The order has been aware of the party since they left Kylmaharju.

## Relationship to Other Factions

| Faction | Relationship |
|---------|--------------|
| Blackthorn Remnants | Cautious — the expedition threatened the seals, but the survivors need help |
| Crown's Shadow | Hostile — the Crown's interest in the ruins is the Wardens' worst fear |
| The Dreaming Chorus | Adversarial — the Wardens exist to contain it |

## Hooks & Opportunities

- Tamar's dual loyalty creates an entry point for the party to engage with the Wardens
- K'ldekari offers rest, Drift reduction, and lore — powerful incentives for cooperation
- Nino's alternative research could give the party an option they didn't know existed
- The Wardens' manpower (~30 fighters) could be decisive in Act 3

## GM Notes

The Wardens are sympathetic antagonists before they become allies. Their secrecy and willingness to sabotage outsiders is frustrating, but their reasons are sound — the seals protect the world. The shift from "obstacle" to "ally" should happen through Tamar's relationship with the party and through demonstrating that the party takes the threat seriously.

The order's decline is important context. Thirty members. An aging elder. Dwindling knowledge. The Wardens can't maintain the containment forever, and they know it. This desperation makes Sulkhan's sacrifice plan feel like pragmatism rather than fatalism, and makes Nino's hope feel urgent rather than naive.
