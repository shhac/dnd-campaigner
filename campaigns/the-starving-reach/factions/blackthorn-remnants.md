# Blackthorn Remnants (Seppanen's Expedition)

**Type**: Military Expedition (fragmented)
**Influence**: Local (the deep Nalkamaa)
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Overview

The remnants of Commander Kaleva Seppanen's forty-soldier expedition into the Nalkamaa. "Blackthorn" was the expedition's operational designation. What began as a well-supplied military survey has become two groups of desperate survivors: Seppanen's retreating contingent (~15 soldiers) and the soldiers who descended deeper into Tsikhe-Dzveli (~20, now consumed by the Chorus).

## Goals

**Public Goal**: Originally: survey and assess a Mtskhuri ruin site for the Crown
**True Goal**: Seppanen's group: survive and get home. The consumed soldiers: none — they are no longer themselves.

## Methods

- Seppanen's group: military discipline, rationing, organized retreat
- The consumed soldiers: no longer operating with agency

## Structure

**Leadership**: Commander Kaleva Seppanen (nominal). Sergeant Matti Korhonen (practical — he holds the survivors together).
**Hierarchy**: Military chain of command, fraying under stress.
**Membership**: ~15 survivors (Seppanen's group). ~20 consumed (within Tsikhe-Dzveli). 5 confirmed dead (various causes during the expedition).

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Whatever military supplies they carried, now largely depleted
- **Military**: 15 trained soldiers — experienced, armed, but exhausted, malnourished, and some at Drift 1-2
- **Magic**: None — the expedition's one scholar was among those who descended deeper
- **Political**: The Crown's backing — if they survive to invoke it

## Hooks & Opportunities

- Finding survivors triggers the next phase of Act 1 and provides crucial exposition
- The survivors' physical state (exhaustion, Drift, dwindling supplies) creates urgency
- Seppanen's journal documents the expedition's collapse in real-time
- Matti's mercy-kill of a Drift 4 soldier establishes the Drift mechanic's stakes
- The party must decide: escort survivors south (safe, slow) or press north to the ruin (risky, purposeful)?

## GM Notes

The Blackthorn Remnants are the human cost of the Crown's recklessness. Forty soldiers sent into danger the Crown suspected was real, with inadequate preparation and embedded spies. The survivors should feel like real people — tired, scared, grateful for rescue, and resentful of whoever sent them here.

The split between Seppanen's retreating group and the consumed soldiers who went deeper is the expedition's defining tragedy. Seppanen chose to save some by abandoning others. This decision echoes Toivo's mountain pass failure and foreshadows the party's eventual Act 3 choice.

Once found, the survivors become logistically complicated. Fifteen additional people need food, warmth, and medical attention. The party may need to split forces — some escorting survivors south, others pressing north. This creates organic party-splitting drama.
