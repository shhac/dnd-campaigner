# Crown's Shadow

**Type**: Imperial Intelligence Agency
**Influence**: Continental
**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Overview

The Crown's covert intelligence and special operations branch. Crown's Shadow operates behind the empire's public institutions — military, diplomatic, commercial — placing agents where they're needed and retrieving information the Crown can't officially seek. In the Nalkamaa, their interest is the Dreaming Chorus: a potential weapon of mass psychic destruction that could shift the balance of power on the continent.

## Goals

**Public Goal**: Protect the Crown's interests and security (the agency's official mandate)
**True Goal**: Assess, and if possible secure, the Dreaming Chorus as a strategic asset. The Crown sees the entity not as a threat to be contained but as a resource to be weaponized.

## Methods

- Embed agents in military and civilian operations under false identities
- Operate independently of local command structures
- Prioritize the mission over personnel safety (including their own)
- Use a network of resonance stones for covert long-range communication
- Authorized to eliminate threats to operational security

## Structure

**Leadership**: Unknown to the party. Crown's Shadow reports directly to the Crown's inner council.
**Hierarchy**: Cell-based. Agents operate in pairs or small teams with minimal knowledge of other operations.
**Membership**: Unknown. Hundreds across the empire. Two in the Nalkamaa: Ilmari and Esko.

## Resources

- **Wealth**: Vast — funded by the Crown's treasury through untraceable channels
- **Military**: Elite — small in numbers but individually formidable, with access to specialized equipment
- **Magic**: Limited — some magical equipment (resonance stones, ward-breakers), no spellcasters in this cell
- **Political**: Dominant — Crown's Shadow operates above the law with the Crown's personal authorization

**Recognition Signs**: Classified code phrases and a resonance stone frequency that identifies Crown's Shadow communications

## Relationship to Party

**Current Standing**: Neutral (the party doesn't know they exist)
**History**: Ilmari has the party's Crown file. He knows their names, backgrounds, and mission parameters.

## Hooks & Opportunities

- Ilmari's reveal as Crown's Shadow creates a trust crisis — the Crown sent spies with the expedition
- His deal ("help me extract a sample, the Crown sends a full rescue") is genuinely tempting
- Esko can be turned if separated from Ilmari — he's frightened and not fully committed
- The demolition charges at Tsikhe-Dzveli's entrance create a ticking clock
- Crown's Shadow's continental resources mean the threat doesn't end with two agents — the Crown will send more

## GM Notes

Crown's Shadow represents institutional evil — not cartoonish villainy, but the cold logic of empire. The Crown sees a weapon where others see a horror. Ilmari isn't evil; he's doing his job, and his job is to secure strategic advantages by any means necessary. This makes him more dangerous than a villain motivated by malice.

The agency's continental scope means the party can't solve the Crown's Shadow problem by dealing with Ilmari and Esko. Even if both agents are neutralized, the Crown knows the Chorus exists (Ilmari has been sending reports). More agents will come. The party's Act 3 choice must account for this — whatever they do with the Chorus, the Crown will eventually learn about it.

Esko is the human face of Crown's Shadow — a specialist in over his head, loyal to his partner out of habit, and increasingly terrified. He's designed to be sympathetic and turnable. Ilmari is designed to be respected and feared.
