### Drift Scale
- **0**: Baseline. No influence.
- **1**: Restless sleep. Vague unease near ruin sites.
- **2**: Vivid dreams. Occasional intrusive thoughts. Disadvantage on WIS saves vs. psychic effects near ruin sites.
- **3**: Whispers at the edge of hearing. The Chorus knows your name. Disadvantage on all WIS saves vs. Chorus effects. May involuntarily share surface thoughts.
- **4**: Waking hallucinations. Difficulty distinguishing memory from implanted imagery. The Chorus can attempt to compel single actions (WIS save DC 15).
- **5**: The Chorus can speak through you. WIS save DC 18 to resist compulsion. At this level, the character is on the threshold of being consumed.

### Drift Sources
- Proximity to active Mtskhuri sites: +1 per long rest within 1 mile

- Direct communion with the Chorus (voluntary or forced): +1 to +2
- **Reduction**: Warden ritual (-1), long rest in warded area (-1), act of strong individual will at GM discretion (-1)

## Recent Events Summary

The party has been assembled in Kylmaharju at the Crown's request. Commander Kaleva Seppanen led an expedition of forty soldiers into the Nalkamaa six weeks ago to investigate a Mtskhuri ruin site emitting a subsonic hum. No word has come back. The Crown cannot afford to send another full expedition, so specialists have been gathered: a paladin to lead, a wizard to study, a druid who knows the wild, and a barbarian who has survived the Nalkamaa before.

The settlers of Kylmaharju are uneasy. The hum is faint but present even here. Dreams have been strange. The whisper owls have gone quiet in the northern forest.
