# Campaign Preferences

**Campaign**: The Starving Reach

## Narrative Style

**narrative_style**: hybrid

Uses clear speaker labels with flowing prose. Dialogue is attributed but woven into descriptive narrative rather than bare script format. Descriptions maintain literary quality while keeping conversation easy to follow.

## Tone Notes

- Cosmic horror meets wilderness survival — the cold is indifferent, the thing beneath is not
- Psychological tension over gore; the horror is in what you can't see
- Survival logistics matter — rations, cold, navigation are real constraints
- Quiet moments between characters are as important as set pieces
- The environment is a character: beautiful, lethal, and slowly wrong
