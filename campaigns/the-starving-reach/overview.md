# The Starving Reach

## Setting

**World**: Homebrew — subarctic frontier on the edge of a dying empire
**Era/Time Period**: Late imperial decline — the Crown's reach exceeds its grasp, frontier settlements fend for themselves
**Starting Location**: Kylmaharju, a frontier outpost at the southern edge of the Nalkamaa

## Tone & Themes

**Tone**: Cosmic horror meets wilderness survival — the cold is indifferent, but the thing beneath the permafrost is not
**Core Themes**:
- Survival against an environment that wants nothing from you (and something that wants everything)
- The cost of tampering with forces beyond comprehension
- Isolation, trust, and the bonds that form when civilization is weeks away
- The line between sacrifice and self-destruction

## The Hook

Six weeks ago, the Crown dispatched Commander Kaleva Seppanen and forty soldiers into the Nalkamaa to investigate reports of a Mtskhuri ruin site that had begun emitting a low, subsonic hum audible for miles. The expedition carried a divination scholar, a cartographer, military escort, and enough supplies for three months. They were to document the site, assess its value, and report back.

They never reported back.

Now a second, smaller party has been assembled — not soldiers but specialists chosen because the Crown cannot afford to lose another full expedition. A paladin to lead, a wizard to study, a druid who knows the land, and a barbarian who survived the Nalkamaa once before. They depart from Kylmaharju with ten days of supplies and a simple mandate: find Seppanen's expedition, determine what happened, and return.

The Nalkamaa does not give things back.

## Major Factions

### The Mkhartveli (Wardens)
- **Type**: Ancient druidic order with roots in the Mtskhuri civilization
- **Goals**: Protect the sealed ruin sites, prevent the Sinatl'e from being disturbed
- **Relationship to Party**: Wary allies — Tamar is one of them, but the order distrusts outsiders

### Blackthorn Remnants (Seppanen's Expedition)
- **Type**: Military expedition, now fragmented and desperate
- **Goals**: Survival; some want rescue, some have been compromised
- **Relationship to Party**: The rescue target — but not everyone wants to be rescued

### Crown's Shadow
- **Type**: Imperial intelligence operatives
- **Goals**: Secure the Mtskhuri site for the Crown's strategic interests, regardless of cost
- **Relationship to Party**: Shadowing the party with their own agenda; will become antagonists

### The Dreaming Chorus
- **Type**: Cosmic entity imprisoned beneath the permafrost
- **Goals**: Feed, grow, break free — it has been starving for millennia
- **Relationship to Party**: The threat they don't yet understand; it reaches through dreams

## Key Locations

### Kylmaharju
A frontier outpost of roughly 200 souls clinging to the southern edge of the Nalkamaa. Part trading post, part military waystation, part last stop before the wilderness. The buildings are low-roofed and stone-founded against the wind. This is where the party assembles and departs.

### Nalkamaa (The Starving Reach)
A vast subarctic wilderness stretching north from Kylmaharju. Boreal forest gives way to tundra, then permafrost. The land is beautiful and lethal — whiteout blizzards, thin ice over deep water, predators that have learned to hunt in silence. Somewhere within it, the Mtskhuri built something that should have stayed buried.

### Tsikhe-Dzveli (Old Fortress)
The Mtskhuri ruin that started everything. A vast underground complex beneath a ring of standing stones, now partially exposed by permafrost melt. The subsonic hum emanates from here. Seppanen's expedition was last known to be heading toward this site.

### K'ldekari (Warden Fortress)
The hidden stronghold of the Mkhartveli, built into a cliff face overlooking a frozen river valley. The Wardens have maintained it for centuries, watching the sealed sites. Few outsiders know it exists.

## The Threat

**Main Antagonist/Threat**: The Dreaming Chorus — a cosmic entity sealed beneath the Nalkamaa by the Mtskhuri civilization millennia ago
**Their Goal**: To feed on conscious minds and eventually break its containment
**Timeline**: Weeks to months — the containment is weakening, accelerated by Seppanen's expedition disturbing the site
**What Happens If They Succeed**: The Chorus consumes every conscious mind within the Nalkamaa first, then expands outward. The land truly starves — emptied of all thought, all will, all identity.

## Campaign Arc (GM Eyes Only)

### Act 1: Into the White
The party ventures into the Nalkamaa, contends with the wilderness, encounters signs of the lost expedition, and reaches Tsikhe-Dzveli. They discover survivors and learn that something beneath the ruin has awakened.

### Act 2: The Singing Dark
The party explores deeper into the Mtskhuri ruins, encounters the Mkhartveli, learns the truth about the Dreaming Chorus and its prison, and discovers that Crown's Shadow operatives are working to weaponize the entity. The Drift mechanic intensifies.

### Act 3: The Starving God
The containment is failing. The party must choose how to reseal (or destroy) the Chorus — each option carries a terrible cost. Eero may or may not be saveable. The final confrontation happens deep in Dzmar-Etseri, the Root Cradle, where the Mtskhuri first bound the entity.

## Notable Items

### The Humming Stone
- **Type**: Mtskhuri artifact (resonance key)
- **Location**: Carried by Aino Lahtinen, the expedition's surviving cartographer
- **Significance**: A fragment broken from Tsikhe-Dzveli's central pillar. It resonates with the ruin network and can open sealed doors — or attract the Chorus's attention.

### Seppanen's Journal
- **Type**: Field journal
- **Location**: Found at the expedition's last camp
- **Significance**: Documents the expedition's deterioration — the dreams, the hum, the arguments, the disappearances. The handwriting degrades as the entries progress.

## House Rules

- **Drift Mechanic (0-5)**: Each character accumulates Drift from exposure to the Chorus's influence. Sources: proximity to ruin sites, failed WIS saves against psychic intrusion, consuming Chorus-tainted food/water, prolonged time in the Nalkamaa. At Drift 3+, characters begin hearing whispers. At Drift 5, the Chorus can speak through them. Drift can be reduced through Warden rituals, long rest in warded areas, or strong acts of individual will (GM discretion).
- **Wilderness Survival**: Rations, cold exposure, and navigation are tracked mechanically. The environment is a constant low-grade antagonist.
- **Psychic Intrusion**: The Chorus communicates through dreams and whispers. WIS saves to resist; failure increases Drift and may provide false or corrupted information.
- **Theater of the Mind**: No grid combat. Narrative positioning, advantage/disadvantage for tactical decisions.

## Session Zero Notes

- **Content boundaries**: Body horror is atmospheric, not gratuitous. Psychological horror over gore. No harm to children.
- **Player expectations**: Survival horror with investigation elements. Resources matter. The wilderness is as dangerous as any monster. Not every fight should be fought.
- **Character connections**: The party is assembled for a specific mission. They may not know each other well, but they share a purpose. Bonds will form under pressure.
