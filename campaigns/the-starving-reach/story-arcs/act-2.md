# Act 2: The Singing Dark

## Status
LOCKED

## Key Secrets
- Tsikhe-Dzveli is not merely a ruin — it is the cap on a containment system the Mtskhuri built to imprison the Dreaming Chorus. The civilization destroyed itself in the process of binding the entity, pouring their collective will into a network of resonance anchors that keep the Chorus dormant. The Sinatl'e — their "light-network" — is this containment system.
- The Mtskhuri did not die. They became the containment. Their consciousness was dissolved into the Sinatl'e, forming a living barrier between the Chorus and the surface world. The standing stones are their graves and their prisons.
- The Humming Stone Aino carries is a Sinatl'e node — a piece of the containment network. Removing it weakened the system locally. The Chorus exploited this gap to reach Seppanen's expedition.
- Crown's Shadow has been studying Mtskhuri ruins across the empire for years. Ilmari has documentation proving the Crown knew about the Chorus before Seppanen's expedition was sent — the expedition was never a simple survey. It was a deliberate probe to test the entity's responsiveness.
- The Mkhartveli are descendants of the Mtskhuri who refused to join the binding. They chose survival over sacrifice and have guarded the sealed sites out of guilt ever since. Tamar's order knows the full history but has kept it secret — revealing the truth would mean admitting their ancestors' cowardice.
- Eero Pellervo is in the deep ruin, alive but serving as the Chorus's primary interface with the surface world. The Chorus has learned to mimic Eero perfectly. Distinguishing real Eero from Chorus-Eero requires a DC 18 Insight check, or knowledge of something only the real Eero would know.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Sulkhan Mtskhureli (Warden Elder)**: Wants to reseal Tsikhe-Dzveli by any means necessary, including sacrificing the expedition survivors still inside. He views this as pragmatic, not cruel — the containment must hold. He will pressure Tamar to choose the order over her companions.
- **Nino Kartvelishvili (Young Warden)**: Disagrees with Sulkhan. She believes the Sinatl'e can be repaired without sacrifice if the original Mtskhuri technique is understood. She's been secretly studying the forbidden archives in K'ldekari. She'll share her findings with the party if she trusts them.
- **Ilmari Niskanen**: Moves from observation to action. He and Esko will attempt to reach Dzmar-Etseri ahead of the party to extract a Chorus sample. Ilmari is willing to destabilize the containment further if it means delivering a weaponizable asset to the Crown.
- **Esko Haapala**: Loyal to Ilmari but increasingly frightened. He's at Drift 2 and climbing. If separated from Ilmari, he can be turned — he doesn't want to die for the Crown's ambitions.

## Planned Events
- The party enters Tsikhe-Dzveli's interior. Architecture shifts subtly — rooms don't connect the same way twice. The Chorus is testing them.
- Meeting the Mkhartveli at K'ldekari. Tamar's dual loyalty is forced into the open. Sulkhan demands the Humming Stone be returned to the containment network.
- Discovery of Mtskhuri records that reveal the civilization's fate. The "binding" was not death — it was dissolution. This recontextualizes everything the Wardens believe.
- Eero appears to the party — lucid, desperate, begging for rescue. Is it real? Partially. The Chorus is using real emotions to manipulate.
- Crown's Shadow makes their move. Ilmari reveals his mission and offers the party a deal: help him extract a Chorus sample and the Crown will send a full rescue for all survivors. The deal is genuine but the consequences would be catastrophic.
- The Sinatl'e network shows visible signs of failure — the standing stones crack, the hum becomes a scream, the permafrost splits.

## Foreshadowing
- Carvings in the ruin show figures walking into light and not emerging — but they're smiling
- The deeper passages are warm, almost comfortable, despite being underground in permafrost
- The Chorus's whispers become more coherent, more personal, harder to dismiss as hallucination
