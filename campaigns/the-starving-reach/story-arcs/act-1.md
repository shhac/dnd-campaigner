# Act 1: Into the White

## Status
UNLOCKED

## Key Secrets
- Seppanen's expedition reached Tsikhe-Dzveli and began exploring the upper levels of the ruin. The hum intensified. On the eighth night, the Chorus broke through — first in dreams, then in waking whispers. Soldiers began arguing, then fighting. Seppanen ordered a retreat but half the expedition refused to leave, insisting the voices were guiding them deeper.
- The expedition fractured. Seppanen and roughly fifteen survivors retreated south but became lost. The remaining soldiers descended deeper into the ruin. They have not emerged.
- Aino Lahtinen, the expedition's cartographer, survived the fracture and has been living rough in the wilderness for weeks. She carries a fragment of the ruin's central pillar — the Humming Stone — which she broke off during the chaos. It keeps the worst of the Chorus's influence at bay but also marks her location to anything listening.
- Crown's Shadow agents Ilmari Niskanen and Esko Haapala were embedded in Seppanen's expedition under false identities. They survived the fracture and have been independently exploring Tsikhe-Dzveli, attempting to assess whether the Chorus can be harnessed as a weapon. They are following the party at a distance.
- Eero Pellervo, Ruska's sibling, was among those who descended deeper. Eero is alive but at Drift 4 — the Chorus uses Eero's voice and memories to lure Ruska.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **Aino Lahtinen**: Traumatized, paranoid, but competent. She trusts no one from the Crown after watching the expedition tear itself apart. She'll test the party before revealing what she knows. Her primary motivation is getting the Humming Stone to someone who understands what it is — she suspects the Mkhartveli.
- **Sergeant Matti Korhonen**: The ranking soldier among Seppanen's surviving group. Loyal, practical, deteriorating. He's been holding the survivors together through discipline alone. He doesn't trust the party's motives but will cooperate if it means getting his people out. He's at Drift 2 and hiding it.
- **Ilmari Niskanen**: Cold, professional, patient. He reports directly to the Crown's military intelligence. He sees the Chorus as a potential strategic asset and will sacrifice anyone to secure it. He'll approach the party as a friendly fellow survivor before revealing his true role.
- **Reeve Harkka**: Knows more than she told the party. She's received fragmentary reports from hunters who ventured north — reports of camps with no bodies, equipment arranged neatly as if the owners simply walked away. She didn't share this because the Crown ordered her not to alarm the follow-up party.

## Planned Events
- **Day 1-2**: Wilderness travel through boreal forest. Navigation challenges, weather, wildlife. Establishing survival rhythms and party dynamics.
- **Day 3-4**: Discovery of the expedition's first supply cache — partially consumed, abandoned in haste. First signs something went wrong.
- **Day 5**: Encounter with Aino Lahtinen, or discovery of her trail. She's been surviving alone and is deeply suspicious of anyone from the Crown.
- **Day 6-7**: The hum becomes audible. First Drift exposure. Dreams intensify for all party members. Tamar recognizes Mtskhuri wards carved into rocks — she'll need to decide whether to share this knowledge.
- **Day 8**: Arrival at Tsikhe-Dzveli's outer perimeter. Evidence of the expedition's camp. Seppanen's journal found among abandoned gear.
- **Flexible**: Encounter with frost wolves driven south by the Chorus's influence (they're fleeing, not hunting — but cornered animals fight). Encounter with Mkhartveli scouts who challenge the party's right to approach the ruin.

## Foreshadowing
- The whisper owls fall silent as the party moves north — first fewer calls, then none at all
- Animal tracks in the snow trend south — everything is leaving
- Occasional standing stones along the route bear Mtskhuri script that Vieno or Tamar might recognize
- The party's campfire flames lean north, toward the hum, as if drawn
- One morning, frost patterns on equipment form geometric shapes that don't occur naturally
