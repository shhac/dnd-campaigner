# Act 3: The Starving God

## Status
LOCKED

## Key Secrets
- The Dreaming Chorus is not evil in any comprehensible sense. It is a vast, ancient consciousness that feeds on thought the way fire feeds on fuel. It was drawn to the Mtskhuri civilization's collective intelligence and would have consumed them slowly over centuries. The binding was a desperate act — the Mtskhuri chose to feed themselves to the containment rather than be consumed by the Chorus on its terms.
- The binding can be renewed, but it requires conscious sacrifice — someone must willingly dissolve into the Sinatl'e to reinforce the barrier. The more powerful the mind, the longer the seal holds. A single person might buy decades. Several might buy centuries. The original Mtskhuri bought millennia.
- There is an alternative: Dzmar-Etseri, the Root Cradle, contains the original mechanism the Mtskhuri used to create the binding. It can theoretically be reversed — not to free the Chorus, but to collapse the containment inward, crushing the entity at the cost of destroying the entire Sinatl'e network and everything connected to it. This would cause seismic devastation across the Nalkamaa and kill anyone inside the ruin complex.
- A third option exists but is almost impossible: communicate with the Chorus directly and convince it to accept a different arrangement. The Chorus is alien but not mindless. It has been imprisoned for so long that it has developed something like loneliness. This option requires someone at Drift 5 to serve as an intermediary — and at Drift 5, the line between intermediary and meal is razor-thin.
- Eero Pellervo is at Drift 5 but has not been fully consumed. Some core of Eero's identity persists — the love for Ruska, specifically, which the Chorus cannot digest because it doesn't understand it. This makes Eero uniquely positioned for the third option, but also uniquely vulnerable.

## NPC Hidden Motivations
- **The Dreaming Chorus**: Wants to feed. But it has been alone for so long that it has begun to value the company of minds as much as their consumption. It doesn't understand the difference between communion and annihilation. If the party can make it understand, there may be a way forward that doesn't require sacrifice or destruction.
- **Ilmari Niskanen**: If still alive, he's at Drift 3+ and increasingly erratic. His mission has become personal — he wants to prove to himself that the sacrifice was worth it. He may sabotage the party's plans if they choose an option that doesn't benefit the Crown.
- **Sulkhan Mtskhureli**: Will volunteer himself for the renewal sacrifice if no other option presents itself. He's been preparing for this possibility his entire life. He sees it as atonement for his ancestors' refusal.

## Planned Events
- Descent into Dzmar-Etseri. The architecture here is alive — the walls breathe, the floors pulse, the air tastes of copper and ozone. The Chorus is strongest here.
- Confrontation with the Chorus in its prison. It manifests as a vast absence — a silence that has weight, a darkness that thinks. It communicates through everyone at Drift 2+ simultaneously, using their voices and memories.
- The choice: Renew (sacrifice), Collapse (destroy), or Commune (negotiate). Each option has a champion among the NPCs. Sulkhan advocates renewal. Nino advocates collapse. Eero — if reached — represents communion.
- Whatever the party chooses, the execution is harrowing. Renewal means watching someone dissolve. Collapse means a desperate sprint to escape a crumbling ruin while the earth splits. Communion means trusting an alien intelligence not to consume the intermediary.
- Aftermath: The party emerges (or doesn't) into a changed landscape. The hum has stopped. The silence is different now — not empty, but still. What they chose determines what the Nalkamaa becomes.

## Foreshadowing
- The dreams stop being frightening and become beautiful — vast spaces, distant music, the feeling of being known completely
- The Chorus stops trying to deceive and starts trying to communicate honestly — but its honesty is alien and hard to parse
- The standing stones begin to glow faintly, as if the Mtskhuri within are stirring
