## Act 2: The Singing Dark
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- Party has reached Tsikhe-Dzveli and found expedition survivors
- Party has encountered the Chorus's influence directly (Drift 1+ on at least two characters)
- Tamar's Warden identity has been revealed or is under serious pressure
- Minimum sessions: 3
**Foreshadowing hints**: The deeper they go, the warmer the stone becomes. Ancient carvings show figures with their hands over their ears — or perhaps their mouths.
**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-2.md

## Act 3: The Starving God
**Status**: LOCKED
**Preconditions**:
- Party understands the Chorus's nature and the Mtskhuri containment system
- Crown's Shadow's true agenda has been exposed
- At least one character has reached Drift 3+
- The containment is visibly failing (the hum is audible everywhere, dreams are constant)
**Foreshadowing hints**: The permafrost is melting in places it shouldn't. Things that were frozen are moving. The silence is no longer empty.
**Read when unlocked**: story-arcs/act-3.md
